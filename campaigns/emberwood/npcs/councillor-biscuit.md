# Councillor Biscuit

**Looks:** Fat, ginger, a tiny ribbon of office, an expression of complete authority.
**Sounds like:** Perfectly ordinary, dry, as if he has always talked and you simply never asked.
**Wants:** The pies to work again and someone else to do the walking.
**Offers:** The Door, the notes with the heroes' names, opinions.

## Secret
He is the Door-keeper for this world and has been for longer than the town has existed. He can fly a little. He does not mention it.
