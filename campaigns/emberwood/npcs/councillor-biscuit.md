# Councillor Biscuit

**Looks:** Fat, ginger, a blue ribbon the council voted him two years ago, which he wears as if it were his idea.
**Sounds like:** Ordinary. "Well. Knock." As if he had always talked and you had simply not asked.
**Wants:** The pies to work again, and someone with legs to do the walking.
**Afraid of:** Rain. He will not say so.
**Offers:** The Door in the oak, the four notes with the heroes' names, and an opinion on everything the council does.
**Thinks:** The mayor bakes better than the baker. This must never be said in the bakery.

## Secret
He has kept the oak Door since before the hedge was planted. He can fly a little. He has never mentioned it because nobody asked that either.
