# Brokk Emberward

**Looks:** Through him you can see the throne. A crown too big. Rings braided into his beard that he turns when he thinks.
**Sounds like:** "Nobody has said that in three hundred years." Gruff, then not.
**Wants:** His people remembered. His son's toy horse, which is in the nursery.
**Offers:** The truth about the Ember and the Heart and the dragon; opens dwarven doors; advises.
**Thinks:** The dragon was always going to be the one who stayed. He is ashamed it was not him, and it was him.

## Secret
He stayed so the Heart would have one memory to run on. It ran on him for three centuries. He is at peace when the Heart lights and stays as the Halls' keeper, happily, until chapter 6, when his people come home and he sleeps.
