# Teddy

**Looks:** Eleven feet of patched brown plush, one button eye, the other a knot of thread. Paws that cover a door.
**Sounds like:** Nothing. Nods. Sits down on problems.
**Wants:** Wren safe. That is the whole of him.
**Offers:** A wall, a ride, and a hug that puts out small fires and one Ember-shape.

## Secret
Wren made him at four with a nursery rhyme and a blanket. He has never been washed because she will not allow it.
