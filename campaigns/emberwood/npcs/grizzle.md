# Grizzle

**Looks:** Green, a tweed waistcoat with a torn pocket, a wicker pack of mushrooms that glow brighter than they should and that he keeps at arm's length.
**Sounds like:** "Three rules." Counts coins twice. Says "friend" to anyone who has not yet haggled.
**Wants:** A fair price and for the mushrooms to stop frightening the customers.
**Afraid of:** The mushrooms. They started glowing like this the night the pies came back.
**Offers:** Market access, lanterns "on credit", a trade token, and in chapter 5 a pie he did not bake.
**Thinks:** Nana Nettle is a witch. She is an herb-wife.

## Secret
He has never baked. The pie he brings in chapter 5 is Nana Nettle's and he will say so if asked directly, because goblins do not lie, they merely do not mention.
