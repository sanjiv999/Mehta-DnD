# Grizzle

**Looks:** Green, a tweed waistcoat, a basket of glowing mushrooms, a merchant's squint.
**Sounds like:** "Friend! Three rules." Haggles reflexively, even with himself.
**Wants:** A fair trade and for the glowing to stop frightening his customers.
**Offers:** Market access, lanterns "on credit", the trade token.

## Secret
He has never baked in his life. The pie he brings in chapter 5 was made by Nana Nettle.
