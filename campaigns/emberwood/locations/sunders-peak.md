# Sunder's Peak

**Built by:** Nobody. The ledge is where the cart track ends because the ox will not go further. The pie tins are stacked by the cave mouth, this year's on top, full.
**First seen:** Above the cloud. The tins. A red glow from the cave that is not a fire.
**Hour:** Cold at any hour. Warm in the cave, from below.
**Sound and smell:** Wind, and a breathing you feel through your boots, and warm stone.
**Who is here now:** Sunder. Kindle, under her.
**Recently changed:** Forty pies, untouched. She could not reach them.
