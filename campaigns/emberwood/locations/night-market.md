# The Night Market

**Built by:** The goblins, on the dark of the moon, on a flat carved stone in the wood they thought was a nice level place. It is the dwarven gate.
**First seen:** Lanterns in the trees. Forty stalls. A pair of boots walking back to their stall on their own because somebody did not pay.
**Hour:** Dusk to dawn, three nights a month.
**Sound and smell:** A hurdy-gurdy, haggling, mushrooms, bottled fog when a cork comes out, wet leaves.
**Who is here now:** Grizzle, Nana Nettle at Stall 9, goblin children under the stalls, and at midnight ember-wisps coming up through the roots to take spoons.
**Recently changed:** Things go missing at midnight, and goblins do not steal.
