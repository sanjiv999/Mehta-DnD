# The Tower

**Built by:** Two wizards, Wren's parents, who are now its walls. It leans because they were tired when they did it.
**First seen:** Crooked. Each window a different colour. Something drifting out of the top window and back in.
**Hour:** It opens the door before you knock, at any hour, and tells you the stairs are on the second floor today.
**Sound and smell:** Ticking, a kitchen pot talking to itself, books rustling like birds, a child laughing somewhere above.
**Who is here now:** Wren and Teddy, and the Tower round them.
**Recently changed:** The Tower has not let Wren out in three years and is about to be called a baby.
