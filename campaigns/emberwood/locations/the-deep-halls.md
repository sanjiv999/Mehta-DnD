# The Deep Halls

**Built by:** The dwarves, over a thousand years, under the wood and up into the mountain: streets, forges, a library of stone, a nursery, a throne room, and at the bottom the Heart.
**First seen:** Stairs down from the gate. Then a ceiling your lantern cannot reach. Then a street with shopfronts and a cup still on a table.
**Hour:** None. Lantern-light and, later, wisps.
**Sound and smell:** Drips. An anvil somewhere, struck once. Cold stone and old lamp oil.
**Who is here now:** Brokk's ghost. Ember-shapes. The Heart, dark. A toy horse on a step.
**Recently changed:** Three hundred years ago, everything, in one night.
