# The Hedge and the Hollow Oak

**Built by:** Planted the year the dwarves left, by people who wanted something between them and the wood. The oak was already there and has a door in it that nobody in Hearth will admit to having opened.
**First seen:** The stile. Then the scarecrow, sitting, which scarecrows do not do. Then the knocker, iron, shaped like a sleeping fox.
**Hour:** At dusk there is light under the door.
**Sound and smell:** Birds in the hedge, the scarecrow sighing, warm air from under the door that smells of hot stone.
**Who is here now:** Hob the scarecrow. Biscuit, when it suits him. A cracked rune-stone in the tunnel behind the door.
**Recently changed:** Light under the door, and a rune on the stone that shows a sleeping dragon with its eyes open.
