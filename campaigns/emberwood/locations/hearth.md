# Hearth

**Built by:** Forty families, over three hundred years, inside a hedge that was planted the year the dwarves left and has grown taller than the church since. Stiles over the hedge in four places. One oak in it with a door.
**First seen:** The hedge. Then the church tower over it. Then, inside, the oven in the square with its chimney going.
**Hour:** Tuesday is bake-day and the square is full. Saturday is market. The bell rings the hours and there is no other clock.
**Sound and smell:** The bell, hens, the mill-wheel's new knock. Woodsmoke and bread. From the far end of town the tanner's vats.
**Who is here now:** Mayor Bramble with flour on his sleeves, Councillor Biscuit on the board, the baker, the whole town on Tuesdays.
**Recently changed:** The pie cart came back down the hill full.
