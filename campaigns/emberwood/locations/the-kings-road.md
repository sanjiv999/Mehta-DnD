# The King's Road and the Hidden Valley

**Built by:** The dwarves, in one night, on their way out, and then hidden by the wood, which moved its paths so no one would follow. The valley at the end has four hundred small stone houses with round doors and a forge that has not been lit in three centuries because they had no Heart to light it for.
**First seen:** A line through the trees that was not there yesterday, lined with fireflies. Seven milestones in dwarven.
**Hour:** Dawn, when the Heart is lit and the paths remember.
**Sound and smell:** Fireflies make no sound. A river. Bread, from the valley.
**Who is here now:** Thane Hild and the dwarves, who tell the story every night.
