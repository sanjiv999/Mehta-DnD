# Ready encounters

## Hearth
- **The council meeting:** Biscuit chairs. The kids are asked to vote on something silly (the colour of the new well bucket). Canon.
- **The bakery:** a healing pie, 1d6, once per session.

## The Emberwood (travel)
Roll d6: 1 paths move (Nature Tricky or lose an hour), 2 fireflies offer a shortcut, 3 a wisp, 4 a goblin peddler, 5 a glowing mushroom ring (step in: swap voices for a scene), 6 Hob the scarecrow has walked here to say hello.

## The Tower
- **The Library:** books fly; shush them (a kid's job).
- **The Kitchen:** the pot wants a recipe; invent one (canon).
- **The Stairs:** one honest question per hero.

## Night Market
- **Trading game:** goblins value stories (worth 10 silver), drawings (20), songs (15), a kid's joke (priceless, once).
- **Walking boots:** take you to a random stall.

## Deep Halls
- **Forge Street:** 1 Ember-Hound, dwarven hammer.
- **Library of Stone:** Wren reads; the thane's name; a joke book.
- **Nursery:** the toy horse's home; the drawing of Sunder; no enemies, ever.

## The mountain road
- **Firefly Bridge:** the wisps form a bridge if asked nicely.
- **Cold Shelf:** CON save Easy or shiver (disadvantage next roll); Nana's cloak negates.
