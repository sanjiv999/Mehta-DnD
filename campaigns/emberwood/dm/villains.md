# There is no villain

The Ember, **Kindle**, is the dwarves' forge-fire spirit, left alone so long it forgot it was a
fire and began becoming whatever it saw. Every encounter with it is a conversation that looks
like a fight. The recurring trick: tell it what it is, and it remembers.

**By chapter**
1. A wisp: a sliver of Kindle, curious, drawn to kids.
2. Not present; the Tower is the obstacle (over-protective, not evil).
3. Wisps stealing shiny things "to remember by"; an Ember-Hound guarding the gate from the lullaby.
4. Ember-Shapes copying dwarves and heroes; the Heart dark.
5. Ember-Sunder: Kindle in the dragon's shape, asking "Who am I?"
6. Peace.

**Sunder** is not an antagonist. She is tired. **Brokk** is not a threat. He is proud.
**The Tower** is Wren's parents. They let her go at the end of chapter 2 and turn back at the feast in chapter 5 (an optional lovely beat: two tired adults walk out of the tower door for the first time in three years).
