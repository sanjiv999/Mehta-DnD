# Bestiary (kid-safe)

## Ember-Wisp
HP 3 · AC 12 · Attack +2 · Damage 1 fire · Trick: splits in two if attacked.
**Calm it:** Animals or Nature Easy, a held-out hand, a mushroom. Then it follows as a lantern.
**How it ends:** lands on someone's hand and crackles happily.

## Tower Tantrum (chapter 2)
Flying furniture: one attack per round at +3, 1d4, DEX save Easy to dodge. Ends when someone says "please" to the door and someone reasons with Teddy.

## Ember-Hound (chapter 3)
HP 22 · AC 13 · Attack +4 · Damage 1d6 fire · Trick: hates the lullaby (disadvantage while it is sung).
**Sleep it:** three rounds of a kid singing the chorus. **How it ends:** shrinks to a wisp, yawning.

## Ember-Shape (chapter 4)
HP 12 · AC 13 · Attack +4 · Damage 1d6 · Trick: copies the nearest hero's powers, badly.
**Dispel:** a hero tells it what it really is. **How it ends:** bows and becomes a wisp.

## Ember-Sunder (chapter 5 boss)
HP 50 · AC 15 · Attack +6 · Damage 2d6 fire breath (DEX save Tricky for half; half again under Sunder's wing) · Trick: each round it asks "Who am I?"
**Cannot be killed.** Four true things about forge-fire (toy horse, nursery warmth, lamp oil, its name Kindle, the dwarven hammer's ring) shrink it to a hearth-fire. Damage makes it smaller and more confused (reduces its attack by 1 per 10 HP).
**How it ends:** "Oh. I'm the forge."

## Crows (any time)
HP 1 · They laugh at scarecrows. Telling them off works.
