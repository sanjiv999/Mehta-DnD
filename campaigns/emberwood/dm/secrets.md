# Secrets and answers

- **Why did the pies stop working?** Sunder stopped being able to leave the crack to eat.
- **What is the Ember?** Kindle, the dwarves' forge-fire spirit, lonely and forgetful.
- **Why did the dwarves flee?** Kindle began copying them. Brokk's wife said "don't let it remember you" and they left so it would forget them, and it did, and that made it worse.
- **What is the Heart?** A memory-stone that holds Kindle asleep as long as the dwarves are remembered. Brokk stayed behind to be its memory.
- **Who wrote the notes?** Councillor Biscuit, with a paw.
- **The Tower:** Wren's parents, transformed. They turn back at the chapter 5 feast if the DM wants a tearjerker.
- **The brass frame:** on the great forge's anvil, kept warm by Kindle for three hundred years.
- **Door:** the hollow oak with the fox knocker.
