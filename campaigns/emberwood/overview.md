# The Emberwood

## The pitch
Hearth is a town where everyone knows everyone and the baker's cat is on the council. Beyond its
hedge is the Emberwood, where the trees glow faintly at night and goblins sell mushrooms at a
night market. On the mountain lives the dragon Sunder, who the town pays with pies. This year
the pies have stopped working: something in the wood is waking up, and the dragon is scared.

## The world in five facts
1. Goblins are merchants, not monsters. Their prices are terrible.
2. The wizard in the tower is seven years old and very powerful. She needs babysitting.
3. The forest rearranges its paths at night. Maps are suggestions.
4. Old dwarven ruins run under everything. The dwarves left in a hurry.
5. Dragons here are honest. Sunder would never lie. She just does not tell you everything.

## What heroes do here
Classic quests from the town notice board, growing into the mystery of what the dwarves fled.

## Factions
Hearth Council · Night Market Goblins · The Tower · The Deep Halls (dwarven ghosts)

## Hooks for each hero
- The notice board has a job with each hero's name already on it, in handwriting they do not recognise.
