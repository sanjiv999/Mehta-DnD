# Chapter 4: The Deep Halls

## Premise
A dungeon crawl for kids: big, echoing, wonderful, not scary for long. The halls are a city
built by a people who are forgotten. The party must find the Heart (the dwarves' memory-stone,
gone dark), wake the ghost of the last thane, and remember the dwarves so the locks hold. The
Ember has started taking shapes: of goblins, of dwarves, of whatever it saw last.

## Goal the party will understand
"Explore the halls, find the Heart, and remember the dwarves so the Ember goes back to sleep."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The Hall of Pillars (exploration)
> The stairs end and the halls begin, and they are so big your lantern cannot find the ceiling.
> Pillars carved like trees. Streets with shopfronts. A fountain, dry. Everything left in a hurry:
> a cup on a table, a toy horse on a step. The self-drawing map draws, and draws, and draws.
> Teddy picks up the toy horse and holds it very carefully.
- **Setup:** Exploration with choices. Three ways: the Forge Street (Ember-Hounds, loot), the Library of Stone (runes, Wren's delight, the thane's name), the Nursery (the toy horse's home; the saddest, kindest room; a kid finds a dwarven child's drawing of a dragon, which matters in chapter 5).
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha chooses the first street. Arya reads the map as it draws.
- **Choices:**
  - 🔥 Forge Street
  - 📚 Library of Stone
  - 🧸 The Nursery
- **Outcomes:** Each street gives one thing: a dwarven hammer (Forge), the thane's name, **Brokk Emberward** (Library), the child's drawing (Nursery). All three are needed for the Heart.
- **Image:** Children holding a lantern in a vast dwarven hall with tree-carved pillars, a toy horse on a step, a map drawing itself in a girl's hands

### Scene 2: The shapes (action)
> Down the street, lights, and the lights are walking, and the walking lights have the shape of
> dwarves: short, broad, bearded in flame. "It remembered them," whispers Wren. "Badly." The
> ember-dwarves stop when they see you. They look at you. And one of them starts, slowly, to
> change shape. Into Keisha.
- **Setup:** The **Ember-Shapes** (dm/bestiary.md, HP 12 each, three of them). The Ember copies what it sees. Each copy is a clumsy, sad version. They stop copying when a hero tells them what they really are ("You're not me; you're the Ember, and you're lost"), which is the campaign's recurring trick. The lullaby works too.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha tells her copy who she is (cannot fail; it bows and becomes a wisp). Arya sings the chorus.
- **Choices:**
  - 🗣️ Tell the copies what they are
  - 🎵 Sing the lullaby
  - ⚔️ Fight
  - 🧸 Teddy hugs one (it goes out, gently)
- **Outcomes:** The shapes collapse into wisps and follow the party like a trail of lanterns. The halls are now lit.
- **Image:** Three figures made of glowing coals shaped like dwarves standing in a dark hall, one of them half-changed into the shape of a small girl who is facing it bravely

### Scene 3: The thane's ghost (social)
> The throne room is small, for a king. On the throne, a ghost: a dwarf in a crown too big for
> him, asleep sitting up, snoring. Arya says the name from the library: "Brokk Emberward." The
> ghost opens one eye. "Nobody has said that in three hundred years," he says. "Who are you? And
> why have you brought a dragon into my hall?" He is looking at the child's drawing.
- **Setup:** The thane remembers everything. The Ember is a sleeping fire-spirit under the mountain. The dwarves held it with the Heart, a memory-stone that ran on being remembered. When they fled and were forgotten, the Heart went dark. Sunder the dragon was a *child* then, the dwarves' friend; the drawing is of her. She has been keeping the Ember asleep with her own warmth ever since, and she is tired.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha shows him the drawing. Arya asks him what the Heart needs (an answer: "to be told a story about us. A true one.").
- **Choices:**
  - 👑 Ask about the Ember
  - 🐉 Ask about the dragon
  - 📖 Ask what the Heart needs
  - 🎁 Give him the toy horse (it was his son's; he weeps; he will come with you)
- **Outcomes:** Brokk joins (as a ghost, can only advise and open dwarven doors). The Heart is in the deepest hall. Nana Nettle's memory and Brokk's story are what it needs.
- **Image:** A small ghostly dwarf king on a stone throne looking at a child's crayon drawing of a dragon, a toy horse in a child's outstretched hand

### Scene 4: The Heart (climax)
> The Heart is a stone the size of Badal's... the size of a cart, in a round room, and it is dark
> grey and cold and the floor around it is cracked with ember-light. The whole mountain is holding
> its breath. "Tell it," says Brokk. "Tell it who we were."
- **Setup:** The climax is storytelling. Each hero tells the Heart one true thing about the dwarves they have learned (Nana's lamp oil, the toy horse, the child who drew a dragon, the lullaby, the last words at the gate). Each true thing lights a facet (Persuasion or Performance Easy; automatic if sincere). Five facets and the Heart glows. Meanwhile, the Ember pushes up through the cracks each round as wisps; the lullaby holds them (a kid sings). On the fifth facet the cracks close, the wisps settle into lanterns, and the mountain exhales.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha sings the lullaby to hold the cracks. Arya tells the story of the child's drawing.
- **Choices:**
  - 📖 Tell a true thing about the dwarves
  - 🎵 Hold the cracks with the lullaby
  - 🔨 Strike the Heart with the dwarven hammer (it rings; one facet lights; Brokk winces)
  - 🧙 Wren puts the drawing on the Heart (one facet lights)
- **Outcomes:** The Heart glows. The locks hold. Brokk's ghost solidifies for a moment, bows, and is at peace (he stays, happily, as the Halls' keeper). In the Heart's light, the party sees a shape moving in the deepest crack: a dragon's eye, tired, closing. Sunder. Level 5.
- **Image:** A cart-sized grey stone in a round chamber lit facet by facet into gold as children tell it stories, cracks of ember-light closing in the floor, a ghost dwarf bowing
- **Music:** deep drums resolving into a warm chord

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The goblin stowaway (if any) is found asleep in the toy horse's stall. He has been very brave.
2. The forge wakes up and starts hammering by itself. It makes one small thing for a kid (name it).
3. A copy of Teddy appears. The two Teddies hug. Both are fine.
4. The map runs out of paper. Arya adds a page from her journal.
5. The library books are in dwarven and Wren reads one aloud; it is a joke book; nobody gets the jokes; everyone laughs anyway.
6. The Heart hiccups and all the wisps turn blue for a scene.

## Rewards
- 300 XP. Level 5. The dwarven hammer (1d8, glows near the Ember). Brokk as a friend. The Deep Halls +3. The child's drawing (keep it; chapter 5).

## How it ends and what it sets up
The locks hold. But the party has seen Sunder under the mountain, keeping the Ember asleep with
her own warmth, and she looked tired. Someone should go up the mountain and tell her she can rest.
