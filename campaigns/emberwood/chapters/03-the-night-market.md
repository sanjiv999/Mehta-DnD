# Chapter 3: The Night Market

## Premise
The goblins' market sits on top of the old dwarven gate and has for three hundred years; they
thought it was a nice flat rock. Grizzle will help if the party trades fairly. The market has
three things the party needs: a map of the halls, the gate-key (a song), and a goblin who
remembers the dwarves because her great-great-grandmother did. And something is stealing from
the stalls at night: ember-wisps, many of them, carrying things underground.

## Goal the party will understand
"Trade for what you need at the market and find the way down."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Market rules (social, trading)
> Lanterns in the trees. Stalls selling glowing mushrooms, bottled fog, maps that draw
> themselves, boots that walk back to the shop if you don't pay. Goblins in waistcoats shouting
> prices. Grizzle waves you over. "Friends! Three rules. Don't touch what you can't pay for.
> Don't say 'cheap' out loud. And everything, everything, is for trade. What've you got?"
- **Setup:** Trading game. Each hero has something to trade (a story, a song, a drawing, a Wish, a kid's tooth, their camel... wait, wrong world: a keepsake copy). Goblins value stories and drawings highly. The party needs: the self-drawing map (Stall 3), the gate-song (sung by Old Nana Nettle, Stall 9), and information (Grizzle).
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha trades a drawing (make it; it becomes an item in the goblin stall forever). Arya haggles for the map (Persuasion Tricky, Easy with a story).
- **Choices:**
  - 🗺️ Trade for the map
  - 🎵 Find the gate-song
  - 🍄 Ask Grizzle about the thefts
  - 👢 Try on the walking boots (they walk you to Stall 9)
- **Outcomes:** Map acquired. Nana Nettle will sing the song if the party catches the thief ruining her stall.
- **Image:** Children at a goblin stall trading a crayon drawing for a map that draws itself, goblins in waistcoats leaning in with interest

### Scene 2: The thieves (exploration, stakeout)
> Midnight. The market dims. You crouch behind Nana Nettle's stall with Wren and a bag of
> mushrooms. And then, from the ground, lights: a dozen ember-wisps squeezing up through the
> roots, each one grabbing something small and shiny and dragging it back down. A spoon. A
> button. A mushroom. A goblin's hat, with the goblin still in it.
- **Setup:** Many wisps (dm/bestiary.md), harmless, kleptomaniac. They are collecting shiny things for the Ember, "to remember by". Catch one, calm it, follow them down.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha's wisp friend from chapter 1 talks to them (it crackles; the kids interpret). Arya rescues the goblin in the hat.
- **Choices:**
  - 🤲 Calm them with your own wisp
  - 🪤 Net them
  - 🏃 Follow them down the roots
  - ✨ Let Wren make them sleepy
- **Rolls:** Animals Easy to calm. Athletics Easy to rescue the goblin.
- **Outcomes:** The wisps lead to a crack in the flat rock: the gate. It needs the song. Nana Nettle, grateful, sings it, and teaches the kids the chorus.
- **Image:** A dozen tiny ember-wisps squeezing up through tree roots at midnight carrying spoons and buttons, a goblin being dragged by his hat

### Scene 3: Nana Nettle remembers (social)
> Nana Nettle is the oldest goblin in the wood, small as a loaf, wrapped in six shawls. "My
> great-great-granny sold the dwarves their lamp oil," she says. "Then one night they all came
> up through this gate, every one, carrying what they could, and the last one said: 'Don't let it
> remember you.' And they walked into the forest and the forest moved its paths and nobody ever
> found where they went." She sniffs. "I remember them. Somebody should."
- **Setup:** Lore scene. The dwarves fled the Ember. "Don't let it remember you" means the Ember learns the shape of what it sees and becomes it. The gate-song is a dwarven lullaby; the Ember sleeps when it is sung.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Learn the chorus (two lines; the DM makes it up with them; canon). Keisha asks Nana what a dwarf looked like (she draws one; that drawing goes in the journal).
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Learn the song
  - ❓ Ask where the dwarves went
  - 🎁 Give Nana something (Goblins +1)
- **Outcomes:** The song is learned. The gate will open. The party understands the rules of the Ember.
- **Image:** A tiny ancient goblin wrapped in six shawls teaching two children a song by lantern-light, a drawing of a dwarf on the table

### Scene 4: The gate opens (action)
> You sing. Badly, mostly, but together. The flat rock the market sits on shivers, and a line of
> light draws itself across it in the shape of a door, and the door grinds open on stairs going
> down. Cold air. Old air. Every goblin in the market goes quiet. And up the stairs, fast, comes
> something that is not a wisp. It is a wisp's big brother: an **Ember-Hound**, made of coals, and
> it has smelled the song.
- **Setup:** First real fight: the **Ember-Hound** (dm/bestiary.md, HP 22). It hates the lullaby; singing it (any hero, no roll) gives it disadvantage. It can be put to sleep with three verses (three rounds of a kid singing) instead of beaten.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Sing the chorus each round. Cannot fail; the hound gets sleepier.
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Sing it to sleep
  - ⚔️ Fight it
  - 🧙 Wren's spell (a bucket of rain)
  - 🧸 Teddy sits on it
- **Outcomes:** The hound sleeps and shrinks to a wisp. The stairs are open. Grizzle hands out lanterns "on credit". Level 4.
- **Image:** A dwarven stone gate grinding open under a goblin market, a dog made of glowing coals leaping up the stairs, children singing with their eyes shut
- **Music:** hurdy-gurdy chorus, then a lullaby

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The walking boots walk a hero to the wrong stall, which sells "second-hand dreams".
2. A goblin child wants to come. His mother says no. He comes anyway (stowaway, found in chapter 4).
3. The self-drawing map draws a picture of the dragon and refuses to draw anything else.
4. Grizzle tries to sell the party their own wisp. Decline politely.
5. Nana Nettle's shawl is a dwarven cloak. It is warm. She lends it.
6. The lullaby makes Teddy fall asleep too. Carry him.

## Rewards
- 200 XP. Level 4. The self-drawing map. The gate-song. Nana's dwarven cloak (resist cold once). Goblins +3.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The stairs go down. The map draws them as it goes. The Deep Halls, three hundred years empty, are
waiting, and somewhere in them is the Heart that forgot.
