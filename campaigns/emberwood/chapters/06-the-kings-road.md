# Chapter 6: The King's Road

## Premise
The wood moved its paths so the dwarves would never be followed. With the Heart lit, the paths
remember, and the map draws a road nobody has walked in three hundred years. At the end, a
valley of dwarves who tell the story every night and do not know the mountain is safe.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Seven milestones (exploration)
> The map draws a line through the trees and the trees step aside for it, which nobody has seen
> the wood do. Fireflies line the road at knee height. Wren rides on Teddy. Sunder flies above,
> slowly, keeping pace, and every so often her shadow goes over you.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Follow the fireflies. Count the milestones (seven; Wren reads them; the seventh says "nearly").
- **Choices:**
  - 🗺️ Follow the map
  - 🐉 Ride ahead on Sunder
- **Outcomes:** A valley with round doors, and smoke from four hundred chimneys.
- **Image:** A path lined with fireflies opening between glowing trees, a map glowing in a child's hands, a dragon's shadow on the road

### Scene 2: Grandfather (social)
> They are short and broad and bearded and very surprised. A dwarf woman with a hammer over
> her shoulder and a baby on her hip looks at you, then up at the dragon, then at the ghost of a
> king in a crown too big standing beside you. "Grandfather?" she says.
- **Setup:** Thane Hild. The dwarves never forgot; the Heart could not hear them from here.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha tells them the Ember is safe (they do not believe her; Sunder nods; they do). Arya gives Hild the toy horse.
- **Choices:**
  - 🐴 Give Hild the horse
  - 📖 Tell them everything, in order
  - 🥧 Pie
- **Outcomes:** They come home. Brokk's ghost, finally, sleeps.
- **Image:** A dwarf woman with a baby on her hip staring at a child holding out a small wooden toy horse, a bronze dragon behind, a ghost king smiling

### Scene 3: Four hundred lanterns (celebration)
> Four hundred dwarves walk the King's Road into the Deep Halls carrying lanterns, singing the
> lullaby as a marching song, which is what it always was. The forge lights. Kindle is beside
> itself. Hearth and the goblins and the dwarves eat for three days.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Name the feast. Choose who sits next to the dragon.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥧 Sit with the dragon
  - 🔨 Watch the forge light
  - 🎵 Lead the song
- **Outcomes:** 300 XP. "Keeper of the King's Road" for everyone.
- **Image:** A long procession of dwarves with lanterns walking into a mountain gate at night, children leading, everyone singing

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The milestones are in dwarven. Wren translates; the seventh is a joke.
2. A firefly leads a child to a dwarven picnic spot. Pointless, lovely.
3. Sunder lands to rest and the dwarves see a dragon before they see the heroes. Talk fast.
4. Hild's baby wants the horse. Negotiate.
5. Four hundred dwarves singing the lullaby puts Teddy to sleep. Carry him.
6. Grizzle has set up a stall at the valley gate before anyone has said hello.

## Rewards
- 300 XP. The dwarves' friendship. A forged gift for each hero from the relit forge.
- "Keeper of the King's Road" achievement.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The Halls are full. Brokk sleeps. The oak Door stands open for the next world.
