# Chapter 6: The King's Road

## Premise
An optional one-session epilogue. The forest moved its paths so the dwarves could never be
found. Now that the Heart glows, the paths remember, and the self-drawing map draws a road
nobody has walked in three hundred years. At the end: a valley of dwarves who have no idea the
mountain is safe. Bring them home.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The road remembers (exploration)
> The map draws a line through the trees, and the trees step aside. Nobody has seen the forest do
> that. Fireflies line the road like lamps. Wren rides on Teddy. Sunder flies above, slowly, keeping pace.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Follow the fireflies. Count the dwarven milestones (seven).
- **Choices:**
  - 🗺️ Follow the map
  - 🐉 Ride ahead on Sunder
- **Outcomes:** A valley with round doors.
- **Image:** A forest path lined with fireflies opening between glowing trees, a map glowing in a child's hands

### Scene 2: The valley (social)
> They are small, broad, bearded, and very surprised. A dwarf woman with a hammer and a baby on her
> hip looks at you, then at the dragon, then at the ghost of a king standing beside you. "Grandfather?" she says.
- **Setup:** Brokk's great-great-granddaughter, **Thane Hild**. The dwarves fled and settled and told the story every night. They never forgot. The Heart just could not hear them from here.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha tells the dwarves the Ember is safe (they do not believe her; Sunder nods; they believe). Arya gives Hild the toy horse.
- **Choices:**
  - 🐴 Give Hild the toy horse
  - 📖 Tell them everything
  - 🥧 Pie
- **Outcomes:** The dwarves come home. The Halls fill. Brokk's ghost, finally, goes to sleep.
- **Image:** A dwarf woman with a baby on her hip staring at a child holding out a small wooden toy horse, a bronze dragon behind, a ghost king smiling

### Scene 3: The homecoming (celebration)
> Four hundred dwarves walk the King's Road into the Deep Halls with lanterns, singing the lullaby
> that was always a marching song. The forge is lit. Kindle is beside itself. Hearth and the goblins
> and the dwarves have a feast that lasts three days and is still spoken of.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Name the feast. Choose who sits with the dragon.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥧 Sit with the dragon
  - 🔨 Watch the forge light
  - 🎵 Lead the lullaby as a marching song
- **Outcomes:** 300 XP. "Keeper of the King's Road" achievement. The Emberwood is at peace.
- **Image:** A long procession of dwarves with lanterns walking into a mountain gate at night, children leading them, singing

## Rewards
- 300 XP. The dwarves' friendship forever. A forged gift for each hero from the reopened forge.

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The milestones are numbered in dwarven. Wren translates; the seventh says "nearly".
2. A firefly leads a kid hero down a side path to a dwarven picnic spot. Lovely, pointless.
3. Sunder has to land to rest; the dwarves see a dragon first and the heroes second. Explain fast.
4. Hild's baby wants the toy horse. Negotiate.
5. The lullaby, sung by four hundred dwarves, puts Teddy to sleep. Carry him home.
6. Grizzle sets up a stall at the valley entrance before anyone has said hello.

## Rewards
- 300 XP. The dwarves' friendship forever. A forged gift for each hero from the reopened forge.
- "Keeper of the King's Road" achievement.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The Deep Halls are full again. Brokk sleeps. The Emberwood is at peace, and the oak Door stands open for the next world.
