# Chapter 5: Sunder's Peak

## Premise
Sunder is honest and does not volunteer. The pies came back because she can no longer leave the
crack to eat. She is afraid that if she sleeps, the Ember wakes. The heroes bring her the news
(the Heart glows), bring her a pie, and face what Kindle does when it tries one last time: it
takes her shape.

## Goal the party will understand
"Take Sunder a pie and tell her the Heart is lit."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Forty pies (travel)
> The whole town is at the hedge. The mayor has baked. The baker has baked. Grizzle, who says
> goblins do not bake, has a pie under a cloth and will not meet anyone's eye. Councillor
> Biscuit is sitting on top of a cart of forty pies. "Apple," he says. "She likes the apple
> ones. Whatever the mayor tells you." The track goes up through the glowing wood into cloud.
- **Setup:** Three stops: the Firefly Bridge (a river crossed by fireflies; the wisps make a bridge if asked nicely), the Cold Shelf (CON save Easy; Nettle's cloak negates), and the ledge, where the ox stops.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu carries one pie herself the whole way. Aarya asks the wisps for the bridge.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥧 The cart, slowly, all forty
  - 🎒 One pie each, fast
  - 🧙 Wren floats the cart (wobbly, works)
- **Outcomes:** The ledge above the cloud, and the tins.
- **Image:** A cart of forty pies with a ginger cat on top being pushed up a mountain track through a glowing wood by children and an eleven-foot teddy bear
- **Music:** a fiddle, out of breath

### Scene 2: The exact truth (social)
> She is bronze going gold at the edges and longer than the alehouse, and she is lying across
> a crack in the ledge that glows red under her, and she opens one eye. "You brought pie," she
> says. "You're the ones from the Halls. Brokk's Heart is lit. I felt it." A long breath goes
> out of her. "I would like very much to eat the pie. I'm afraid that if I move, it wakes."
- **Setup:** Pure talk. She answers every question exactly. She knows the drawing. The Heart holds most of the Ember; one crack remains, hers. To close it, the Ember must be reminded it was a forge-fire with a name, Kindle, that the dwarves talked to.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu gives her the pie (she eats it where she lies, carefully). Aarya shows her the drawing.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥧 Give her the pie
  - 🖼️ Show her the drawing
  - ❓ Ask what she is afraid of
  - 😴 Tell her she can rest (not yet, she says, and why)
- **Outcomes:** The first pie in a year. The Ember's name. "If you can get it to remember being a forge, I can get up."
- **Image:** A huge bronze dragon taking an apple pie from a small girl's hands without lifting her body off the glowing crack
- **Music:** wind, and a dragon chewing

### Scene 3: Who am I (climax)
> The moment she shifts her weight, the crack flares, and something stands up out of it in her
> shape, because her shape is what it has looked at for three hundred years: a dragon of coals
> with ash for eyes, taller than she is. It does not roar. It speaks, and the sound is a bellows working:
> "Who am I?"
- **Setup:** **Ember-Sunder** (dm/bestiary.md, HP 50, cannot be killed). Each round a hero attacks (it gets smaller and more confused) or tells it a true thing about forge-fire: it made the toy horse; it warmed the nursery; it lit Nettle's lamp oil; its name is Kindle; the hammer's ring. Four true things and it is a hearth-fire. Sunder will not fight her own shape but shelters the kids under a wing.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu tells it about the toy horse. Aarya tells it its name.
- **Choices:**
  - 🗣️ Tell Kindle what it was
  - 🔨 The hammer (it rings; a true thing)
  - 🎵 The lullaby (holds it a round)
  - 🐉 Under Sunder's wing
  - 🧙 Wren makes it rain pies (it is confused; a free round)
- **Rolls:** Breath: DEX save Tricky, 2d6 fire, halved under the wing. True things: Persuasion Easy, automatic if sincere.
- **Outcomes:** It shrinks to a fire the size of a dog with a face and says, "Oh. I'm the forge." Sunder stands up for the first time in three centuries and the ledge shakes and she laughs, which sounds like a landslide.
- **Image:** A dragon made of coals and ash looming over a small girl holding up a wooden toy horse and speaking to it, the real bronze dragon sheltering the other children under a wing
- **Image (after):** A small friendly fire the size of a dog with a shy face sitting on a mountain ledge, and a huge bronze dragon standing up for the first time and laughing, the heroes cheering beside a cart of pies
- **Music:** horns, then a hearth

### Scene 4: The anvil (epilogue)
> Kindle goes down into the crack, small now, and you follow, and it is not a crack. It is
> a forge, the dwarves' great forge, cold three hundred years except for the small fire walking
> ahead of you. On the anvil, finished, never collected, a frame of brass the size of a
> lantern with a space for a wick and a shade. "They made it for a king who never came," says
> Kindle. "I kept it warm."
- **Setup:** The brass frame. Sunder flies the party home. Hearth feasts. Kindle moves into the bakery oven. The Halls reopen. If the DM wants the beat: two tired adults walk out of the tower door at the feast.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu names the bakery's oven-fire (it is Kindle). Aarya decides what the town does with the Halls.
- **Choices:**
  - 🐉 Ride Sunder home
  - 🔥 Invite Kindle into the oven
  - 🏛️ Reopen the Halls
- **Outcomes:** `python tools/campaign.py complete emberwood`. The oak Door is open. Level 6.
- **Image:** A bronze dragon flying over a small village at sunset with children on her back, a small fire glowing in a bakery window below
- **Music:** a fiddle, everyone

## Complications (roll d6)
1. Pies bring every bird on the mountain. Hob was right about crows.
2. The Firefly Bridge is a toll bridge. The toll is a joke, and it must be good.
3. Sunder sneezes. The party is briefly airborne.
4. Kindle takes the shape of a pie for a round. Nobody can hit a pie.
5. Grizzle followed the cart up "to check the pies arrived". He is behind Teddy.
6. Councillor Biscuit reveals he can fly, a little, and always could.

## Rewards
- 500 XP. The brass frame. "Friend of Sunder" for everyone. Kindle in the oven. A dragon ride.

## How it ends and what it sets up
Three pieces of the Lantern. One world left. And a road in the wood that was not there yesterday.
