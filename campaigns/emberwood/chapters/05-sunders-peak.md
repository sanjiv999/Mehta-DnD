# Chapter 5: Sunder's Peak

## Premise
Sunder is honest and does not tell you everything. The pies stopped working because she stopped
eating; she has been lying across the Ember's deepest crack for three hundred years, and lately
she cannot leave it even to eat. She is afraid that if she sleeps, the Ember wakes, and she is
so tired. The heroes must bring her the good news (the Heart glows), bring her a pie, and face
what the Ember does when it tries one last time: it takes *her* shape.

## Goal the party will understand
"Climb the mountain, give Sunder a pie, and tell her the Heart is lit."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The pie cart (social, travel)
> The whole town comes to the hedge to see you off. Mayor Bramble has baked. The baker has baked.
> Grizzle, who claims goblins do not bake, has baked. Councillor Biscuit sits on top of a cart
> of forty pies and says, "She likes the apple ones. Don't let the mayor tell you otherwise."
> The mountain road goes up through the glowing wood into the clouds.
- **Setup:** A journey with three stops: the Firefly Bridge (a river crossed only by fireflies; the wisps make a bridge if asked), the Cold Shelf (Nana's cloak; a CON save Easy for the rest), the Dragon's Doorstep (bones of old pies, politely stacked).
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha chooses which pie to carry herself. Arya asks the wisps for a bridge.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥧 Take the cart (slow, all forty pies)
  - 🎒 Take one pie each (fast)
  - 🧙 Wren floats the cart (works; wobbly)
- **Outcomes:** The party arrives at the ledge with pies.
- **Image:** A cart of forty pies with a ginger cat on top being pushed up a mountain path through a glowing forest by children and an eleven-foot teddy bear

### Scene 2: Sunder (social)
> She is bronze and gold and bigger than the town hall, and she is lying across a crack in the
> mountain that glows red under her belly, and she looks at you with eyes like old lamps and
> says: "You brought pie. You're the ones from the Halls. Brokk's Heart is lit; I felt it from
> here. Thank you." A long breath. "I'd like to eat the pie. I'm afraid that if I move, it wakes."
- **Setup:** Pure roleplay. The kids talk to a dragon. She answers every question honestly. She shows them the drawing is of her, as a child, drawn by Brokk's son. She asks if it is really safe. The honest answer: the Heart holds *most* of it. One crack remains, the one she is lying on. To close it, the Ember must be *remembered* too: it is not evil; it is a fire-spirit that was the dwarves' forge-fire once, and it got lonely.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha gives Sunder the pie. Arya shows her the drawing. Both ask her anything.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥧 Give her the pie
  - 🖼️ Show her the drawing
  - ❓ Ask her what she is afraid of
  - 😴 Tell her she can rest (she cannot, yet)
- **Outcomes:** Sunder eats (the first time in a year). She tells the party the Ember's name: **Kindle**. She says: "If you can get it to remember being a forge-fire, I can finally get up."
- **Image:** A huge bronze dragon gently taking an apple pie from a small girl's hands, a crack of red light beneath the dragon

### Scene 3: Kindle (action, climax)
> The moment Sunder shifts her weight, the crack flares, and out of it rises a shape of fire. It
> is Sunder's shape, because that is what it has seen for three hundred years: a dragon of coals
> with eyes of ash, bigger than the real one, looking down at you.
> It does not roar. It says, in a voice like a forge: "Who am I?"
- **Setup:** The boss: **Ember-Sunder** (dm/bestiary.md, HP 50, cannot be "killed", only reminded). The fight is interleaved with the trick: each round a hero can attack (damage makes it smaller and more confused) or *tell it what it is* (one true thing about forge-fire: it made the toy horse; it warmed the nursery; it lit Nana's lamp oil; it is Kindle). Four true things and it shrinks to a hearth-fire. The real Sunder cannot fight it (it is her shape; she will not), but she can shield the kids with a wing (advantage on saves).
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha tells Kindle about the toy horse. Arya tells it its name. Each is a true thing.
- **Choices:**
  - 🗣️ Tell Kindle what it was
  - 🔨 Dwarven hammer (it rings; Kindle remembers the forge; counts as a true thing)
  - 🎵 The lullaby (holds it a round)
  - 🐉 Ask Sunder to shield you
  - 🧙 Wren makes it rain pies (it is confused; one free round)
- **Rolls:** Its breath: DEX save Tricky, 2d6 fire, halved under Sunder's wing. True things: Persuasion Easy, automatic if sincere.
- **Outcomes:** Kindle shrinks to a small, warm, embarrassed fire the size of a dog and says "Oh. I'm the forge." Sunder stands up for the first time in three centuries and stretches, and the mountain shakes with it, and she laughs.
- **Image:** A dragon made of coals and ash towering over a small girl who is holding up a toy horse and speaking to it, the real bronze dragon sheltering the other children under her wing
- **Music:** horns, then a warm crackling

### Scene 4: What the dwarves left (epilogue, exploration)
> Kindle leads you down, small and warm, into the deepest crack, which is not a crack at all now
> that it is lit: it is a forge. The dwarves' great forge. And on the anvil, finished and never
> collected, a frame of brass the size of a lantern, with a space for a wick and a shade.
> "They made it for a king who never came," says Kindle. "I kept it warm."
- **Setup:** The brass frame of the Lantern of Many Roads. Sunder flies the party home (the kids ride). Hearth throws a feast. Kindle moves into the bakery oven. Brokk's Halls reopen; goblins and townsfolk trade there.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha names the bakery's new oven-fire officially (it is Kindle). Arya decides what the town does with the Halls.
- **Choices:**
  - 🐉 Ride Sunder home
  - 🔥 Invite Kindle to live in Hearth
  - 🏛️ Reopen the Halls
- **Outcomes:** The campaign is complete. `python tools/campaign.py complete emberwood`. The oak Door is open. Level 6.
- **Image:** A bronze dragon flying over a cosy town at sunset with children on her back, a small forge-fire glowing in a bakery window below

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The pies attract every bird on the mountain. Hob the scarecrow was right about crows.
2. The Firefly Bridge is a toll bridge. The toll is a joke.
3. Sunder sneezes and the party is briefly airborne.
4. Kindle takes the shape of a pie for a round. Nobody can attack a pie.
5. Grizzle followed the party up to "check the pies arrived". He is hiding behind Teddy.
6. Councillor Biscuit reveals that he has always been able to fly, a little. He never mentioned it.

- 500 XP. The brass frame. "Friend of Sunder" achievement. Kindle as the town's hearth. A dragon ride.

## How it ends and what it sets up
Three pieces of the Lantern. One more world.
