# Chapter 1: The Notice Board

## Premise
The heroes arrive in Hearth and find the notice board. Four jobs, one with each hero's name on
it. A missing cat (who is on the council), a scarecrow wanting a hat, a goblin who overcharged a
widow, and "a light in the hedge". All four lead to the hollow oak with the fox door-knocker.

## Goal the party will understand
"Do the jobs on the board and find out who wrote your names."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Hearth (social)
> Thatched roofs, a bakery that smells like heaven, a town square with a well, and a notice
> board covered in paper. A fat ginger cat sits on top of it like it owns it. Four notes have
> names on them. Your names. The baker leans out of her window. "Oh good, you're here. Biscuit
> said you would be."
> The cat yawns.
- **Setup:** Mayor Bramble (a nervous man) and Councillor Biscuit (a cat) explain the pies came back. Four jobs. The kids pick first.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha picks a job. Arya reads the handwriting and notices it is tiny, like a paw.
- **Choices:**
  - 🐱 "Find the councillor's cat" (he is right there; he wants you to follow him)
  - 🎩 "The scarecrow wants a hat"
  - 💰 "Goblin overcharged a widow"
  - ✨ "A light in the hedge"
- **Outcomes:** Jobs chosen. Biscuit walks toward the hedge and looks back, meaningfully.
- **Image:** A cosy town square with a bakery, a notice board with four notes, a fat ginger cat sitting on it, four heroes reading
- **Music:** fiddle

### Scene 2: The scarecrow and the goblin (social, two short scenes)
> The scarecrow in the hedge is sitting down, which scarecrows do not do, and it is sad. "Crows
> laugh at me," it says. "No hat. A scarecrow with no hat is just a stick in a shirt."
> Further along, a goblin in a waistcoat is arguing with an old woman over a basket of mushrooms.
> "Twelve silver!" "They were three yesterday!" "Yesterday they were not glowing, madam."
- **Setup:** Two small kindnesses. The scarecrow needs any hat (a kid's drawing of a hat counts; Wren can make it real later). The goblin, **Grizzle**, is overcharging because the glowing mushrooms are new and strange and he is scared of them. Settle it (Persuasion Easy) and he becomes a contact.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha gives the scarecrow a hat (describe it; canon). Arya negotiates a fair price (she can count silver).
- **Choices:**
  - 🎩 Give the scarecrow a hat
  - 🍄 Ask Grizzle why the mushrooms glow
  - 💰 Pay the widow's price yourself
  - 😠 Threaten the goblin (he faints; goblins +0, widow unimpressed)
- **Outcomes:** The scarecrow, **Hob**, stands up and offers to guard the hedge. Grizzle says the mushrooms started glowing the night the pies came back, "like something under the ground woke up and the roots felt it".
- **Image:** A sad scarecrow sitting in a hedge being handed a hand-drawn hat by a child, a goblin in a waistcoat haggling nearby

### Scene 3: The light in the hedge (exploration)
> At dusk the hedge glows in one place. Biscuit the cat is sitting in front of it, tail
> twitching. Behind the glow is an oak so old it is hollow, and set into the hollow is a door
> with a knocker shaped like a sleeping fox. The light comes from under the door.
> "Well," says the cat, in a perfectly ordinary voice, "knock."
- **Setup:** Biscuit can talk and has always been able to. He wrote the notes. He is the Door's keeper for this world. Behind the door is a short tunnel into the Emberwood proper, and a thing the cat wants the heroes to see: a dwarven rune-stone, cracked, leaking ember-light.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha knocks. Arya reads the rune (it is a picture of a sleeping dragon with its eyes open).
- **Choices:**
  - 🚪 Knock
  - 🐱 Ask the cat what he wants
  - 🔍 Look at the light first
- **Outcomes:** The door opens into the forest. The rune-stone is one of many seals holding something under the mountain asleep. This one is cracked. Sunder the dragon can feel it.
- **Image:** A hollow oak in a hedge with a fox-shaped door-knocker, warm light under the door, a ginger cat sitting in front of it at dusk

### Scene 4: The thing in the hedge (action)
> The forest at night is full of little lights: glowing mushrooms, fireflies, the trees themselves
> faintly gold. And one bigger light, coming toward you through the trunks. A ball of ember-light
> with legs, like a lantern that has learned to walk. It stops in front of Keisha and crackles.
> It seems to want something.
- **Setup:** An **Ember-Wisp** (dm/bestiary.md): a sliver of what sleeps under the mountain. Harmless if calmed; sparky if scared. It is drawn to the kids. Calm it (Animals or Nature Easy) and it follows the party as a lantern. Fight it and it splits in two.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha holds out her hand. The wisp lands. Arya names it (canon; it stays).
- **Choices:**
  - 🤲 Hold out your hand
  - 🗡️ Attack it
  - 🏃 Run
  - 🍄 Offer a glowing mushroom (it eats it; very pleased)
- **Outcomes:** The wisp joins the party as a light source. Biscuit says: "That's a piece of the Ember. There are more cracks. We should talk to Wren."
- **Image:** A small ball of ember-light with tiny legs landing on a child's outstretched hand in a glowing night forest, a cat watching
- **Music:** gentle chimes

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The crows do laugh at the scarecrow. Keisha tells them off. They stop.
2. Grizzle's mushrooms make a hero's nose glow for the session.
3. Mayor Bramble follows the party into the hedge and gets stuck. Pull him out.
4. The oak door is locked. The fox knocker wants a riddle ("What has a tail and a door?" A fox. Also this oak).
5. The wisp sets a leaf on fire. Stamp it out; the wisp apologises in sparks.
6. Biscuit falls asleep mid-sentence. He is a cat.

## Rewards
- 100 XP. Level 2. The wisp (companion, 3 HP, light). Hob the scarecrow as hedge guard. Grizzle's trade token. Council +1, Goblins +1.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The Ember is waking under the mountain. The seals are cracking. Biscuit knows someone who can
read dwarven: a seven-year-old wizard in a tower who will not come out until someone beats her at chess.
