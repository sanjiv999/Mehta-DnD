# Chapter 1: The Notice Board

## Premise
The heroes take their first jobs from Hearth's notice board: a missing cat (the councillor), a
goblin who overcharged a widow, and a strange light in the hedge. All three lead to the same
hollow oak.

## Scenes
### Scene 1: The board (social)
### Scene 2: The night market (exploration)
### Scene 3: The thing in the hedge (action): an animated scarecrow that only wants a hat.
## Rewards
A goblin trade token, the councillor cat's loyalty, 100 XP each.
