# Lake Kagami

**Built by:** The lake was here first. The lantern on the island was carried out on a raft two hundred years ago by a keeper who thought the spirits would see it better over water.
**First seen:** The maples, red, upside down in the water. Then snow on them. Then the island lantern, with ice on it.
**Hour:** Still at all hours. The snow started three weeks ago.
**Sound and smell:** Nothing. Then the creak of ice. Pine, cold.
**Who is here now:** Keeper Yuki. The dragon under the surface. Her daughter, who comes to the shore at night.
**Recently changed:** It is autumn, and it is snowing.
