# Castle Shirogane

**Built by:** A lord's grandfather, white-plastered, on a hill, with a town below it and a checkpoint on the road where papers are read. The castle is warded: no spirit can enter without an invitation, which was once a courtesy and is now a wall.
**First seen:** Banners. The checkpoint, with a queue. The castle above, white.
**Hour:** The autumn festival: the lion-dance on the stage, fireworks after dark.
**Sound and smell:** Lion-dance drums, grilled squid, fireworks, paper.
**Who is here now:** Lord Shirogane and his baby, Captain Masaru at the checkpoint, the wardens, a hundred tanuki disguised as umbrella sellers outside the gate, and Kiko and Daigoro, who cannot come in.
**Recently changed:** The courtyard lantern is out and the lord does not know what it was for.
