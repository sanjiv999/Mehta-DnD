# The Bamboo Sea

**Built by:** Nobody. The road was cut through it and the forest has been trying to close it since. The tanuki village of Pon-Pon in the middle is built of drums, because tanuki drum.
**First seen:** The light goes green and the air goes cool. A signpost with four arms. The signpost has five arms when you look again.
**Hour:** Dusk, always, under the canopy. At night, drumming.
**Sound and smell:** Creaking stalks, drums a long way off, wet leaves, sake from somewhere.
**Who is here now:** Ponkichi and a hundred tanuki, on strike. Mischief sprites. A bird that knows the forest's song.
**Recently changed:** The parade has not walked for a month and the paths have stopped holding still.
