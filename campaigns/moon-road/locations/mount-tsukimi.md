# Mount Tsukimi

**Built by:** Pilgrims, one step and one lantern at a time, over four hundred years. The shrine at the top is one room: a broom, a kettle, a bed, the first lantern.
**First seen:** Steps going up into mist. A lantern every ten steps. The ones behind you are lit; the ones ahead are not.
**Hour:** Night, under the moon the mountain is named for.
**Sound and smell:** Wind, a bell every ten steps, cold clean air, and near the top, tea.
**Who is here now:** Old Keeper Hoshi. A tengu with a riddle. The mountain spirits, waiting below for a road of light.
**Recently changed:** A straw hat on the step that the keeper does not remember buying.
