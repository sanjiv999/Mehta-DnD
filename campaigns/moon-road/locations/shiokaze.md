# Shiokaze

**Built by:** Fishers, along one street with deep eaves against the wind. The pier was built for the pilgrims who used to land here, and the torii at its end marks where the road begins, because the shrine is the mountain.
**First seen:** The gate in the water. Then the drying racks, strips of fish stiff in the wind. Then the cold lantern at the land end of the pier.
**Hour:** Boats go out at first light and the village sleeps after dark. Nothing has gone out this week because the lantern is cold.
**Sound and smell:** Wind chimes, gulls, salt, fish, wet rope.
**Who is here now:** The fishers, who will not launch. A cat that owns the pier. Gen in the tide pools. Kame on the sand. Masaru, passing through on a horse he does not need.
**Recently changed:** Three-toed wet footprints lead up the hill from the lantern.
