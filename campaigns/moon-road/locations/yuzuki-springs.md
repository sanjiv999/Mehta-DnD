# Yuzuki Springs

**Built by:** Innkeepers, around a hot spring, up a hillside, each inn a storey above the last. The water comes out of the rock too hot to touch and is cooled in wooden tubs under the pines.
**First seen:** Steam. Then the inns, stacked. Then a monkey sitting on a roof with somebody's towel.
**Hour:** Evening, when the clogs go up and down the wet wooden stairs and every door is open.
**Sound and smell:** Clogs on wood, water running, sulphur, grilled rice cakes, monkeys arguing.
**Who is here now:** Okami Tama at the Moonlight Inn. Kappa Shin among the pipes. Kasa in a corner. Daigoro in custody. Bath monkeys everywhere. A pilgrim in a straw hat who checked out at dawn.
**Recently changed:** The station lantern is gone from its post, not blown out. Lifted.
