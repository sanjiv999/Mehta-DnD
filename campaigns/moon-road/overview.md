# Lanterns of the Moon Road

## The pitch (read this to the players)
There is an old road from the sea to a mountain. It has six stations, and at each end of each
station's single street there is a stone lantern on a post. Every autumn, when the mountain
spirits come back up from the valleys to sleep through the winter, the villages light the
lanterns at dusk so the spirits can find their way. Most people have forgotten that this is why.
They light them because their grandmothers did.

Seventy years ago the Shogun built a wider road down in the valley, and the travellers went
there. Grass grows between the paving stones of the old road now. This autumn, its lanterns are
going out, one station at a time, from the sea toward the mountain, and nobody can say why.

You arrive through a red gate standing in the sea at the end of a pier, with no shrine behind it.
A small fox with two tails is sitting on the pier. She says she has forgotten her name, and that
you are late.

## The world in five facts
1. Spirits are everywhere and mostly polite. Rudeness is the real danger.
2. Paper, salt and song work on spirits. A sword only annoys them.
3. The Shogun's wardens keep order on the roads and cannot see spirits. They think you are talking to yourself.
4. Bow to a kappa and it must bow back, and the water spills from its head, and it is honest until it refills.
5. The dragon under Lake Kagami dreams the weather. This month she has been dreaming winter.

## What heroes do here
Walk the road, station by station. At each, help whoever keeps it, relight the lantern, earn a
paper charm, and learn a little more about the one in the straw hat who is blowing them out.
Then climb the mountain.

## Factions
| Faction | What they want this autumn | Face |

| The shrine keepers | The lanterns lit, and the rice that used to come | Old Keeper Hoshi |
| The road wardens | Order, papers, and for the strangers to stop waving at nothing | Captain Masaru |
| The Hundred-Night Parade | An invitation | Boss Ponkichi |
| The lake | A better dream | Ryūko's daughter |
| Whoever is in the straw hat | Nobody knows yet | |

## Hooks for each hero
- A **kid hero** is chosen by the fox, who climbs onto her shoulder and will not be moved.
- A **grown-up hero** has walked this road in a dream and knows what the sixth station looks like before seeing it.
- Any hero's **keepsake** goes warm when the first lantern is relit.
