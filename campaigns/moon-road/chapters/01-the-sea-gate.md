# Chapter 1: The Sea Gate

## Premise
The heroes step through the Door and arrive at Shiokaze, a fishing town. The first lantern on
the pier has gone cold, and the fishers will not launch without it. A kappa stole the flame to
warm a sick sea turtle. Meanwhile, a shadow in a straw hat has been seen on the rooftops.

## Goal the party will understand
"Relight the pier lantern so the boats can go out."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The fox on the pier (social)
> Salt wind. Grey waves. A red gate standing in the sea with nothing behind it but more sea, and
> you have just walked through it. On the pier a small fox with two tails sits very straight.
> "You're late," she says. "The lantern went out at dawn. I'd light it myself, but I've forgotten
> my name, and a fox without a name can't hold fire." She looks at the smallest of you. "You'll do."
- **Setup:** Kiko the kitsune joins the party, riding on a kid hero's shoulder. She knows the road and speaks in riddles when it matters.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha agrees to carry Kiko. Arya asks Kiko one question (Kiko answers with a riddle; the answer is "footprints").
- **Choices:**
  - 🦊 Ask Kiko what happened
  - 🎣 Ask the fishers
  - 🏮 Look at the cold lantern
- **Outcomes:** The lantern is cold and wet. Tiny wet footprints with three toes lead up the hill.
- **Image:** A two-tailed fox sitting on a stone pier in front of a red torii in the sea, speaking to a small child
- **Music:** koto and waves

### Scene 2: Three-toed footprints (exploration)
> The footprints go up the hill, around the fish-drying racks, through a cat's territory (the
> cat is not pleased), and into a tide pool behind the shrine. Something green is in the pool,
> hugging a turtle, and it is crying.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Follow the footprints. Count the toes (three: a kappa). Keisha greets the cat correctly (a bow).
- **Choices:**
  - 🙇 Bow to the kappa (its head-dish spills; it must bow back and is now honest)
  - 🥒 Offer a cucumber (kappa love them; the fishers have one)
  - 😠 Demand the flame
- **Rolls:** Persuasion Easy. Bowing works automatically; this is the campaign's first lesson.
- **Outcomes:** The kappa, **Kappa Gen**, stole the lantern's flame to keep a sick turtle warm. The turtle is a spirit turtle, Old Kame, who carries messages between the sea and the mountain.
- **Image:** A small green kappa with a water-dish on its head hugging a huge old turtle in a tide pool, a child bowing to it

### Scene 3: The turtle's fever (exploration, care)
> Old Kame's shell is cold where it should be warm, and warm where it should be cold. "Mountain
> sickness," says Kiko. "He came down the road to carry a message and the lanterns were dark, so
> he lost his way, and now he's confused inside." The kappa sniffs. "I was only trying to help."
- **Setup:** Heal the turtle: Medicine Tricky, or Animals Tricky, or a kid sings to him (automatic). Then he gives the message he was carrying, which is from the mountain shrine: "Someone is walking the road the wrong way."
- **Jobs for the kids:** Sing to the turtle. Keisha names the song. Arya writes the message down (it is the campaign's first clue).
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Sing to Kame
  - 🩹 Treat the shell (Medicine)
  - 🔥 Give him the flame for one more hour, then take it back
- **Outcomes:** Kame recovers. Gen gives back the flame in a cupped leaf. Kame will carry one message for the party later (a free "send help" once per campaign).
- **Image:** Children singing to an enormous old sea turtle on the sand at dusk, a kappa sniffling nearby, a leaf holding a tiny flame

### Scene 4: The straw hat (action)
> The flame goes back into the pier lantern and it blooms warm and gold. The fishers cheer.
> And on the roof of the fish-shed, a shadow in a straw hat raises a hand and blows, gently, the
> way you blow out a birthday candle. The lantern flickers. Kiko's fur stands on end.
- **Setup:** Rooftop chase after the **Hollow Monk's shadow** (dm/bestiary.md). It cannot be caught this chapter, but it can be chased off, and it drops something.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha shields the lantern with her hands (it stays lit; cannot fail). Arya spots which way the shadow runs.
- **Choices:**
  - 🏃 Chase across the rooftops
  - 🛡️ Protect the lantern
  - 🧂 Throw salt (Kiko suggests it; it works: the shadow hisses and flees)
- **Rolls:** Acrobatics Tricky to keep up. Salt works automatically.
- **Outcomes:** The shadow drops a **paper charm with a hole burned in it**. First clue. The lantern stays lit. First **ofuda** earned: advantage against fear, once.
- **Image:** A shadowy figure in a wide straw hat on a rooftop blowing toward a glowing stone lantern, a child shielding the flame with cupped hands
- **Music:** fast taiko drum, then quiet

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The cat demands tribute. A fish will do.
2. Captain Masaru of the Road Wardens asks what the heroes are "waving at". Bluffing Easy.
3. Gen's cucumber was the last one in town. Somebody has to apologise to the cook.
4. Kame sneezes; everyone within ten feet is now slightly salty.
5. A fisher offers a boat ride to the next station; take it for a short, lovely interlude.
6. Kiko remembers one letter of her name: "K".

## Rewards
- 100 XP. The first ofuda. Kame's message. Kiko as a companion (3 HP, trick: one riddle-answer per session).
- Reputation: Shrine Keepers +1.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The road inland is lined with dark lanterns. The next station is the Bamboo Sea, where the
Hundred-Night Parade has been cancelled because the tanuki are "on strike".
