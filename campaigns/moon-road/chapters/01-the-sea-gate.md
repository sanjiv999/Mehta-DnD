# Chapter 1: The Sea Gate

## Premise
The heroes arrive through the torii in the sea at the fishing town of Shiokaze, where the first
lantern has just gone out and the fishers are too afraid to launch.

## Scenes
### Scene 1: The empty pier (exploration)
- **Setup:** Wet stone, the lantern cold, a trail of tiny wet footprints leading up the hill.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Follow the footprints; count how many toes (three: a kappa).
### Scene 2: The kappa's apology (social)
- **Setup:** A kappa stole the lantern flame to warm a sick turtle. Bowing empties its head-dish.
- **Choices:** Help the turtle (Medicine or Animals, Tricky) or trade for the flame.
### Scene 3: The Hollow Monk's shadow (action)
- **Setup:** As the lantern is relit, a shadow in a straw hat tries to blow it out. Chase across rooftops.
## Rewards
First ofuda (advantage against fear, once). 100 XP each. The kappa becomes a friend.
