# Chapter 1: The Sea Gate

## Premise
The heroes come through the Door onto a pier in a fishing village. The lantern at the land end
of the pier went out at dawn and the fishers will not launch without it; a boat that goes out
under a cold lantern does not come back, everyone knows that. Kappa Gen took the flame in a leaf
to keep a sick sea turtle warm. The turtle is carrying a message from the mountain. And on the
roof of the fish-shed, a shadow in a straw hat is waiting for the lantern to be relit so it can
blow it out again.

## Goal the party will understand
"Light the pier lantern so the boats can go out."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: You're late (social)
> A red gate stands in the sea at the end of a stone pier, and you have just walked through it
> with wet feet. Behind you, more sea. Ahead, a village of deep-eaved houses with fish drying
> on racks, and every boat pulled up on the stones. Nobody is on the water. On the pier a small
> fox with two tails is sitting very straight. "You're late," she says. "The lantern went out
> at dawn. I'd light it myself, but I've forgotten my name, and a fox without a name can't
> hold fire." She looks at the smallest of you. "You'll do."
- **Setup:** Morning, grey. Kiko climbs onto a kid hero's shoulder. The fishers will talk if given tea first; Masaru is at the village well watering a horse and watching strangers.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu agrees to carry Kiko and asks her one thing; Kiko answers with a riddle whose answer is "footprints." Aarya counts the boats on the stones (eleven; none out).
- **Choices:**
  - 🦊 Ask Kiko what happened
  - 🎣 Ask a fisher, over tea
  - 🏮 Look at the lantern
  - 🐴 Ask the samurai by the well (he asks for your papers)
- **Outcomes:** The lantern is cold and the stone around it is wet. Small wet prints with three toes go up the hill past the drying racks.
- **Image:** A two-tailed fox sitting upright on a stone pier in front of a red gate in the sea, a small child crouched to talk to her
- **Music:** koto and waves

### Scene 2: Three toes (exploration)
> The prints go up between the fish racks, through a yard where a cat is sitting in the
> middle of the path and does not intend to move, around the shrine fence, which is hung with
> dozens of worn-out straw sandals, and down into a tide pool behind it. Something green is
> crouched in the pool with its arms round a turtle the size of a cart wheel, and it is crying.
- **Setup:** The cat must be bowed to or paid in fish. The sandal fence is where pilgrims hang their worn pair for luck; there have been no new ones for years. Gen bows first, out of caution.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Follow the prints. Count the toes. Keshu bows to the cat (it lets him pass).
- **Choices:**
  - 🙇 Bow to the kappa (he bows back; his dish spills; he is honest and harmless)
  - 🥒 Offer a cucumber (a fisher's wife has one and a story about her son's name on it)
  - 😠 Demand the flame (he panics and runs; the turtle is left)
- **Rolls:** Persuasion Easy. Bowing works without a roll and is the campaign's first lesson.
- **Outcomes:** Gen has the flame in a cupped leaf under his arm. He took it because the turtle was cold. The turtle is Old Kame, and he is carrying a message.
- **Image:** A small green kappa hugging an enormous old turtle in a tide pool, a child bowing to him, a fence of worn straw sandals behind
- **Music:** gulls, water in a pool

### Scene 3: A message from the mountain (care)
> The turtle's shell is cold where it should be warm and warm where it should be cold, and his
> eyes are half shut. A folded paper is tied to one flipper with red thread. "He came down the
> road with a message," Kiko says, "and the lanterns were dark, so he lost the road, and now
> he's lost inside as well." The kappa sniffs. "I was only trying to help."
- **Setup:** Warm the turtle: Medicine Tricky, Animals Tricky, or a kid sings to him (works). Then the message, in shaking handwriting: "Someone is walking the road the wrong way."
- **Jobs for the kids:** Sing to Kame. Keshu names the song. Aarya unties the red thread and reads the message aloud.
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Sing
  - 🩹 Treat the shell (Medicine)
  - 🔥 Give Kame the flame for one more hour, then take it to the lantern
- **Outcomes:** Kame recovers enough to swim. Gen gives back the flame. Kame will carry one message for the party later, to the sea or the mountain.
- **Image:** Two children singing to an old sea turtle on the sand at dusk, a kappa sniffling beside them, a leaf holding a small flame
- **Music:** one voice, waves

### Scene 4: Straw hat (action)
> The flame goes into the lantern and takes, and the fishers on the stones cheer and start
> dragging boats. On the roof of the fish-shed a figure in a wide straw hat stands up. It
> raises a hand to its mouth and blows, the way you blow out a birthday candle. The lantern
> gutters. The fox's fur stands on end.
- **Setup:** The **Shadow** (dm/bestiary.md) cannot be caught this chapter. It flees from salt and from a lantern held close. It drops something.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu cups his hands round the flame (it stays lit; cannot fail). Aarya sees which way the hat goes.
- **Choices:**
  - 🏃 Chase it over the roofs
  - 🛡️ Guard the lantern
  - 🧂 Throw salt (Kiko: "salt!"; it works; the shadow hisses and goes)
- **Rolls:** Acrobatics Tricky to keep up on wet roofs. Salt works without a roll.
- **Outcomes:** A paper charm on the roof tiles, a keeper's charm from the mountain shrine, with a hole burned through where the seal should be. The lantern holds. First **ofuda**: advantage against fear, once. Kiko: "K. That's a letter. I think it's mine."
- **Image:** A figure in a wide straw hat on a rooftop blowing toward a stone lantern, a child below shielding the flame with cupped hands
- **Music:** one drum hit, then quiet

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The cat wants fish. A fisher's wife provides it, for a story.
2. Masaru asks what the heroes are "waving at". Bluffing Easy, or honesty, which he writes down.
3. The cucumber was the cook's last. Apologise.
4. Kame sneezes. Everyone within ten feet is salted.
5. A fisher offers a boat ride to the next station. A quiet, lovely half hour; Kame swims alongside.
6. The sandal fence falls over. Hanging the sandals back up, in order, is a job Keshu takes seriously.

## Rewards
- 100 XP. The first ofuda. Kame's message. Kiko as a companion (3 HP, one riddle-answer per session).
- Shrine keepers +1.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The boats go out. The road inland is lined with dark lanterns. The second station is a forest
whose paths do not stay still, and the tanuki who keep them still are on strike.
