# Chapter 2: The Bamboo Sea

## Premise
The Bamboo Sea rearranges its paths every hour. The station lantern sits in a tanuki village
in the middle, and the tanuki, who normally carry the Hundred-Night Parade through the forest to
keep the paths honest, are on strike because nobody invited them to the Shogun's festival. With
no parade, the forest is lost, and so is the lantern.

## Goal the party will understand
"Find the lantern in the moving forest and get the parade going again."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The paths move (exploration, puzzle)
> The bamboo is so tall it makes its own evening. Paths open to the left and close behind you.
> A signpost points three ways and then, while you are reading it, points four. Kiko sighs. "The
> forest only stays still for a parade. Or for someone who knows its song."
- **Setup:** Navigation puzzle. Three ways to pass: learn the forest song from a bird (Nature Tricky), follow a tanuki's drum (Perception Easy), or walk with eyes closed holding hands (kids' way; works).
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha holds everyone's hands in a chain. Arya listens for the drum and points.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥁 Follow the drum
  - 🐦 Learn the song from the bird
  - 🙈 Walk with eyes closed, holding hands
  - 🪓 Cut through (the forest is offended; Mischief Sprites appear)
- **Outcomes:** The party reaches the tanuki village, Pon-Pon, where a hundred round creatures are sitting with their arms folded.
- **Image:** Four children in a chain holding hands walking through towering bamboo, eyes closed, a fox on a shoulder pointing

### Scene 2: The strike (social)
> Pon-Pon is a village of drums. Big drums for houses, small drums for stools, and on every drum
> a tanuki with its arms folded. Boss Ponkichi, round as a moon with a leaf on his head, glowers.
> "No parade. The Shogun's festival didn't invite us. Fifty years we've kept this forest honest.
> So. Strike." Behind him the station lantern sits dark, with a tanuki asleep on top of it.
- **Setup:** Negotiation. The tanuki want an invitation. The heroes can promise one (they will need Captain Masaru), perform for them, or join the parade themselves.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha tries a drum. Ponkichi is impressed despite himself. Arya writes an invitation in her best handwriting.
- **Choices:**
  - ✉️ Promise a real invitation
  - 🎭 Put on a show for the tanuki
  - 🥁 Offer to carry the parade lanterns yourselves
  - 💰 Bribe with rice cakes (they eat them and keep striking)
- **Rolls:** Performance Tricky for a show. Persuasion Tricky for the promise.
- **Outcomes:** Ponkichi agrees to one parade tonight, if the heroes carry the lanterns and a kid leads the drum.
- **Image:** A village of giant drums with round tanuki sitting on them arms folded, a boss tanuki with a leaf on his head glaring at a child holding a drumstick

### Scene 3: The Hundred-Night Parade (action, celebration)
> Night. A hundred lanterns light at once. The tanuki stand up, stretch, and transform: into
> teapots, into umbrellas with one eye, into enormous versions of themselves. Keisha hits the
> drum. The forest, all at once, holds still, and the paths line up like a road of light to the
> station lantern. And at the far end of that road, something in a straw hat is waiting.
- **Setup:** The parade marches. Every hero carries a lantern. The forest's **Mischief Sprites** try to blow them out (snatch, tickle). At the end: the Hollow Monk's shadow, stronger here (dm/bestiary.md: Shadow, 12 HP), between the party and the lantern.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha keeps the drum beat (if she stops, the paths move; she will not stop). Arya protects her lantern from sprites (Dexterity save Easy).
- **Choices:**
  - 🥁 Keep drumming no matter what
  - 🧂 Salt the shadow
  - 📜 Use the first ofuda
  - 🌙 Ask Ponkichi to transform into something huge
- **Rolls:** Attacks on the Shadow at normal odds while a lantern is near it; it flees at 0 HP and drops a second burned charm.
- **Outcomes:** The station lantern is relit by the parade's hundred lamps. Ponkichi is moved. The tanuki are, as of tonight, friends of the party.
- **Image:** A night parade of a hundred glowing lanterns through bamboo, tanuki transformed into teapots and umbrellas with eyes, a small child drumming at the front
- **Music:** taiko, flute, laughter

### Scene 4: The charm with a hole (social, investigation)
> In the morning Ponkichi serves tea on a drum. Kiko lays the two burned charms side by side.
> Each has a hole in exactly the same place: where the shrine's seal should be. "These are
> keeper's charms," Kiko says slowly. "From Mount Tsukimi. Whoever is blowing out the lanterns
> was given these by the mountain shrine itself."
- **Jobs for the kids:** Match the two charms. Decide what to tell the tanuki.
- **Choices:**
  - 🔍 Keep the charms as evidence
  - 📨 Send Kame with a message to the mountain
  - 🤫 Tell no one yet
- **Outcomes:** Second ofuda: a lantern-charm that lights any lantern once. Ponkichi gives a tiny drum that summons one tanuki for a scene. The road goes on to Lake Kagami.
- **Image:** Tea on a drum in morning light, two burned paper charms side by side, a fox and a tanuki boss peering at them

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The paths move while someone is tying a shoe. They are now alone; Kiko finds them by riddle.
2. A tanuki transforms into a copy of a hero. The copy is terrible at it. Everyone laughs.
3. Captain Masaru rides through and sees "a child drumming in an empty forest". He is confused and leaves.
4. A sprite steals Keisha's drumstick. A kid's job: get it back by being funnier than a sprite.
5. The lanterns attract fireflies in the thousands. Just enjoy it.
6. Kiko remembers another letter: "I".

## Rewards
- 150 XP. The lantern-charm ofuda. Ponkichi's drum. Hundred-Night Parade +2.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The charms came from the mountain. The next lantern is at Lake Kagami, where it has started to
snow in autumn because the dragon is having nightmares.
