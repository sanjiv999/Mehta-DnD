# Chapter 3: The Dreaming Lake

## Premise
Ryūko dreams the weather. A month ago a keeper's charm with a hole in it drifted into the lake
and settled beside her ear, humming "sleep, winter, sleep" in Hoshi's sleeping voice, and she
has been dreaming winter since. The island lantern is in a block of ice. The only way to change
the weather is to go into the dream and give her daughter a better one, and whatever the
children tell her becomes the climate for the rest of the road.

## Goal the party will understand
"Stop the snow, light the island lantern, and find out who is whispering to the dragon."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Snow on maples (exploration)
> It is autumn and it is snowing. Red leaves come down and snow comes down with them and the
> lake is so still the maples are in it upside down. Under the surface something very large
> turns over, slowly, and the snow comes harder. On the shore a woman in a padded coat is
> sweeping snow off a rowing boat that is already clean. "You're the lantern-lighters," she
> says. "Good. Tea. The island lantern's frozen and she won't wake. I've tried everything but
> going in."
- **Setup:** Keeper Yuki. The lantern on the island is iced solid and refreezes if melted. The way in is sleep; Yuki's tea does it.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha picks a maple leaf to take into the dream; it matters. Arya looks at the lake and says what the dragon is dreaming: a figure in a straw hat, bent over, whispering.
- **Choices:**
  - 🚣 Row to the island first
  - 🧊 Try to melt the ice (it refreezes while you watch)
  - 😴 Drink the tea
- **Outcomes:** You sleep on the floor of Yuki's hut with the fire going.
- **Image:** A still lake reflecting red maples with snow falling, a keeper in a padded coat sweeping a boat, a long shape under the water
- **Music:** nothing, then one koto string

### Scene 2: Walking on the lake (surreal)
> You wake up standing on the lake, and the lake is the sky, and you are walking on it. Fish go
> past at shoulder height. The maples are blue. Your voice comes back a moment after you speak.
> Kiko has three tails here and is delighted. "A dream," she says. "The rules are soft. Be kind
> to everything. It's all her."
- **Setup:** Three dream rooms, in any order: the Room of Lost Things (each hero finds something they really lost; ask the kids what; it is real in the dream), the Room of the Whisper (the charm, humming), the Room of the Egg (the dragon's daughter, asleep).
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha finds something she actually lost at home. Arya hears the whisper and repeats it exactly.
- **Choices:**
  - 🔎 Lost things
  - 👂 The whisper
  - 🥚 The egg
- **Rolls:** WIS save Easy against the whisper's sleepiness. In a dream any good idea works at Easy.
- **Outcomes:** The whisper is a charm with a hole in it lying by the dragon's ear, humming in an old man's sleeping voice. It can be picked up. The daughter wakes and asks for a story.
- **Image:** Children walking on the surface of a lake that is also the sky, fish passing at shoulder height, blue maples, a fox with three tails
- **Music:** slow koto, underwater

### Scene 3: A better dream (creative)
> The dragon's daughter is the size of a cat and gold, with a crest she has not grown into.
> She blinks up at you. "I had a bad dream," she says. "A man kept saying winter. Tell me a
> good one. Then I'll tell Mama." Kiko, quietly: "Whatever you tell her, she'll dream. And
> whatever she dreams, the weather will be. For the whole road."
- **Setup:** The kids tell her a dream. The DM writes it into state.yaml as the campaign's weather from now on. This is canon.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Tell the dream, both of them. Keisha's maple leaf goes into it.
- **Choices:**
  - ☀️ Sun
  - 🌈 Rainbows
  - 🎉 A festival every day
  - 🎨 Something else
- **Outcomes:** Under you, the lake turns over and sighs. The snow stops in the air.
- **Image:** A gold dragon the size of a cat curled in a child's lap listening, maple leaves and small stars drifting around them
- **Music:** a lullaby, hummed

### Scene 4: The island (action)
> You wake on Yuki's floor. The snow has stopped and the sky is whatever you told her. Yuki is
> already at the oars. On the island the ice around the lantern is cracking by itself, and in
> the cracks small jagged things are waking up and are not pleased about it.
- **Setup:** Four **Ice-Sprites** (dm/bestiary.md). They melt when warmed: a lantern-charm, a song, a hug.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha lights the lantern with the lantern-charm. Arya hugs a sprite; it melts into a puddle and says "oh, thank you."
- **Choices:**
  - 📜 Lantern-charm
  - 🤗 Warm them
  - ⚔️ Fight
- **Outcomes:** The lantern lights. The lake lifts, and for the length of a breath there is a silver dragon in the air over the island, looking down at the small people, and then there is a lake again, and on the shore a dragon scale for each of them. Third ofuda: the weather-charm (change the weather for one scene). Kiko: "K again. K, I, K."
- **Image:** A silver dragon rising from a lake over a small island with a lit stone lantern, children on the shore with their arms up, the maples red
- **Music:** a gong, once

## Complications (roll d6)
1. In the dream a hero's shoes are fish. They walk fine.
2. The daughter wants a second story before she will go and tell her mother. Keisha has one.
3. Yuki's boat leaks. Kiko plugs it with a tail and is furious about it.
4. The charm tries to put a hero to sleep on the way out. Shake them.
5. The new weather is "cherry blossom falling instead of rain". It does, for the rest of the road.
6. Yuki, at the end, opens the letter she has never opened. She reads it and says nothing and makes more tea.

## Rewards
- 200 XP. Level 4. Dragon scales. The weather-charm. Lake +3.
- The third charm, which was whispering: a recording, not a person.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The weather is whatever the children chose. The next station is a village of hot springs where
the lantern was not blown out. It was taken.
