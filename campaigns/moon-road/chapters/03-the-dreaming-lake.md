# Chapter 3: The Dreaming Lake

## Premise
Ryūko the dragon sleeps under Lake Kagami and dreams the weather. Someone has been whispering to
her in her sleep, and now she dreams of winter, so it snows. The station lantern is on an island
in the lake, frozen. To relight it the heroes must enter the dragon's dream and give her a better one.

## Goal the party will understand
"Stop the snow, wake the lantern, and find out who is whispering to the dragon."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Snow on the maples (exploration)
> It is autumn, and it is snowing. Red maple leaves fall and white snow falls with them, and the
> lake is so still it is a mirror. In the mirror, under the surface, something enormous turns
> over in its sleep, and the snow falls harder. On the shore a woman in a keeper's robe is
> sweeping snow off a boat, uselessly. "You're the lantern-lighters," she says. "Good. The
> island lantern is frozen. And the dragon won't wake. I've tried everything but going in."
- **Setup:** Keeper **Yuki** has a boat. The island lantern is in a block of ice. The only way to stop the snow is to change the dream.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha chooses a maple leaf to bring into the dream (it matters). Arya looks into the mirror-lake and describes what the dragon is dreaming (a dark figure in a straw hat, whispering).
- **Choices:**
  - 🚣 Row to the island
  - 🧊 Try to melt the lantern first (it refreezes)
  - 😴 Go to sleep on the shore and enter the dream
- **Outcomes:** The way in is to sleep. Yuki makes tea that helps.
- **Image:** A still lake reflecting red maples and falling snow, a shrine keeper sweeping snow off a small boat, a dragon's shape under the water

### Scene 2: Into the dream (exploration, surreal)
> You wake up in the lake, but the lake is the sky, and you are walking on it. Fish swim through
> the air. The maples are blue. Your own voices come out slightly delayed. Kiko has three tails
> here. "Dream," she says, delighted. "Rules are soft. Be kind to everything; it's all her."
- **Setup:** Dream logic. Three dream-rooms: the Room of Lost Things (each hero finds something they lost; a kid's keepsake moment), the Room of the Whisper (the Hollow Monk's voice, saying "sleep, winter, sleep"), and the Room of the Egg (the dragon's heart: a sleeping dragon-child).
- **Jobs for the kids:** In the Room of Lost Things, Keisha finds something she actually lost in real life (ask her); it is real in the dream. Arya hears the whisper and repeats it so the party knows the words.
- **Choices:**
  - 🔎 Room of Lost Things
  - 👂 Room of the Whisper
  - 🥚 Room of the Egg
- **Rolls:** WIS saves Easy against the whisper's sleepiness. In a dream, any good idea works at Easy.
- **Outcomes:** The whisper is a recording, not a person: a charm with a hole in it, humming on a loop beside the dragon's ear. The party can take it. The dragon-child wakes and asks for a story.
- **Image:** Children walking on the surface of a lake that is also the sky, fish swimming through the air, blue maples, a fox with three tails
- **Music:** slow koto, underwater echo

### Scene 3: A better dream (social, creative)
> The dragon-child, small as a cat and gold as a coin, blinks up at you. "I had a bad dream," she
> says. "Tell me a good one. Then I'll tell Mama." Kiko whispers: "Whatever you tell her, she will
> dream. And whatever she dreams, the weather will be."
- **Setup:** The kids tell the dragon a dream. The DM turns it into the weather for the rest of the campaign (rainbows, warm wind, "snow but only on Sundays"). This is canon.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Tell the dream. Both kids contribute. Keisha's maple leaf becomes part of it.
- **Choices:**
  - ☀️ A dream of sunshine
  - 🌈 A dream of rainbows
  - 🎉 A dream of a festival
  - 🎨 Your own dream
- **Outcomes:** Ryūko turns over and sighs. The snow stops mid-air. The dream dissolves gently.
- **Image:** A tiny golden dragon curled in a child's lap listening to a story, stars and maple leaves drifting around them

### Scene 4: The island lantern (action)
> You wake on the shore and the snow is gone and the sky is whatever you dreamed. Yuki is already
> rowing. On the island the ice around the lantern is cracking by itself, and in the cracks,
> ice-sprites are waking up grumpy: little jagged things that do not want to melt.
- **Setup:** Short fight: **Ice-Sprites** (dm/bestiary.md), four of them. They melt when warmed: a lantern-charm, a song, or a hug (kids' choice; a hug works).
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha uses the lantern-charm ofuda. Arya hugs an ice-sprite; it melts into a puddle and says "oh, thank you".
- **Choices:**
  - 📜 Use the lantern-charm
  - 🤗 Warm the sprites
  - ⚔️ Fight them
- **Outcomes:** The lantern is relit. Ryūko surfaces, briefly, enormous and kind, and gives each hero a **dragon scale** (float on water once). Third ofuda: the weather-charm (change the weather for one scene).
- **Image:** A vast silver dragon surfacing from a lake beside a small island with a glowing stone lantern, children on the shore waving, maples red again
- **Music:** rising strings, a gong

## Complications (roll d6)
1. In the dream someone's shoes become fish. They walk fine; they just wriggle.
2. The dragon-child wants a second story. Keisha obliges.
3. Yuki's boat has a hole; Kiko plugs it with a tail.
4. The whisper-charm tries to put a hero to sleep on the way out. Shake them awake (a kid's job).
5. The new weather is "raining cherry blossoms". It is, for a week.
6. Kiko remembers: "K", "I", and now "K" again.

## Rewards
- 200 XP. Level 4. Dragon scales. The weather-charm. Lake +3.
- The third burned charm, which was whispering. The party now knows it is a *recording*, not a person.

## How it ends and what it sets up
Three charms from the mountain shrine. The weather is whatever the kids chose. The next station
is a hot-spring village of tanuki where the lantern was not blown out: it was *stolen*.
