# Chapter 5: The Castle of No Spirits

## Premise
Castle Shirogane holds the Shogun's autumn festival. The courtyard lantern is the last station
before the mountain, and the castle is warded: no spirit can enter, which means no Kiko, no
Daigoro, no tanuki. The lantern has gone out and the samurai do not even know what it is for.
The heroes must get the spirits invited, get themselves into the castle, and relight the lantern
in front of a lord who cannot see any of it.

## Goal the party will understand
"Get the tanuki their invitation, get into the castle, and light the courtyard lantern."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The festival town (social)
> Flags, food stalls, a stage with a lion-dance, and the white castle above it all like a cloud.
> Kiko stops at the first gate and winces. "Wards. I can't go further, and neither can anyone
> with more than one tail or less than two legs." Daigoro bows and sits down to wait. The tanuki,
> a hundred of them, are waiting too, in disguise as a very suspicious group of umbrella sellers.
- **Setup:** The heroes must do this one as humans. Captain Masaru can get them an audience with **Lord Shirogane**, who is kind, busy, and blind to spirits.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha picks a festival mask. Arya presents the tanuki's invitation request to Masaru.
- **Choices:**
  - 👮 Ask Masaru for an audience
  - 🎭 Join the lion-dance to get into the castle
  - 🪁 Fly over the wall (a kid's idea; it almost works)
  - ☂️ Buy an umbrella from a "seller"
- **Rolls:** Persuasion Tricky with Masaru (Easy if Daigoro was freed in chapter 4).
- **Outcomes:** An audience is granted, at the festival's end.
- **Image:** A festival town below a white castle, a lion-dance on a stage, a line of round "umbrella sellers" who are clearly tanuki in disguise

### Scene 2: The lord who cannot see (social)
> Lord Shirogane is young, tired, and holding a baby. "Lanterns?" he says. "For spirits? My
> grandmother said that. I thought it was a story." The baby reaches toward the window, where
> nobody but you can see Kiko sitting on the wall, waving. The baby waves back.
- **Setup:** Convince the lord. He cannot see spirits, but the baby can. Proof is possible: the weather-charm, the dragon scales, or the baby.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha plays with the baby, who points at every spirit in the room. Arya uses the weather-charm to make it rain cherry blossoms in the hall (the lord believes).
- **Choices:**
  - 🌸 Use the weather-charm
  - 👶 Let the baby show him
  - 📜 Show him the burned charms
  - 📖 Tell him his grandmother's story
- **Outcomes:** The lord invites the tanuki, formally, with a seal. He asks the heroes to light the lantern "if there is anything to light".
- **Image:** A young lord holding a baby in a castle hall, the baby pointing at a fox on the windowsill that the lord cannot see, cherry blossoms falling indoors

### Scene 3: The courtyard lantern (action)
> The wards drop as the invitation is read aloud. A hundred tanuki pour over the walls. Kiko
> streaks across the courtyard. And the courtyard lantern, the moment the wards fall, is
> surrounded by straw hats: a dozen Shadows, and in the middle, one that is not a copy. It is
> taller. It has a face, almost. It looks at Kiko and says, in a voice like wind in a dry field:
> "Little fox. Have you remembered your name yet?"
- **Setup:** The **Hollow Monk** appears in person (dm/bestiary.md: HP 35, cannot be killed, can be driven off). This is the pre-finale. He knows Kiko. He flees when the lantern is lit with the parade, the drum, and the salt ring.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha leads the tanuki drum. Arya throws the salt ring. Both cannot fail; they decide the order.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥁 Tanuki parade around the lantern
  - 🧂 Salt-charm ring
  - 📜 Lantern-charm to light it instantly
  - 🗡️ Fight the Monk (he is slow, sad, and hits hard)
- **Rolls:** Attacks normal. CON save Tricky against his cold breath (lose a turn).
- **Outcomes:** The lantern lights. The Monk looks at it as if it hurts him and walks away up the mountain, not running. He drops a fifth charm. On this one, not burned, is a name: **Kikyo**.
- **Image:** A castle courtyard at night, a tall hollow figure in a straw hat facing a small two-tailed fox, a ring of salt, tanuki pouring over the walls
- **Music:** stately drums, then a single flute

### Scene 4: Kikyo (social, revelation)
> Kiko stares at the charm. "Kikyo," she says. "That's... that was my name. He wrote it. He's
> the one who gave me charms, when I was the mountain shrine's fox. Before I forgot." She looks
> up the road toward the mountain. "He's not stealing the lanterns to lose the spirits. He's
> doing it so they'll come to the shrine looking for the light. So somebody will finally come."
- **Setup:** The turn. The Hollow Monk is the old mountain keeper's loneliness given a body. Kiko was his fox. She forgot her name when she left him alone.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Say Kiko's name out loud. She gets her third tail.
- **Choices:**
  - 🏔️ Go to the mountain tonight
  - 🎉 Stay for the festival first (the tanuki insist; Level 6 reward either way)
- **Outcomes:** Fifth ofuda: the name-charm (say a spirit's true name once; it must listen). Level 6.
- **Image:** A fox with three tails holding a paper charm with a name on it, looking up at a mountain under the moon

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The baby lord-to-be crawls after Kiko. Return him.
2. A tanuki disguised as a samurai is given actual orders. He is very bad at them.
3. Fireworks start during the fight. Everyone has advantage on one roll; it's just that kind of night.
4. Masaru finally sees a spirit, by accident, and sits down.
5. The lion-dance lion is a real lion-dog. It wants to help.
6. Okami Tama arrives with rice cakes for a hundred tanuki and four heroes.

## Rewards
- 300 XP. Level 6. The name-charm. Castle +3. Tanuki: a lifetime invitation.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The mountain. The shrine. The keeper. The sixth station, where the first lantern was lit.
