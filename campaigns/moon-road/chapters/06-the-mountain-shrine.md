# Chapter 6: The Mountain Shrine

## Premise
The path up Mount Tsukimi is lined with a thousand small lanterns, all of which light as the
party passes. At the top, the first shrine and the first lantern, dark, and Old Keeper Hoshi,
who is so old he has become partly a spirit himself. The Hollow Monk is what he does at night
without knowing. He wants someone to come. Someone has. The finale is kindness, a climb, and a
choice about what to do with the paper shade of the Lantern of Many Roads.

## Goal the party will understand
"Climb to the shrine, light the first lantern, and bring Kikyo home."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The thousand lanterns (exploration, wonder)
> The path up the mountain is stone steps and stone lanterns, one every ten paces, and as you pass
> each one it lights by itself. Behind you the mountain is a river of gold going down into the
> dark. Ahead it is dark still. Kikyo, three tails, walks beside you now instead of riding. "I
> walked this every night," she says. "Lighting them. Until I thought no one noticed."
- **Setup:** Travel montage with three small trials from the mountain spirits (a tengu's riddle, a stone that must be moved by two heroes together, a cold wind that only a song warms). Each gives advantage in the finale.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha answers the tengu's riddle (it is "a shadow"). Arya and a grown-up move the stone.
- **Choices:**
  - 🦅 The tengu's riddle
  - 🪨 The stone
  - 🎵 The cold wind
- **Outcomes:** The party reaches the shrine as the moon rises.
- **Image:** Four small figures and a three-tailed fox climbing a mountain path lined with lanterns that light one by one behind them
- **Music:** wind, a bell every ten steps

### Scene 2: Old Keeper Hoshi (social)
> The shrine is tiny. A sweeping-brush, a kettle, a bed. A man so old he is partly see-through
> is sitting on the step with the straw hat in his lap, looking at it as if he does not know whose
> it is. "Visitors," he says, and his voice is the dry-field wind. "Nobody comes up here. I light
> the lantern, I sweep. I had a fox once." He looks at Kikyo and does not recognise her. "She left."
- **Setup:** The Monk is Hoshi's loneliness walking at night. He does not know. Kikyo did not leave; she went down to light the road lanterns and forgot her name and the way back.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Use the name-charm: say "Kikyo" so Hoshi can hear it. Keisha hands him the kettle (he makes tea; it is the first thing he has done for someone in years).
- **Choices:**
  - 📜 Say her name with the name-charm
  - 🍵 Make him tea
  - 😠 Confront him about the lanterns (he weeps; he did not know)
  - 👹 Let Daigoro deliver his grandfather's apology (Hoshi laughs for the first time in a century)
- **Outcomes:** Hoshi remembers. The straw hat in his lap goes soft and empty. But the Monk is already out, tonight, walking down the mountain to blow out the thousand lanterns, and he cannot stop it from here.
- **Image:** A tiny mountain shrine, a translucent old keeper on the step with a straw hat in his lap, a three-tailed fox approaching him slowly

### Scene 3: The last night (action, climax)
> Below you, the river of gold is going out, lantern by lantern, as the Hollow Monk walks down.
> Hoshi stands, shaking. "That is me," he says. "That is what I do when I sleep." Kikyo puts her
> head against his hand. "Then let's wake you up."
> The Monk turns, far below, and begins to walk back up. Toward the first lantern.
- **Setup:** The finale. The Monk (HP 35, cannot be killed) climbs to blow out the first lantern. The heroes must light it before he arrives and hold it lit. Every ofuda, ally, and trial gift matters: the weather-charm (make it windless), the salt-charm (a ring), the lantern-charm (light it instantly), the tanuki (a parade around the shrine), Daigoro (holds the lantern; nothing an oni holds goes out), the dragon scales, Kame's message (summons help from the sea). Victory condition: the lantern lit, Hoshi's name said by Kikyo, and one hero sitting with Hoshi so he is not alone when the Monk arrives. Then the Monk walks into the light and is just a straw hat on the ground.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha sits with Hoshi (the most important job; cannot fail). Arya lights the first lantern with the lantern-charm.
- **Choices:**
  - 📜 Lantern-charm on the first lantern
  - 👹 Daigoro holds it
  - 🥁 Tanuki parade around the shrine
  - 🧂 Salt ring
  - 🤲 Sit with Hoshi
- **Rolls:** The Monk's breath: CON save Tricky each round; the lantern flickers on a fail and goes out on two fails in a row unless Daigoro holds it. Attacks slow him (each 10 damage = one round delay). Three rounds to light and hold.
- **Outcomes:** Dawn. Every lantern on the mountain and the road relights at once, all the way to the sea. The straw hat lies on the step. Hoshi is solid for the first time in years. Kikyo has four tails. In the first lantern, now, is the **paper shade** of the Lantern of Many Roads, painted with a fox and a mountain.
- **Image:** Dawn on a mountain top, a river of lanterns relighting all the way down to the sea, an old keeper with a four-tailed fox, a straw hat empty on the step
- **Music:** all the bells at once

### Scene 4: Going home (epilogue)
> The spirits come home. Tengu and tanuki and kappa and a dragon-child, up the road of light,
> into the mountain for the winter. Hoshi makes tea for all of them. Kikyo looks at you with her
> four tails and says, "I'm staying. But the torii in the sea is yours whenever you want it."
- **Jobs for the kids:** Choose a gift for Hoshi. Say goodbye to Kikyo.
- **Choices:**
  - 🦊 Ask Kikyo to come along (she stays; but gives a kid one of her tails as a keepsake, a fox-fire charm)
  - 🍵 Stay for tea
  - 🌊 Walk back to the sea gate
- **Outcomes:** The campaign is complete. `python tools/campaign.py complete moon-road`. The Door at the sea gate is open.
- **Image:** A procession of spirits walking up a lantern-lit road into a mountain shrine at dawn, an old keeper pouring tea for all of them

## Complications (roll d6)

3. Masaru climbed up too, out of stubbornness, and sees everything. He bows to Kikyo.

5. The Monk's breath blows a kid's hat all the way to the sea. Kame brings it back next session.
6. The dragon-child wants to hear the dream story again, during the fight.
