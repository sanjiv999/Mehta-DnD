# Bestiary (kid-safe)

## Shadow (the Hollow Monk's copy)
HP 6 (ch1: flees at any hit) / 12 (ch2) · AC 12 · Attack +3 · Damage 1d4 cold · Trick: blows at a lantern (lantern goes out unless shielded or held by an oni).
**Weakness:** salt (flees), lantern light (disadvantage within 10 ft).
**How it ends:** dissolves into a handful of straw.

## Mischief Sprite (Bamboo Sea)
HP 3 · AC 13 · Attack +2 · Damage 1 (a pinch) · Trick: snatch a small item or tickle (lose a turn).
**How it ends:** laughs, gives the item back, follows the parade.

## Ice-Sprite (Lake Kagami)
HP 5 · AC 12 · Attack +3 · Damage 1d4 cold · Trick: freezes a hero's feet to the ground for a turn.
**Weakness:** warmth. A hug, a song, or a lantern-charm melts one instantly.
**How it ends:** a puddle that says "oh, thank you".

## Bath Monkey Gang (Yuzuki)
HP 4 each · AC 13 · Attack +2 · Damage 1 (a flung towel) · Trick: steal towels.
**How it ends:** they return everything for a rice cake.

## The Hollow Monk (in person, ch5 and ch6)
HP 35 · AC 13 · Attack +5 · Damage 1d8 cold · Trick: cold breath, CON save Tricky or lose a turn; blows out an unheld lantern each round.
**Cannot be killed.** Driven off at 0 HP in ch5. In ch6, ends when the first lantern is lit and held and Hoshi is not alone.
**How it ends:** walks into the light and is a straw hat on the step.

## Tengu (Mount Tsukimi, trial)
HP 25 · AC 15 · Attack +5 · Damage 1d8 · Trick: wind gust (DEX save Tricky or pushed 10 ft). Never actually fights the heroes; asks a riddle ("What follows you all day and vanishes at noon?" A shadow.)
**How it ends:** bows, gives a feather (advantage once in the finale).
