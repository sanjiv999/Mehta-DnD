# The Hollow Monk

Not a villain. Old Keeper Hoshi's loneliness, which gets up at night while he sleeps, puts on
his straw hat, and walks the road blowing out lanterns so that the spirits, lost, will come up
the mountain looking for light. So that someone will come. Hoshi does not know he does this.

**By chapter**
1. A shadow; flees from salt; drops a burned keeper's charm.
2. Stronger in the dark forest (12 HP); flees from the parade; drops a second charm.
3. Not present; a charm with a hole whispers nightmares to the dragon on a loop.
4. Learns: stops blowing, starts stealing; makes copies (6 HP each); fourth charm.
5. In person (35 HP, cannot be killed, driven off by light + parade + salt); knows Kiko; drops the charm with her name.
6. Climbs to blow out the first lantern. Ends when the lantern is lit, Kikyo says Hoshi's name, and a hero sits with him. Becomes an empty straw hat.

**Kikyo's name, letter by letter:** K (ch1), I (ch2), K (ch3), O (ch4), then the charm (ch5) completes it: KIKYO. Tails: 2 → 3 (ch5) → 4 (ch6).
