# Ready encounters by station

## Shiokaze
- **The cat's toll:** a fish or a bow. Animals Easy.
- **Masaru's question:** "What are you waving at?" Bluffing Easy or honesty (he frowns and lets it go).
- **Fisher's boat:** a ride to station two; a quiet lovely scene; Kame swims alongside.

## Bamboo Sea
- **Lost in the stalks:** a hero separated. Kiko's riddle leads them back ("I am tallest when I'm new" = a bamboo shoot).
- **The bird's song:** Nature Tricky to learn three notes; the forest holds still for a scene.
- **Sprite tag:** three sprites, towels... no, hats. They steal hats.

## Lake Kagami
- **Dream rooms:** see chapter 3.
- **Ice on the boat:** Yuki's boat freezes mid-lake; the dragon scales (if owned later) or a song frees it.

## Yuzuki Springs
- **Suspect interviews:** Gen's cousin Shin (saw a floating light), Kasa (saw the hat walk through a wall), Daigoro (was in the bath), the Wardens (arrested the wrong oni), the pilgrim (gone, left a hat and a charm).
- **Bath monkeys:** towel heist.

## Castle Shirogane
- **Lion-dance infiltration:** Performance Tricky; kids under the lion costume.
- **The baby:** sees spirits; a kid's job to keep him from crawling after Kiko.

## Mount Tsukimi
