# Secrets and answers

- **Who is blowing out the lanterns?** Old Keeper Hoshi's loneliness, as the Hollow Monk, without his knowledge.
- **Why?** So the lost spirits will climb the mountain looking for light, and someone will come.
- **Who is Kiko?** Kikyo, Hoshi's shrine fox, who went down to light the road and forgot the way home. Saying her name restores her, a tail at a time.
- **The charms:** Hoshi's own keeper's charms, burned through at the seal because the Monk is not the keeper, only his shadow.
- **The dragon's nightmare:** a charm humming "sleep, winter, sleep" on a loop; a recording of Hoshi talking in his sleep.
- **Daigoro's apology:** his grandfather ate Hoshi's kettle. The apology makes Hoshi laugh, which makes him solid.
- **The paper shade:** inside the first lantern once it is lit. Painted with a fox and a mountain, by Kikyo, long ago.
- **Door:** the torii in the sea at Shiokaze.
