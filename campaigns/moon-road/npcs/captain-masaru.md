# Captain Masaru

**Looks:** Lacquered armour kept too clean for this road. Adjusts his helmet strap when he is confused, which is most of this story.
**Sounds like:** "What are you waving at." Not a question. Fair, and would rather be fair than right.
**Wants:** Order on a road nobody uses, and to see a spirit again. He saw one as a boy and was told he was lying.
**Afraid of:** Looking foolish in front of the men he brought with him.
**Offers:** The checkpoint, the castle, arrests (sometimes of the wrong oni), and a surprising amount of help once he decides you are not spies.
**Thinks:** The lantern-lighters are mad or spies. He is hoping for spies.

## Secret
He was sent here after arguing with a magistrate about a fine levied on a boatwoman. He was right. He will see a spirit in chapter 5 and sit down on the ground.
