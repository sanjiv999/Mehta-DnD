# Kiko

**Looks:** Small, russet, two tails, and sits as if someone is grading her posture. Her left ear has a notch.
**Sounds like:** Says "you're late" as a greeting. Speaks in a riddle only when it matters. Sighs when the tanuki are mentioned.
**Wants:** Her name back, and does not know that is what she wants. She thinks she wants the lanterns lit.
**Afraid of:** Hot water. She will not explain.
**Offers:** The road, one riddle-answer per session, and a pointed opinion about everyone.
**Thinks:** The tanuki are vulgar. She would never say she envies the parade.

## Secret
Kikyo, the mountain shrine's fox. She went down the road to light the lanterns the villages had stopped lighting and stayed away too long for a fox, and forgot. One letter of her name returns at each station; a tail at the fifth and the sixth. The Hollow Monk knows her.
