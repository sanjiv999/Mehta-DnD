# Kiko

**Looks:** Small, russet, two tails (more later), eyes too clever.
**Sounds like:** Riddles when it matters, sighs when it doesn't, calls everyone "late".
**Wants:** To remember her name.
**Offers:** Knowledge of the road, one riddle-answer per session, a shoulder-sized friend.

## Secret
She was the mountain shrine's fox, Kikyo. She went down the road to light the lanterns and forgot the way home. The Hollow Monk knows her.
