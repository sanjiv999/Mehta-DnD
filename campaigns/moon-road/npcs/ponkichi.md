# Boss Ponkichi

**Looks:** Round, a leaf on his head that he adjusts before he transforms, a sash that was red once. Sits on a drum. Everyone in Pon-Pon sits on a drum.
**Sounds like:** "Fifty years!" Drums his belly with both hands when he is thinking and does not know he is doing it.
**Wants:** An invitation to the Shogun's autumn festival at the castle. Written. On paper. He has never been invited to anything.
**Afraid of:** Being laughed at by the fox.
**Offers:** The parade, which is the only thing that keeps the forest's paths still; a small drum that summons one tanuki for a scene; a hundred friends who turn into teapots.
**Thinks:** The fox is stuck up. He is not entirely wrong.

## Secret
He would do anything for a child who hands him a hand-written card with his name on it. Anything.
