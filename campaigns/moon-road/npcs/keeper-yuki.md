# Keeper Yuki

**Looks:** A padded coat, a broom, a boat that does not need sweeping. She sweeps it anyway.
**Sounds like:** Short. Kind without saying so. "You're the lantern-lighters. Good. Tea."
**Wants:** The snow to stop in autumn. She has tried everything except going into the dream.
**Offers:** The boat, a tea that puts you to sleep on purpose, and a bed on the floor.

## Secret
Her grandmother was Hoshi's wife's sister. She has a letter from the mountain she has never opened because the handwriting is shaking.
