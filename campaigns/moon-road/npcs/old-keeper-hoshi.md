# Old Keeper Hoshi

**Looks:** So old he is see-through at the edges. A broom. A kettle.
**Sounds like:** Wind in a dry field. Gentler when he remembers things.
**Wants:** For someone to come.
**Offers:** Tea, the first lantern, the paper shade.

## Secret
The Hollow Monk is his loneliness, walking at night while he sleeps. He does not know. Kikyo was his fox.
