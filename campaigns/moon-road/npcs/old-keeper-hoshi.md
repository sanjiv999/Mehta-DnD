# Old Keeper Hoshi

**Looks:** So old the moonlight goes partway through him. A broom across his knees. A kettle at his side that he puts on for visitors who do not come. A straw hat in his lap that he does not remember buying.
**Sounds like:** Wind in dry grass. Gentler when he is remembering.
**Wants:** For someone to come up the road. He has lit the first lantern every night for seventy years and the villages stopped sending the autumn rice when the highway opened.
**Offers:** Tea, the first lantern, and inside it, when it is lit, the paper shade.

## Secret
The Hollow Monk is his loneliness, which gets up while he sleeps, puts on the hat, and walks down the road blowing out lanterns so that lost spirits will climb the mountain looking for light. He does not know. Kikyo was his fox. The oni's apology makes him laugh, and laughing makes him solid.
