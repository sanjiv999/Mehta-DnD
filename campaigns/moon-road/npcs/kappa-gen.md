# Kappa Gen

**Looks:** Green, a beak, webbed hands, a dish of water on his head he carries like a full cup. Young for a kappa, which still makes him older than the village.
**Sounds like:** Apologises halfway through the first sentence. Bows first, so that nobody can spill his dish before he has explained.
**Wants:** To be useful. He has never been thanked for anything.
**Afraid of:** The cat on the pier.
**Offers:** News from the water, the flame he took, and the name of his cousin Shin, who fixes pipes at the hot springs.

## Secret
He took the pier lantern's flame in a leaf to keep Old Kame warm. He will give it back the moment someone bows. Parents here write children's names on cucumbers and throw them in the river; he keeps every one and knows every child in the village.
