# Kappa Gen

**Looks:** Green, beaky, a dish of water on his head he must never spill.
**Sounds like:** Sniffly, earnest, apologises before he finishes a sentence.
**Wants:** To help, and cucumbers.
**Offers:** News from the water; a plumber's skills in chapter 4 (his cousin Shin).

## Secret
Bowing to him empties his dish and makes him honest. He knows this and bows first to be safe.
