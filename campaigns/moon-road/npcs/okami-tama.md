# Okami Tama

**Looks:** Plump, aproned, leaf always slipping.
**Sounds like:** Bows mid-sentence. Feeds you. Then gossips.
**Wants:** Her guests comfortable and her oni freed.
**Offers:** Rooms, rice cakes, six witnesses, the whole village's gossip.

## Secret
She is the one who sent for the lantern-lighters, via Kame, before anyone else believed the lanterns mattered.
