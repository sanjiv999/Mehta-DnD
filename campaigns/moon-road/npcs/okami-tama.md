# Okami Tama

**Looks:** An indigo apron, a tray always level, a leaf that falls off when she bows, which is constantly.
**Sounds like:** Bows mid-sentence. Feeds you. Tells you who was in the bath and when.
**Wants:** Her guests warm and her oni out of custody. She has six witnesses and a list.
**Offers:** A room, rice cakes, the gossip of a whole village of inns, and the fact that she sent for the lantern-lighters by turtle before anyone else believed in them.

## Secret
She knows Daigoro's grandfather's story and finds it very funny. She has not told Daigoro that Hoshi forgot it years ago, because the walk is doing him good.
