# Daigoro

**Looks:** Six feet of red muscle, small horns, a bathrobe, an expression of permanent apology.
**Sounds like:** "Excuse me. Pardon me. If I may." Bows seventeen times.
**Wants:** To apologise to Old Keeper Hoshi for something his grandfather did.
**Offers:** Nothing blows out a lantern an oni is holding. He carries things. He is a wall.

## Secret
His grandfather ate Hoshi's kettle a hundred years ago. Hoshi has forgotten; the apology makes him laugh, and laughing makes him solid again.
