# Daigoro

**Looks:** Six feet of red, small horns, a cotton bathrobe that is too short, feet that are too big for the inn's slippers.
**Sounds like:** "Excuse me. Pardon me. If I may." Counts his bows. Seventeen is his record.
**Wants:** To apologise to Old Keeper Hoshi for what his grandfather did, a hundred years ago, and be told it is all right.
**Afraid of:** Being told it is not.
**Offers:** A wall. Carrying things. The one rule the road cares about: nothing an oni is holding can be blown out.

## Secret
His grandfather ate Hoshi's kettle. Hoshi has forgotten. The apology makes Hoshi laugh for the first time in decades, and laughing makes him solid.
