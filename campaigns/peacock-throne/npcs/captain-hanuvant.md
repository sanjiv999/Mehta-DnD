# Captain Hanuvant

**Looks:** A large grey langur with a red sash, a tiny brass telescope and a general's posture.
**Sounds like:** Formal, offended easily, secretly delighted by children who climb.
**Wants:** Respect for the Guild. Also mangoes.
**Offers:** Fast travel across the city, rooftop intelligence, a flying leap when you need one.

## Secret
He is innocent of the lamp thefts and knows exactly who did it but has been told nobody will believe a monkey.
