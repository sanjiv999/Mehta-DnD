# Captain Hanuvant

**Looks:** A grey langur, bigger than the others, red sash, a brass telescope he holds like a baton because he has never got it to focus.
**Sounds like:** Formal. Takes offence quickly and forgives in exchange for fruit. Stands like a general and then scratches like a monkey, and knows it.
**Wants:** The right for the guild to sit on the top floor of the Panch Mahal at noon, where the breeze is. He has petitioned eleven times. The astronomer is up there.
**Afraid of:** That nobody will ever take a monkey's word, which is the one thing a postal service needs.
**Offers:** A letter across the city faster than a horse, rooftop intelligence, and in chapter 5 a way out through the roof.
**Thinks:** Bibi Chandni is "a civilian."

## Secret
He saw the clockwork peacock take the lamps and knows exactly who built it, because the guild carried the Vizier's parcels for a year. He has told no one, because he has told people things before.
