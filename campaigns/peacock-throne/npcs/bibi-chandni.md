# Bibi Chandni

**Looks:** Round, bright, silver bangles, a tray of jalebi and laddoo that is never empty.
**Sounds like:** Grandmotherly, feeds you before she tells you anything, then tells you everything.
**Wants:** Good gossip and good children.
**Offers:** Information in exchange for favours, and free sweets for kids who say please.

## Secret
Runs the Vanara Guild's mango supply and so knows everything they see from the rooftops.
