# Bibi Chandni

**Looks:** Stout, silver bangles that ring when she works, a pan of oil in front of her and a pot of syrup beside it. Drops jalebi coils in without looking. Keeps a ledger of who owes her.
**Sounds like:** Feeds you first. Asks after your mother. Then tells you what you came for and two things you did not.
**Wants:** Customers who say please, and one day off in thirty-one years. She has never taken one.
**Afraid of:** The lake. She has watched the waterline on the ghats for three decades.
**Offers:** Everything the water-carriers hear in every house in Sikri, for a favour recorded in the ledger.
**Thinks:** The Vanara Guild is overpaid and underwashed.

## Secret
She knew the parrots had gone quiet two days before the court did, because the water-carrier for the palace gardens told her. She knows which day each gem went grey. She has not been asked.
