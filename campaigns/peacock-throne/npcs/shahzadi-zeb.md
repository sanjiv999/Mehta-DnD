# Shahzadi Zeb

**Looks:** A servant's cotton shawl over silk she forgot to change out of. Ink to the second knuckle on her right hand. A ledger under her arm that is the elephant register, which she is copying because she believes the old one is wrong about Badal's age.
**Sounds like:** Gives instructions before she says hello. Says "obviously" when she is unsure.
**Wants this week:** Her brother's elephant found before the sunset parade, quietly.
**Afraid of:** Being "settled" somewhere quiet. It is what happens to princesses who are noticed.
**Offers:** A way into the palace, a royal seal she "borrowed", and the plain truth, once she trusts you.
**Thinks:** The astronomer is a fraud. (He is not.)

## Secret
She unlatched the stable gate because Badal asked her to, in the way he asks, and she could hear it. She has been able to hear animals since she was small and has told no one. She knows the Vizier's mirror is wrong but has nothing she could say to the court.
