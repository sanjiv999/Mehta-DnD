# Shahzadi Zeb

**Looks:** Fourteen, ink on her fingers, a shawl she uses as a disguise that fools nobody but guards.
**Sounds like:** Fast, precise, slightly bossy, warm underneath. Says "obviously" a lot.
**Wants:** To protect her little brother and to be taken seriously by the court.
**Offers:** Palace access, maps, the occasional royal seal "borrowed" from her brother.

## Secret
She has noticed the Vizier's mirror and suspects him but has no proof. She is also the one who
left the stable gate unlatched, because Badal asked her to (she can hear animals, a gift she hides).
