# Hakim Fariduddin

**Looks:** Tall even bent, a dark robe, a lens cloth he has lost and so polishes lenses on his sleeve. His astrolabes were built where they stand because they would not fit up the stairs.
**Sounds like:** Answers a question with a better question. Takes a long time. Is always right at the end of it, which Zeb finds unbearable.
**Wants:** A natural law that explains why the gems grey. He has measured the lake every month for forty years and the numbers are in a book he has not shown anyone.
**Afraid of:** That the law, when he finds it, will have his own name in it.
**Offers:** Healing between sessions, lore with advantage, and an astrolabe that points at the nearest lie (it was built for finding the direction of prayer; the lie-finding was an accident).
**Thinks:** The Vanara Guild should have the top floor. He would say so if anyone asked.

## Secret
He taught Qamar optics when Qamar was fourteen, brought him to court, and keeps Qamar's first mirror in a locked drawer: a hand mirror that shows things slightly too true. It will crack the big one.
