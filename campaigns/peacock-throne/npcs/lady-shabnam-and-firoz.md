# Lady Shabnam and Firoz

**Looks:** Shabnam is made of blue evening mist and wears the stars. Firoz is a small whirl of
orange smoke with big eyes who turns things colours by accident.
**Sounds like:** Shabnam speaks in echoes. Firoz speaks in hiccups and giggles.
**Wants:** Their well's water back to its true colour.
**Offers:** Truthful riddle-answers, water-breathing (chapter 4), and Firoz, who wants to come along.

## Secret
Firoz can follow the party as a companion from chapter 2 if a kid hero asks. He has 5 HP and one
trick: turn one thing a colour. Shabnam is the Door-keeper for this world.
