# Lady Shabnam and Firoz

**Looks:** Shabnam is the cold air that comes up from deep water, given a shape; she is clearer in the morning. Firoz is a small orange whirl of smoke with eyes, five years old in every way that matters, who turns the nearest thing a colour when he hiccups.
**Sounds like:** She speaks in echoes, because that is what a well does. He speaks in hiccups and questions.
**Wants:** The lake kept. That is the whole bargain and the whole of her.
**Offers:** One true answer each, in a riddle. Water-breathing when it is needed. Firoz, who wants to come along and will.

## Secret
She made the bargain with the first Padishah: the thousand gems for the promise that the well would never run dry. The dry lowest step is the Door to other worlds and opens when the wick is recovered. Firoz is a companion from chapter 2 with 5 HP and one trick: turn one thing a colour.
