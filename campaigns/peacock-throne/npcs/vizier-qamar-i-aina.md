# Vizier Qamar-i-Aina

**Looks:** Silver robes, a small hand mirror on a chain, a smile that is exactly symmetrical.
**Sounds like:** Smooth, helpful, uses the heroes' names a lot. Never raises his voice.
**Wants:** (Publicly) to help the heroes. (Really) see dm/villains.md.
**Offers:** Guidance that is always slightly wrong.

## Secret
The villain. He is stealing the throne's colours into his mirror. He is not a monster: he was a
poor boy who believed colour was the only thing that made people important, and he can be
defeated by heroes who show him that is not true. He is redeemable in the finale.
