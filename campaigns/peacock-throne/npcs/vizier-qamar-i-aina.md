# Vizier Qamar-i-Aina

**Looks:** Grey silk, which is unusual at a court where everyone wears colour. A small round mirror on a chain, turned to face away from him. He keeps it that way with his thumb.
**Sounds like:** Remembers what you said last week and quotes it back to you kindly. Never raises his voice. Uses your name.
**Wants:** To help. He says so, and in his way he means it.
**Afraid of:** The south wall of the city, below which the dyers work. He has not been there in thirty years.
**Offers:** Guidance that is always slightly wrong, and a mirror "to catch a thief's reflection" that leads you the wrong way.
**Thinks:** Hakim Fariduddin is the only person at court who ever looked at him properly, and he cannot forgive it.

## Secret
Born Qamar, a dyer's son from the indigo quarter. His father's arms were blue to the elbow for life, and the one time his father carried cloth to the palace no one would take it from his hands. Qamar decided at nine that colour was a wall between the people who wore it and the people who were stained by it. His mirror takes colour because he built it to. He wants a throne with no colour so that a dyer's son can sit on it. He is not wrong that the wall is there. The heroes beat him by showing him something colour is not. He is redeemable, and his brother is the key.
