# Rukmini

**Looks:** Bare feet that grip the deck, a red scarf against the sun, a flat boat with eyes painted on the prow so it can see the rocks.
**Sounds like:** Sings badly while she rows. Talks to the river like an aunt she is fond of and slightly afraid of.
**Wants:** The waterline back where it was when she was a girl. She has scratched the old mark into the ghat steps.
**Offers:** A boat, a dolphin called Chhaya who follows it, and the way upriver when the river turns.

## Secret
She felt the river change direction a day before anyone saw it. She did not say, because the last time she said something about the water a magistrate fined her.
