# Rukmini

**Looks:** Strong arms, a red scarf, bare feet that grip the deck.
**Sounds like:** Sings while she rows; talks to the river like it is her aunt.
**Wants:** The river happy.
**Offers:** A boat, a dolphin named Chhaya, the way upriver.

## Secret
She can hear the river's moods and knows it is being pulled home before anyone else does.
