# Dhoomketu

**Looks:** Tiger's face, dancer's hands, silks in every colour, a paintbrush he waves like a sceptre.
**Sounds like:** Theatrical, pauses for applause, genuinely delighted by honest praise.
**Wants:** To be seen. Really seen.
**Offers:** Counter-illusions, the jars of colour, dramatic entrances.
