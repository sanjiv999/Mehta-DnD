# Dhoomketu

**Looks:** A tiger's face and a dancer's hands, silks in every colour at once, a paintbrush he holds like a sceptre. Four hundred portraits of himself hang in the fort and he is not happy with any of them.
**Sounds like:** Pauses for applause. Asks, every few minutes, whether you thought that was magnificent. Can tell a lie from a compliment instantly and is hurt by both.
**Wants:** To be looked at by someone who then stays.
**Offers:** Counter-illusions, three jars of colour, and in chapter 5 an entrance.

## Secret
The Vizier came to the fort once, twenty years ago, said "magnificent", left the jars, and never came back. Dhoomketu has kept them for that one word. Three sincere compliments and one honest criticism turn him; the criticism matters more than the compliments.
