# Daulat

**Looks:** Forearms blue to the elbow, as every dyer's are. One hand grey from a mordant burn years ago. A stall of hand mirrors, all the same, none sold.
**Sounds like:** Says little. Looks at your hands when you talk, out of habit.
**Wants:** His brother to come down to the vats once.
**Offers:** A mirror that shows the truth for one question and then cracks. In chapter 5, a child's drawing of a blue peacock that his brother made at eight.

## Secret
The Vizier's older brother. Qamar sends him money by having him sell mirrors no one buys, so that they never have to meet. Treated with ordinary kindness before chapter 5, Daulat brings the drawing to the throne room, and it gives the heroes advantage on every "true colours" roll.
