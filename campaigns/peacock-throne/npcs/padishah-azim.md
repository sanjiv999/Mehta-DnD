# Padishah Azim

**Looks:** The turban is his father's and sits on his ears. He sits sideways on the throne with one foot swinging when the court is not looking, and most of the court is always looking.
**Sounds like:** Asks permission. Asks a hundred questions. Calls the heroes "my heroes" within a minute of meeting them.
**Wants:** Someone his own age to play pachisi with on the courtyard board. For Badal to be happy.
**Afraid of:** That people only pretend to like him. He is right about some of them.
**Offers:** Anything that is his, which is nearly everything, which is why Zeb worries.
**Thinks:** The Vizier is the kindest man at court, because the Vizier remembers what he said last week.

## Secret
The throne is bound to its Padishah. As the gems grey he goes quiet. By chapter 4 he cannot laugh, and the court pretends not to notice. Restoring the gems restores him.
