# Padishah Azim

**Looks:** Eleven, enormous turban, enormous grin, sits sideways on the throne when no one is looking.
**Sounds like:** Enthusiastic, asks a hundred questions, calls the heroes "my heroes" immediately.
**Wants:** Friends his own age (the kid heroes), and for Badal to be happy.
**Offers:** Anything that is his to give, which is nearly everything and is why Zeb worries.

## Secret
The throne is bound to him; as it greys, he gets quieter. By chapter 4 he can barely laugh. Restoring it restores him.
