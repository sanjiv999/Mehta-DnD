# The Peacock Throne

## The pitch (read this to the players)
Sikri is the most beautiful city in the world. Its walls are red sandstone, its palaces are
white marble, and at its heart sits the Peacock Throne: a seat of gold set with a thousand gems
that glow like a peacock's tail. The young Padishah Azim rules from it, with his sister Shahzadi
Zeb at his side, who is cleverer than every vizier put together.

But this year something is wrong. The gems on the throne are losing their colour, one each
night. The Padishah's white elephant has wandered off. The parrots in the palace gardens have
stopped talking. And the stepwell below the city, where the djinn live, has begun to whisper
your names.

You are four heroes who have come to Sikri for the Nauroz festival. By sunset you will be in
the middle of all of it.

## The world in five facts
1. **Magic is everywhere and polite.** Djinn live in stepwells, nagas rule the rivers, yakshas guard
   treasure, and a well-mannered hero can talk to any of them.
2. **The bazaar knows everything.** Bibi Chandni's sweet stall is the real intelligence service.
3. **Animals matter.** The court keeps cheetahs for hunting, elephants for parades, and a parrot
   named Mirza who remembers every secret ever spoken in the palace.
4. **The Vanara Guild carries the mail.** Monkey messengers run the rooftops. They can be hired,
   bribed with mangoes, or offended, which is worse.
5. **Colour is power.** In this world, a thing's colour is its life. Grey means something has been taken.

## What heroes do here
Solve the mystery of the greying throne across five chapters, from the festival grounds to a
desert fort made of mirrors, a river that flows uphill during the monsoon, and finally the
throne room itself.

## Factions
| Faction | What they want | Face |
|---|---|---|
| The Court | Keep the empire calm and the festival perfect | Shahzadi Zeb |
| The Bazaar | Business as usual, and good gossip | Bibi Chandni |
| The Vanara Guild | Respect, mangoes, and rooftop rights | Captain Hanuvant |
| The Naga of the Deep Baoli | Their stolen rain | Rani Padma |
| The Observatory | Knowledge, calmly | Hakim Fariduddin |

## Hooks for each hero
- A **kid hero** is found by Mirza the parrot, who lands on their shoulder and refuses to leave.
- A **warrior or protector** is asked by a stable boy to help find the elephant quietly.
- A **clever or magical hero** notices that their own keepsake has gone slightly grey at one edge.
- A **charming hero** has already been given a sweet by Bibi Chandni "on credit, for a favour later".
