# Villains and the plan

## Vizier Qamar-i-Aina, the Vizier of Mirrors
Born poor in the grey quarter of Sikri. Taught by Hakim Fariduddin. Decided young that colour
is what makes people matter. Built a mirror that drinks colour. Has spent twenty years at
court waiting for a child on the throne.

**Plan by chapter**
1. Steal one gem's colour per night. Keep smiling. Notice the heroes.
2. Use the Clockwork Peacock to harvest lamp-light; frame the Vanara Guild.
3. Store colours at the Mirror Fort with Dhoomketu. Heroes get the first jar back.
4. Trade the sky's colour to Rani Padma for her rain, making the monsoon fail in the capital.
5. With the last gem grey, hold the mirror up to the throne and claim it. Defeated when each
   hero shows him a true colour (a value: courage, kindness, honesty, joy) and the mirror cracks.

**Redemption:** If at least two heroes choose mercy, he becomes the Keeper of the Baoli's Door,
grey-robed, helping heroes cross between worlds. Mirza forgives him last.

## Dhoomketu the Rakshasa (chapter 3)
Vain, theatrical, lonely. Keeps the jars because the Vizier called him "magnificent". Turns ally after three sincere compliments and one honest criticism.

## The Clockwork Peacock (chapters 2 and 5)
A machine. Not evil. Wants to dance. In chapter 5 it can be re-wound to dance for the heroes if someone kept its key from chapter 2.
