# Secrets and answers

- **Why is the throne greying?** The Vizier's mirror drinks one gem's colour each midnight while the court sleeps.
- **Why did Badal leave?** He heard Firoz crying from the baoli, and Zeb let him out.
- **Why did the parrots stop talking?** Mirza's voice is in the mirror. The others are loyal and went silent in solidarity.
- **The well's riddle answers** (prepare one per hero at session time; keep them true): e.g. "What is wrong with the throne?" → "A mirror is drinking what a peacock was given."
- **Lantern wick:** sits inside the throne's central peacock feather, visible only when all gems are restored.
- **Door:** the dry step in the baoli; Lady Shabnam opens it.
