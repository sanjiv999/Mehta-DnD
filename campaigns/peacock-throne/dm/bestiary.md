# Bestiary (kid-safe)

Every entry: HP, AC, attack, damage, trick, and **How it ends**.

## Mirror-Shard (chapter 1)
HP 7 · AC 12 · Attack +3 · Damage 1d4 (a cold sting) · Trick: copies the last action a hero took.
**Weakness:** showing it something colourful (any hero describes a colourful thing, Performance Easy) deals 1d8.
**How it ends:** shatters into coloured sand that Firoz scoops up.

## Bazaar Monkey Gang (chapter 1 to 2)
HP 4 each (groups of 3) · AC 13 · Attack +2 · Damage 1 (a thrown banana, mostly embarrassing) · Trick: steal one small item.
**How it ends:** they return the item for a mango or a good joke.

## Clockwork Peacock (chapter 2 boss, chapter 5 ally)
HP 25 · AC 15 · Attack +4 · Damage 1d6 (tail-feather dart) · Trick: fans its tail to dazzle (WIS save Tricky or lose a turn).
**Weakness:** the winding key on its back. Acrobatics Hard to reach it; one turn of winding stops it.
**How it ends:** winds down, bows, and asks for a dance partner.

## Sand Djinn-let (desert travel, chapter 3)
HP 10 · AC 13 · Attack +3 · Damage 1d6 (sandblast) · Trick: becomes a dust-devil and moves 60 ft.
**How it ends:** settles into a sandcastle shaped like whoever beat it.

## Reflection (chapter 3)
A grey copy of one hero with the same stats but 5 HP. Trick: knows the hero's powers.
**How it ends:** the hero says one true thing about themselves and the reflection nods and steps back into the glass.

## Dhoomketu, Rakshasa Illusionist (chapter 3 boss)
HP 40 · AC 14 · Attack +5 · Damage 1d8 (illusory tiger) · Trick: three false images; only the one with a shadow is real.
**How it ends:** takes a bow and asks if the heroes thought he was magnificent.

## Makara (chapter 4 rapids)
HP 30 · AC 15 · Attack +5 · Damage 1d10 (bite, mostly of the boat) · Trick: capsizes.
**How it ends:** it was guarding its eggs; show them you mean no harm (Animals Tricky) and it ferries you.

## Mirror-Maze Guardian (chapter 5)
HP 20 · AC 16 · Attack +5 · Damage 1d8 · Trick: can only be hit by a hero looking away from it (disadvantage unless using a reflection).
**How it ends:** it is Mirza's missing feather, grown huge. Returned to him, it shrinks.
