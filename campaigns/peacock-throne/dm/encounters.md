# Ready encounters by location

## Festival grounds
- **Kite tangle:** Acrobatics Easy to free a kid; the kite-maker gives a small kite (+10 ft jump once per session).
- **Cheetah nap:** a royal cheetah is asleep on the only path. Animals Tricky to move it, or Sneaking Tricky to step over.

## Great Bazaar
- **Banana dispute:** see chapter 1. Persuasion Easy; truth-telling resolves it automatically.
- **Monkey toll:** three bazaar monkeys demand a toll. Bribe, joke, or climb.
- **The grey mirror-seller:** optional, plants the chapter 5 ally.

## Ghats
- **Lamp race:** d20 contest to float a lamp furthest.
- **Dolphin message:** a river dolphin delivers a riddle from Rani Padma (chapter 4 foreshadowing).

## Baoli of Whispers
- **Whisper test:** each hero hears a whisper of their fear (from character.yaml). WIS save Easy; failure means disadvantage on the next roll only.
- **Mirror-Shards:** one per hero present.

## Desert road (chapter 3)
Roll d6 per day: 1 sandstorm (CON save Tricky or lose a day), 2 caravan of friendly traders, 3 sand djinn-let, 4 oasis with a yaksha riddle, 5 mirage of home (WIS Easy), 6 clear skies and stars (Hakim's astrolabe reveals a secret).
