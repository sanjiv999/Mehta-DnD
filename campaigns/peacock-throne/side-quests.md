# Side quests: The Peacock Throne

Short, optional, 20 to 40 minutes each. Drop them in when a session needs a change of pace or a
kid needs a win. Each gives 50 XP and one small reward.

## The grey mirror-seller (any time after chapter 1)
A stall in the Great Bazaar sells the Vizier's mirrors. Nobody buys. The seller, **Daulat**, has one
grey hand. He is the Vizier's brother. A kind word, a purchase, or a shared sweet earns his trust.
In chapter 5 he brings the party the Vizier's childhood drawing, which gives automatic advantage
on the "true colours" rolls. **Reward:** a small mirror that shows the truth for one question.

## Ustad Patang's lost kite (chapter 2)
The kite-maker's masterwork, a kite shaped like a peacock, blew onto the palace roof. Rooftop
climb (Acrobatics Tricky) with the Vanara Guild. **Reward:** the Big Kite that can carry a kid hero.

## The yaksha's lost coin (chapter 3, oasis road)
A plump yaksha guarding the oasis has lost his lucky coin in the pool. Swim for it (Athletics Easy)
or ask the fish politely (Animals Easy). **Reward:** the Lucky Coin: once per campaign, reroll a Whoops.

## Gulnar's eleventh camel (chapter 3)
The eleventh camel is a prince under a curse and is very polite about it. He can be freed by
being named correctly; the name is on his saddle, backwards. **Reward:** Prince Kamil owes the
party a favour and shows up in chapter 5 with a hundred camels.

## Nagini Tara's first day above water (after chapter 4)
Padma's daughter has never seen the sky. Show her Sikri: three places (kids choose). She is
delighted by everything and terrified of pigeons. **Reward:** Naga +1 and Tara as a friend who
can be summoned once for a scene by pouring water on the ground.

## The farmers' feast (after chapter 4)
The villages whose fields were saved throw a feast. Each hero gets a toast. Keshu names the
dish. **Reward:** Court +1, Bazaar +1, and a jar of pickle that heals 1d6 (one use).

## Mirza's lost feather (chapter 5, if skipped in the maze)
If the party never returned Mirza's feather, he asks for it afterward. A gentle scene.

## The Padishah's secret (any time)
Azim wants to sneak out and see the bazaar like a normal kid. Zeb forbids it. Take him anyway
(Sneaking Tricky) and give him the best afternoon of his life. **Reward:** Court +2 and a royal
pardon for one future mistake.
