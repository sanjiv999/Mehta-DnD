# Chapter 3: The Mirror Fort
A desert fort built entirely of mirrors, where the **Rakshasa Illusionist Dhoomketu** keeps the
stolen colours in jars for the Vizier. Travel by camel caravan (random desert encounters), a
puzzle of mirrors where each kid hero's reflection has a job, and the first real boss. Dhoomketu
is not evil, just vain, and can be talked into switching sides if complimented sincerely three
times. Reward: the first jar of colour, which restores one gem when poured on the throne.
