# Chapter 3: The Mirror Fort

## Premise
Three days west by camel is a fort that cannot be looked at directly at noon. The rakshasa
illusionist **Dhoomketu** guards jars of stolen colour there because the Vizier once called him
"magnificent". The fort has no door: you enter by stepping into your own reflection at sunset.
This chapter is a journey, a puzzle, and a boss who can be won over.

## Goal the party will understand
"Reach the Mirror Fort, get the colours back, and find out who is really in charge."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The caravan (exploration, travel)
> Gulnar the camel-driver has eleven camels, each with a name and an opinion. "Three days," she
> says, "if the wind is kind. Four if it is not. Pick a camel. The camel also picks you."
> The desert begins where the last mango tree ends.
- **Setup:** Three days of travel. Each day roll on the desert table (dm/encounters.md). Each hero picks a camel; the camel's name is a kid's job.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Name your camel (canon). Each morning Keisha rolls the weather die. Arya reads Hakim's astrolabe at night (it points at the nearest lie: on night two it points at the fort).
- **Choices:**
  - 🐪 Take the fast road (two days, two encounters, rough)
  - 🌴 Take the oasis road (three days, one encounter, meet the yaksha)
  - 🌙 Travel by night (cool, stars, one strange encounter)
- **Rolls:** CON save Tricky in a sandstorm. Animals Easy to calm a camel. Survival-style checks are Nature.
- **Outcomes:** The oasis yaksha gives a riddle whose answer is "shadow" (useful in scene 3). A caravan of traders mentions that the fort "sings at noon".
- **Image:** A caravan of eleven camels crossing golden dunes under a huge sky, children riding and pointing at a distant glint
- **Music:** oud and wind

### Scene 2: The fort that is not there (exploration, puzzle)
> At noon the fort is a blaze you cannot look at. At sunset it is a palace of glass on a dune,
> and every wall shows you yourself. There is no gate. There is no door. Gulnar shrugs. "Nobody
> goes in. The ones who try just... walk into the wall and stand there feeling silly."
> Your reflection on the north wall raises a hand. You did not.
- **Setup:** Entry puzzle. Each hero must step into their own reflection, and the reflection only lets you in if you say one true thing about yourself.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Say one true thing ("I am brave", "I love my sister"). Cannot fail. The reflection smiles and steps aside.
- **Choices:**
  - 🪞 Step into your reflection
  - 🔨 Try to break the glass (it shows you crying; it is sad, not dangerous)
  - 🐪 Send a camel first (the camel's reflection says "no")
- **Outcomes:** Inside, a maze of mirrors. Three hallways; only the one where your reflection has a shadow is real (yaksha riddle). Wrong turns meet a **Reflection** (dm/bestiary.md), which steps aside when the hero says one true thing.
- **Image:** Four heroes facing their own reflections in a mirrored wall at sunset, one reflection waving
- **Music:** glass chimes

### Scene 3: The colour vault (exploration)
> The hall at the centre of the fort is the only room with colour. Hundreds of glass jars on
> shelves, each glowing: the red of a ruby, the green of a parrot feather, the exact blue of the
> Baoli's water. In the middle, on a cushion, a creature with a tiger's face and a dancer's
> hands is painting its own portrait. "Visitors," says Dhoomketu, delighted. "Tell me honestly.
> Am I magnificent?"
- **Setup:** Dhoomketu is vain, lonely, theatrical. He can be fought (HP 40) or won over with three sincere compliments and one honest criticism. Fighting works but he keeps the jars behind an illusion; talking wins the jars.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Each kid gives Dhoomketu one real compliment (he can tell if it is fake). Keisha picks which jar she thinks is the Baoli's blue (she is right).
- **Choices:**
  - 🎭 Compliment him sincerely
  - 🗡️ Fight him
  - 🤥 Flatter him falsely (he grows three more heads, each more offended)
  - 🖼️ Critique his painting honestly
- **Rolls:** Persuasion Tricky per compliment, auto-success if the player says something specific and true. Fight: three illusions, only the one with a shadow is real.
- **Outcomes:** Won over, Dhoomketu confesses the Vizier never came back, and gives the party **three jars** (one restores a gem). He asks to come to Sikri "for an audience". He is now an ally.
- **Image:** A tiger-faced rakshasa in silks painting his own portrait in a hall of hundreds of glowing colour jars
- **Music:** theatrical sitar flourish

### Scene 4: The mirror speaks (action, escape)
> The moment the first jar leaves its shelf, every mirror in the fort turns black. And then, in
> all of them at once, a face: the Vizier, smiling exactly symmetrically. "Friends of the Throne.
> So far from home." The black mirrors begin to crack, and from each crack crawls a shard of your
> own reflection.
- **Setup:** Escape sequence. Mirror-Shards pour in (one per hero per round for three rounds). The walls are collapsing into sand. Dhoomketu knows the back way: a reflection of the Baoli.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha carries the blue jar and must not drop it (she will not). Arya counts the rounds out loud; on three, the way opens.
- **Choices:**
  - 🏃 Run with Dhoomketu through the back mirror
  - 🛡️ Hold the hall while the jars are gathered
  - 🌈 Open a jar to blast the shards with colour (works: 2d8 to all shards, but loses that jar)
- **Rolls:** Dexterity saves Tricky for falling glass. Attacks as normal.
- **Outcomes:** The party tumbles out of the Baoli of Whispers in Sikri, three days away, in one step. Lady Shabnam is waiting. The water is a little bluer when the jar is opened over it. Firoz asks to come along.
- **Image:** Heroes running through a collapsing hall of black cracking mirrors, each carrying a glowing jar, a tiger-faced rakshasa leading the way
- **Music:** fast drums, breaking glass, then sudden silence and water

## Complications (roll d6)
1. Sandstorm buries the camp; dig out the camels (Athletics Easy, everyone).
2. A sand djinn-let steals a kid's shoe. Chase it; it settles as a sandcastle shaped like the kid.
3. A mirage of home: WIS Easy or lose an hour arguing with a mirage parent.
4. Gulnar's lead camel refuses to move until someone sings.
5. A Reflection of Zeb appears in the maze and says the party is being followed.
6. Dhoomketu sneezes and swaps two heroes' voices for a scene.

## Side quests
- The yaksha's lost coin (oasis).
- Gulnar's eleventh camel is a prince under a curse (optional, silly, kids love it).

## Rewards
- 250 XP each. Level 4.
- Three jars of colour. Dhoomketu as an ally. Firoz as a companion (5 HP, trick: turn one thing a colour).
- A camel each, in theory.

## How it ends and what it sets up
One gem is restored in front of the court. The Vizier congratulates the heroes warmly and that
night the monsoon fails to arrive. The river begins to run backward. Chapter 4.
