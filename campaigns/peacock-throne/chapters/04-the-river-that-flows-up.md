# Chapter 4: The River That Flows Up
The monsoon arrives and the river runs backward toward the mountains, because **Rani Padma**,
queen of the naga, is pulling her stolen rain home. The heroes ride a boat uphill, pass the
Makara rapids, and reach the naga court underwater (Firoz's gift lets them breathe). Padma
reveals the Vizier promised her rain in exchange for the colour of the sky. The heroes broker peace.
Reward: Padma's pearl, which turns any grey thing back to colour once.
