# Chapter 2: The Night Market of Lamps

## Premise
Every family in Sikri floats a lamp on the river on the Festival of Lamps. This year something
fast is snatching lamps off the water. The Vanara Guild is blamed, Captain Hanuvant is under
arrest, and the Vizier has kindly offered to "handle the monkeys". The thief is the Vizier's own
**Clockwork Peacock**, harvesting lamplight to feed his mirror.

## Goal the party will understand
"Find out who is stealing the lamps and clear the monkeys' name before the last lamp goes out."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Hanuvant in chains (social)
> The Vanara Guild's captain sits in a cage in the palace courtyard with his red sash still on
> and his tiny telescope confiscated. He straightens when he sees you. "Friends of the Throne.
> Good. Somebody with sense. We did not take the lamps. We saw what did. Nobody will listen to a
> monkey, so you will have to listen for us."
- **Setup:** Hanuvant describes "a bird made of knives that walks on water". A guard is nervous; the Vizier is nearby, smiling.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha chooses what to bring Hanuvant (mango, blanket, or his telescope back). Arya notices the Vizier's mirror is turned to face the cage.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥭 Comfort Hanuvant and ask gently
  - 👑 Ask Zeb to release him
  - 🔭 Steal back his telescope
  - 🪞 Ask the Vizier directly what he saw
- **Rolls:** Persuasion Tricky to get Zeb to release him (she will, if the party promises results by dawn). Sneaking Tricky for the telescope.
- **Outcomes:** Hanuvant lends his rooftop runners. The Vizier offers "a mirror to catch the thief's reflection" (it is a tracker; see dm/secrets.md).
- **Image:** A large grey langur in a red sash sitting with dignity in a golden cage, children peering in

### Scene 2: Lamp-making (exploration, craft)
> Bibi Chandni's whole street is making lamps. Clay, oil, cotton wicks, marigolds, paint in forty
> colours. "Everyone floats one," she says, handing each of you a lump of clay. "The lamp is a
> wish. Make it the shape of what you want most."
- **Setup:** Each hero makes a real lamp. Kids describe or draw theirs. The DM writes it on their sheet as an item: the **Wish Lamp**, which does one small thing matching its shape, once.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Make the lamp (draw it; it becomes canon). Pick a colour for the flame.
- **Choices:**
  - 🐘 Elephant lamp (strength)
  - 🦜 Parrot lamp (a message)
  - ⭐ Star lamp (light)
  - 🎨 Your own shape
- **Outcomes:** Lamps are made. Ustad Patang the kite-maker offers a kite "big enough to carry a small hero" for later.
- **Image:** Children painting small clay lamps in a lantern-lit alley, marigolds and paint pots everywhere
- **Music:** gentle tabla and humming

### Scene 3: The floating (exploration, then action)
> The river is a second sky. Ten thousand lamps drift out from the ghats, and the city holds its
> breath. Then: a ripple. Something fast and glittering skates across the water, and twenty lamps
> wink out in a line. People scream. Hanuvant's runners whistle from the rooftops: it is heading
> for the old bridge.
- **Setup:** Chase along the ghats and rooftops. The Clockwork Peacock (dm/bestiary.md) can dazzle with its tail.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha floats her lamp and watches which way it drifts (it drifts toward the thief). Arya rides Ustad Patang's kite to see from above (Acrobatics Easy, cannot really fail: the kite catches her).
- **Choices:**
  - 🪁 Chase from the air on the kite
  - 🐒 Chase across the rooftops with the vanara
  - 🚣 Chase on the water by boat
  - 🪞 Use the Vizier's mirror (it leads you the wrong way; a clue)
- **Rolls:** Acrobatics or Athletics Tricky to keep up. The boat route needs Rukmini the boatwoman (dm/encounters.md).
- **Action:** Corner the Clockwork Peacock under the old bridge. It fans its tail (WIS save Tricky or lose a turn). Its weakness is the winding key on its back (Acrobatics Hard, or a Wish Lamp shaped like a key or a hand works automatically). **How it ends:** winds down, bows, and asks for a dance partner.
- **Outcomes:** Inside its chest: a mirror shard showing a fort in the desert made of glass, and a jar of lamplight. A kid who keeps the **winding key** will need it in chapter 5.
- **Image:** A brass clockwork peacock with a fanned tail of mirrored feathers cornered under a stone bridge by lamp-light, a kite overhead
- **Music:** fast tabla, then music-box tinkling as it winds down

### Scene 4: Who built the bird? (social, investigation)
> Morning. The peacock sits in Hakim Fariduddin's observatory with its chest open, ticking
> gently. The old astronomer peers inside with a lens the size of a plate. "This craftsmanship,"
> he says quietly, "I have seen once before. Twenty years ago. A student of mine made a music box
> for the old Padishah." He does not say the name. He does not need to.
- **Setup:** Fariduddin shows the maker's mark: a crescent and a mirror. The kids can match it to the Vizier's mirror chain if they saw it (or roll Investigation Easy).
- **Jobs for the kids:** Match the maker's mark. Choose what to tell Hanuvant when he is freed.
- **Choices:**
  - 👑 Tell Zeb everything
  - 🤐 Keep it secret until there is proof
  - 🪞 Confront the Vizier now (too early; he smiles and the peacock vanishes)
- **Outcomes:** Hanuvant is freed; Vanara +2. Zeb gives the party a royal travel seal for the western desert. The Vizier "recommends" the Mirror Fort too, to get them out of the city.
- **Image:** A very old astronomer with a star-embroidered robe examining the open chest of a clockwork peacock with a huge lens, children peering over the table
- **Music:** quiet strings, ticking

## Complications (roll d6)
1. A lamp sets a stall awning on fire. Buckets, fast. Athletics Easy, everyone.
2. The dazzle makes a kid hero see three peacocks. Pick the one with the shadow.
3. Rukmini's boat leaks. Someone bails (a job for Keisha).
4. The Vizier's mirror whispers a hero's fear. WIS Easy or disadvantage next roll.
5. A river dolphin surfaces with a pearl and a riddle from Rani Padma (chapter 4 seed).
6. Hanuvant's runners bring the wrong lamp back, a gold one belonging to the Padishah. Return it for Court +1.

## Side quests (optional, see side-quests.md)

- Ustad Patang's lost kite.

## Rewards
- 150 XP each. Level 3 for most heroes.
- Wish Lamps (one use each). The winding key. A jar of lamplight (restores one small grey thing).
- Vanara +2, Court +1.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The party knows the Vizier built the bird but cannot prove it. The mirror shard shows the
Mirror Fort. Zeb's seal opens the desert road. Chapter 3 is a journey.
