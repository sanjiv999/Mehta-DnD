# Chapter 2: The Night Market of Lamps

## Premise
Three nights after the parade the city floats its lamps. Last night, forty lamps went out in a
line as something crossed the lake, and the dock-master blamed the vanara guild, because langurs
are what get blamed. Hanuvant has been caged in the palace courtyard. The thief is a
brass peacock the Vizier built twenty years ago as a toy for the old Padishah and has rewound
to gather lamplight for his mirror: colour is water-luck, and a lamp on water is as pure as it
gets. Hanuvant knows who built it. He has not said, because he has said things before.

## Goal the party will understand
"Find out what is taking the lamps and get the monkey captain out of the cage."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The captain in the cage (social)
> The cage is gold and the langur inside it has kept his sash on. His telescope is on a table
> outside the bars, where he can see it. He is sitting very straight. When he sees you he
> stands up, which takes a moment, because the cage is short.
> "Friends of the Throne," he says. "Good. We did not take the lamps. We saw what did. Nobody
> listens to a monkey. You will have to listen for us."
- **Setup:** Morning in the palace courtyard. Hanuvant will describe the thief as "a bird made of knives that walks on water" and will not say whose it is until the party has done something for him. The Vizier is across the courtyard being kind to a gardener. His mirror is turned to face the cage.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu chooses what to bring Hanuvant through the bars: a mango, a blanket, or his telescope. Aarya notices which way the Vizier's mirror is facing.
- **Choices:**
  - 🥭 Bring him something and wait
  - 👑 Get Zeb to open the cage on a promise
  - 🔭 Pass him the telescope when no one is looking
  - 🪞 Ask the Vizier what he saw last night
- **Rolls:** Persuasion Tricky to get Zeb to release him by dawn tomorrow against the party's word. Sneaking Tricky for the telescope. The Vizier says he saw nothing and offers a "mirror that shows a thief's last reflection", which leads the wrong way (Investigation Tricky to notice it always points away from the bridge).
- **Outcomes:** Hanuvant, freed or promised, lends the guild's rooftop runners. He adds, quietly: "It came from inside the walls. Not over them."
- **Image:** A dignified grey langur in a red sash sitting very straight inside a golden cage in a red courtyard, a brass telescope on a table outside the bars
- **Music:** courtyard birds, a fountain, nothing else

### Scene 2: Clay and wick (craft)
> Bibi Chandni's street has stopped selling sweets. Every stall has clay on it. Children are
> pinching lamps into shape with their thumbs, old women are rolling wicks between their palms,
> and a boy is painting marigolds on anything that holds still. Chandni hands each of you a
> lump of clay the size of an egg without asking. "The lamp is a wish," she says. "Make it the
> shape of the thing you want."
- **Setup:** Afternoon before the floating. Each hero makes a lamp. The kids describe or draw theirs; the DM writes it on the sheet as a **Wish Lamp** that does one small thing matching its shape, once. Ustad Patang the kite-maker, fingers bandaged, mentions he can build a kite that would carry "a small one" and has wanted to try for years.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Make the lamp. Choose the colour of its flame; Firoz, who has followed the party up from the well, hiccups it that colour.
- **Choices:**
  - 🐘 An elephant lamp
  - 🦜 A parrot lamp
  - 🔑 A key lamp
  - 🎨 Any shape at all
- **Outcomes:** Four lamps, drying on a stall. Patang's kite, half built, in the alley behind.
- **Image:** Children at a bazaar stall pinching small clay lamps into shapes, marigold paint, an old woman rolling cotton wicks between her palms
- **Music:** a slow flute, hands on clay

### Scene 3: The floating (exploration, then action)
> The city goes quiet all at once. Ten thousand clay lamps go out onto the black lake from the
> steps, each one carried by someone who has walked down into the water to let it go, and the
> lake is lit from underneath. Then a ripple, moving fast and straight. Twenty lamps go out in
> a row with a sound like someone blowing out candles very quickly. People scream. From the
> roofs, a whistle: the guild has seen where it is going. The old bridge.
- **Setup:** Night. The **Clockwork Peacock** (dm/bestiary.md) skates the water on folded feet and swallows lamps whole. It is heading for the bridge, under which the Vizier's man waits with a jar. Chase by roof, water, or air.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu floats her Wish Lamp and watches which way it drifts: it drifts after the thief, against the current (cannot fail). Aarya goes up on Patang's kite with the guild holding the string (Acrobatics Easy; a fail means she comes down in the lake, which she will enjoy).
- **Choices:**
  - 🪁 From the air, on the kite
  - 🐒 Across the roofs with the runners
  - 🚣 On the water in Rukmini's boat
  - 🪞 Follow the Vizier's mirror (it leads to the wrong bridge; a clue)
- **Rolls:** Acrobatics or Athletics Tricky to keep pace. Rukmini's boat is fastest and needs Chhaya the dolphin to find the thing in the dark.
- **Action:** Under the old bridge the peacock turns and fans a tail of mirrored feathers; WIS save Tricky or lose a turn looking at yourself. Its weakness is the winding key on its back: Acrobatics Hard to reach it, or any Wish Lamp shaped like a key or a hand works at once. **How it ends:** it runs down with a long tick, folds its tail, and bows to the smallest hero, who it seems to think is in charge.
- **Outcomes:** Inside the chest, a jar of lamplight and a mirror shard that shows a square building on a dune with no door. The **winding key** is small and brass and a kid will want it; it matters in chapter 5. The Vizier's man is gone.
- **Image:** A brass clockwork peacock with a fanned tail of mirror pieces standing on black water under a stone bridge, lamps going out around it, a kite overhead
- **Music:** fast tabla, water, then a music-box ticking down

### Scene 4: The maker's mark (investigation)
> The fifth floor of the Panch Mahal is one square of shade with the whole lake below it. The
> peacock lies on its back on a brass table with its chest open, ticking. The astronomer has a
> lens the size of a plate and is not looking through it. He is looking at the wall.
> "I have seen this work once before," he says. "A music box, for the old Padishah. Twenty
> years ago. The boy who made it is not a boy now." He does not say the name. Zeb, by the stair,
> has gone very still.
- **Setup:** Morning. Inside the chest, stamped in the brass: a crescent and a mirror. Fariduddin will not name the Vizier, because he taught him and because he has no proof. He gives the party the facts and lets them say it.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Aarya matches the stamp to the mirror on the Vizier's chain (Investigation Easy; she saw it in chapter 1). Keshu decides what the party tells Hanuvant when they let him out.
- **Choices:**
  - 👑 Tell Zeb what the stamp means
  - 🤐 Say nothing until there is proof
  - 🪞 Confront the Vizier (he smiles, says "how clever", and the peacock is gone from the table by evening)
  - 🔭 Ask Fariduddin why he keeps looking at that drawer
- **Outcomes:** Hanuvant freed; Vanara +2. Zeb gives the party a travel seal for the western road and says, carefully, that the Vizier "also thinks the fort is worth seeing." Fariduddin says nothing about the drawer. Mirza says: "Twenty."
- **Image:** A very old astronomer on an open pillared rooftop looking at a wall while a brass peacock lies open on the table beside him, a girl frozen by the stair
- **Music:** wind through pillars, ticking

## Complications (roll d6)
1. A lamp catches an awning. Everyone with hands: Athletics Easy, one round, buckets.
2. The tail-dazzle makes a kid see three peacocks. Only one has a shadow on the water.
3. Rukmini's boat takes water. Keshu bails with her lamp and counts the scoops.
4. The Vizier's mirror says a hero's fear back to them. WIS Easy or disadvantage next roll.
5. Chhaya surfaces with a river pearl in her mouth and will not say where she got it.
6. The runners bring back the wrong lamp: gold, the Padishah's own. Returning it is Court +1.
