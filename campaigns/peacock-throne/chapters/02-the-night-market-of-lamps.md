# Chapter 2: The Night Market of Lamps
On the Festival of Lamps every family floats a lamp on the river. This year the lamps are being
stolen off the water by something fast. The Vanara Guild is blamed. Captain Hanuvant swears
innocence and asks the heroes to clear the guild's name. The thief is a **Clockwork Peacock**
built by the Vizier, gathering lamp-light to feed his mirror. Scenes: rooftop race with the
vanara, a lamp-floating ceremony where the kids design their own lamp (the design becomes a
real item), the chase and capture of the peacock, and a clue inside it: a mirror shard that shows
the Mirror Fort in the western desert.
