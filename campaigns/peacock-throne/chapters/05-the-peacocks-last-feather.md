# Chapter 5: The Peacock's Last Feather
The last gem is going grey. The Vizier of Mirrors reveals his plan: a throne with no colour can
be ruled by anyone who holds the mirror. The finale is in the throne room with the whole court
watching: a mirror maze, the Clockwork Peacock rebuilt, and the Vizier himself, who can only be
beaten by the heroes each showing him their truest colour (a roleplay moment per hero, then a
roll). Mirza the parrot finally speaks and tells everyone what the Vizier said twenty years ago.
Reward: the **wick of the Lantern of Many Roads** falls from the restored throne's peacock feather.
The Baoli's dry step opens as a Door. Badal gets a medal.
