# The Shahi Bazaar

**Built by:** The palace, as a single straight street of stone stalls running downhill from the Elephant Gate, so the court could buy without going far. The cloth awnings came later and are so low the grown-ups duck.
**First seen:** Bibi Chandni's pan at the top. Then cones of turmeric and chilli as tall as a child. Then the street falling away under cloth, loud.
**Hour:** Mornings are for bargaining. At noon the awnings come down and the street is empty except for the monkeys.
**Sound and smell:** A spice seller shouting prices at someone who has not asked. Hot ghee, turmeric, dung-fire. Bangles.
**Who is here now:** Everyone. The kite-maker Ustad Patang with scarred fingers from glass-dust string. At the quiet end, Daulat with mirrors nobody buys.
**Recently changed:** Something elephant-sized has walked down the middle through a spilled sack of turmeric, and the footprints go all the way down the hill.

## Secret
Daulat is the Vizier's brother. Kindness to him before chapter 5 is the best investment in the campaign.
