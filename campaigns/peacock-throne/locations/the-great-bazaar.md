# The Great Bazaar

**First impression:** A canyon of colour. Spice pyramids, silk awnings, a hundred voices bargaining.
**Sights, sounds, smells:** Turmeric and cinnamon, bells, copper being hammered, monkeys overhead.
**Who is here:** Bibi Chandni at the mouth, every merchant in the empire, the Vanara Guild above.
**What can happen here:** Tracking, bargaining, rooftop chases, a job for every hero.

## Three stalls worth remembering
1. **Chandni's sweets**: information.
2. **The kite-maker Ustad Patang**: can build a kite big enough to carry a kid hero (chapter 2).
3. **The mirror-seller with no customers**: sells the Vizier's mirrors. Nobody buys. He looks grey.

## Secret
The mirror-seller is the Vizier's brother and will help the party in chapter 5 if treated kindly before.
