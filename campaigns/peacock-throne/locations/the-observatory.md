# The Panch Mahal

**Built by:** The first Padishah, as five storeys of pillars with no walls, each floor smaller than the one below, so the court could sit in the wind. 176 pillars. The top floor is one small square of shade with the best breeze in the city.
**First seen:** The pillars. Then the brass instruments on the top floor that were built in place because they would not fit up the stairs.
**Hour:** Noon, when the whole city wants the top floor and only the astronomer has it. The Vanara Guild has petitioned eleven times.
**Sound and smell:** Wind through pillars, the creak of a great brass ring turning, oil, old paper.
**Who is here now:** Hakim Fariduddin and his apprentice Shireen, who does the stairs.
**Recently changed:** A locked drawer that was always locked has a new key on the astronomer's belt.
