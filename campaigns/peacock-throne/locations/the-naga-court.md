# The Naga Court

**First impression:** A city of pearl under green water. Snakes with crowns. Everyone is extremely polite.
**Sights, sounds, smells:** Bubbles, bells, lotus.
**Who is here:** Rani Padma and her court, river dolphins who deliver messages.
**What can happen here:** Chapter 4.

## Secret
Padma is a victim of the Vizier, not an ally of his. She will weep when she learns the sky's colour was never his to trade.
