# The Naga Court

**Built by:** The naga, of river shell, under the lake that feeds the river, over a very long time. Everything is round because nothing underwater needs corners.
**First seen:** Lights under green water. Then pillars of shell. Then crowned heads turning to look at you, every one at the same moment, politely.
**Hour:** The court keeps no hours. The light from above is the only clock and it is dim.
**Sound and smell:** Bubbles, a bell that rings underwater without a clapper, lotus.
**Who is here now:** Rani Padma, her court, Nagini Tara under a table, river dolphins carrying messages, and the sky-jar.
**Recently changed:** The rain has been pulled home. The court musicians are playing the Padishah's parade song slowly, because that is what they were given with the jar.

## Secret
Padma does not know the sky was a child's. Telling her is the whole of chapter 4.
