# The Diwan-i-Am

**Built by:** The first Padishah, as the Hall of Public Audience: open on three sides so the people could see the throne and the throne could see them. The throne itself is gold set with a thousand gems in the pattern of a peacock's tail.
**First seen:** The throne, from the courtyard, through the pillars. Then the parrot perch above it. Then, if you look, the grey gems.
**Hour:** Thursdays, when petitions are heard, and festival evenings when the throne is carried out to the parade.
**Sound and smell:** The scrape of silk slippers on stone, rose water, drums from the gate when someone important arrives.
**Who is here now:** The Padishah when he must be; Zeb beside him; the Vizier one step behind; Mirza above.
**Recently changed:** A ruby the size of a fist went grey last night. By morning there are four.
