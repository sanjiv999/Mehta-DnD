# The Ghats

**Built by:** The city, as wide stone steps into the lake for washing, boats, and the lamp festival. The steps go on under the water, which is the point.
**First seen:** The steps, the boats with eyes on their prows, and a line scratched into the stone two steps above where the water is now.
**Hour:** Washing at dawn. Boats all day. On the Festival of Lamps, after dark, ten thousand clay lamps go out on the water and the city goes quiet to watch.
**Sound and smell:** Water on stone, bells from the shore temples, wet rope, marigolds.
**Who is here now:** Rukmini and the boat families, Chhaya the dolphin who follows Rukmini's boat, and on festival nights everyone.
**Recently changed:** The scratched line. The water used to reach it.
