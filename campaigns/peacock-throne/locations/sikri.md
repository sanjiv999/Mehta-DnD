# Sikri, the Red City

**First impression:** Red sandstone walls catching the sun, white marble domes above them, kites in every colour.
**Sights, sounds, smells:** Drums and parrots, cardamom and dust, the hum of the bazaar, cool marble underfoot in the palace.
**Who is here:** The court, the bazaar, the Vanara Guild on the rooftops, the Observatory on the hill, the Baoli below.
**What can happen here:** Nearly everything in chapters 1, 2 and 5.

## Districts
- **Diwan-i-Am** (Hall of Public Audience): where the throne sits and the festival parades end.
- **The Great Bazaar**: see its own page.
- **The Ghats**: stone steps to the river, lamp-floating, boats.
- **The Observatory**: Hakim Fariduddin's tower of brass instruments.
- **The Baoli of Whispers**: see its own page.
