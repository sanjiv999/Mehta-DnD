# Sikri

**Built by:** The first Padishah, on a ridge above a lake, in red sandstone from the ridge itself. The only white building is the saint's tomb by the mosque.
**First seen:** From the plain, the walls and the great gate. Closer, the drum house over the Elephant Gate with two stone elephants either side, and from the Elephant Gate the smell of the stables.
**Hour:** At dawn the Padishah shows himself at the balcony and the drums play. At noon the awnings come down and the langurs own the roofs. In the last hour of light the kites go up from every roof. At night the loudest thing is the water in the channels.
**Sound and smell:** Drums on the hour; wet sandstone and rose attar inside the palace; dung-fire and ghee in the bazaar; indigo and ash below the south wall.
**Who is here now:** The court inside the walls, the bazaar below them, the dyers below that, the Vanara Guild above everyone, the astronomer on the fifth floor, and the baoli under the whole city.
**Recently changed:** The lake is lower than the mark Rukmini scratched on the ghats. The parrots in the gardens have gone quiet.

## Districts
- **Diwan-i-Am**, the Hall of Public Audience: a pillared hall open on three sides with the throne at one end. Thursdays.
- **The pachisi court** by the pool: the Padishah's giant board, servants for pieces.
- **The Panch Mahal**: five storeys of pillars holding up shade. The astronomer has the top one.
- **The Shahi Bazaar**, downhill under awnings. See its own page.
- **The ghats** on the lake. **The baoli** under the old city. See their pages.
