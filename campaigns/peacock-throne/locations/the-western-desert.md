# The Western Road

**Built by:** Nobody. Camels made it. The caravanserais along it were built by merchants who were tired of being robbed: square courtyards with one gate, a well in the middle, rooms around the walls.
**First seen:** Thorn scrub and dust for a day. Then the ground turns to sand and the sky gets bigger than seems reasonable.
**Hour:** Walk at dawn and in the last hours of light. At noon, sit under the camel.
**Sound and smell:** Camel bells, wind, nothing. At night, more stars than the city has lamps.
**Who is here now:** Gulnar and her eleven camels, salt traders, a yaksha at the oasis who has lost his coin, sand djinn-lets, and sometimes a mirage of your own house.
**Recently changed:** The traders say the fort "sings at noon" this year, which it never used to.
