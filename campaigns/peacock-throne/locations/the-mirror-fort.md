# The Mirror Fort

**First impression:** A fort on a dune that you cannot look at directly at noon. Every wall reflects.
**Sights, sounds, smells:** Silence, hot glass, the smell of rain that is not coming.
**Who is here:** Dhoomketu the rakshasa illusionist and his reflections. Jars of colour.
**What can happen here:** Chapter 3.

## Secret
The fort has no door. You enter by stepping into your own reflection on the north wall at sunset.
