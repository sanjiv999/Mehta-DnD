# The Mirror Fort

**Built by:** A rakshasa, from a ruined caravanserai, by setting every outer wall with thousands of pieces of mirror the way palace ceilings are set with mirror-work, so that at noon you cannot look at it and at sunset it shows you yourself.
**First seen:** A flash on the horizon at noon that the camels refuse to walk toward. At sunset, a square building on a dune with no gate, with you standing on every wall.
**Hour:** Sunset is the only hour you can approach it.
**Sound and smell:** Hot glass ticking as it cools. The smell of rain that is not coming. Silence that makes the camels nervous.
**Who is here now:** Dhoomketu, his four hundred portraits, and the jars.
**Recently changed:** Three of the mirror pieces on the north wall have gone black.

## Secret
You enter by stepping into your own reflection on the north wall at sunset and saying one true thing. Wrong turns inside are mirrors; the real corridor is the one where your reflection has a shadow.
