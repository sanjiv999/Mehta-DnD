# Chapter 3: Into the Cloud

## Premise
The nebula is beautiful, confusing, and alive. Inside, instruments fail and the only way to
navigate is by the song: the *Pakora* can follow it. A lost ship, the *Lantern Jack*, is drifting
inside with a Choir crew; rescuing them is the first job. The second is the thing that turns:
a Cloud-Whale the size of a city, old, gentle, and the source of half the song. The other half
is a child.

## Goal the party will understand
"Fly in, find the lost ship, and find out what is singing."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The edge (exploration, flight)
> The *Pakora* slips into the cloud and the stars go out. Violet mist, lit from inside like a lamp
> through a curtain. The instruments spin and give up. "Right," says the ship. "Eyes and ears.
> The song's louder on the left. Who's got a good ear?"
- **Setup:** Navigation by sound. Each hero takes a job: ears (Perception), hands (piloting, DEX), eyes (spotting shapes, WIS), heart (talking to the ship, CHA). Three legs of flight; each leg one roll, Easy; a kid's hum counts as a success automatically.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha hums the melody and points where it is louder. Arya watches for shapes (cloud-fish, drifting cargo, a glowing path).
- **Choices:**
  - 👂 Follow the song
  - 👀 Follow the lights
  - 🐟 Follow the cloud-fish
- **Outcomes:** Cloud-fish (harmless, curious, glowing) escort the ship. On the third leg: a hull, dark, drifting: the *Lantern Jack*.
- **Image:** A small round ship inside a violet cloud with glowing cloud-fish swimming alongside, a child pointing, instruments spinning uselessly

### Scene 2: The Lantern Jack (exploration, rescue)
> The lost ship is dark and cold and full of singing. Its crew are in the galley, holding hands
> in a circle, humming in perfect harmony with their eyes closed, smiling. The air is nearly out.
> Their captain, a young man named Tomas, opens his eyes when Keisha hums. "You hear it," he
> whispers. "It's a child. It's a *child* singing. And something enormous is singing back."
- **Setup:** Rescue: get the crew to the *Pakora* through a drifting ship during a gravity flicker (zero-g for the whole scene: the *Lantern Jack*'s gravity is dead). Six crew; each hero guides one or two (Athletics Easy). Tomas is lucid when a kid hums; the song answered makes the Choir *wake*. This is the campaign's trick: answer the song, and the singers come back.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha hums to wake each crew member. Arya guides Tomas through the dark corridors (Perception Easy with the ship's lamp).
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Hum to wake the crew
  - 🪢 Line them up and tow
  - 🔦 Light the corridors with the cloud-fish (they follow if asked)
  - 🤖 Send PIP or KNIT-9 (if brought) to patch the air
- **Outcomes:** All six saved. The *Lantern Jack* is towed. Tomas says the song has two voices: a small one, lost, and a vast one, answering. The *Pakora*, quietly: "The big one's turning toward us. I think it's curious."
- **Image:** A dark ship's galley lit by a child's lamp, six crew floating in a circle holding hands with their eyes closed, a small girl humming to them

### Scene 3: The Cloud-Whale (wonder, social)
> It fills the window. It fills every window. A shape like a whale, like a mountain, like a
> cloud that decided to be an animal, lit from within in slow violet pulses, with an eye the
> size of the station that opens, slowly, and looks at the *Pakora*. And it sings the vast half
> of the song, so close that the ship's cup of tea trembles. The *Pakora* says, very small: "Hello."
- **Setup:** First contact. The Whale (dm/bestiary.md: not a fight) communicates in song and light. Communication minigame: each hero offers something (a hummed phrase, a light pattern on the hull, a drawing held to the window, a question). The Whale answers in images in the cloud (the DM narrates). It shows: a small glowing shape, lost in the deep cloud, singing. Its child. And a station, taking the child's light in a jar, long ago, by accident.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha holds her drawing to the window. Arya flashes the ship's lights in the melody's rhythm.
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Sing to it
  - 💡 Flash the lights
  - 🖼️ Show it a drawing
  - ❓ Ask it what it wants
- **Outcomes:** The Whale wants its child found and its child's light returned. The jar in Airlock 7 is the child's light, taken by the crew (the heroes) before ORBIT forgot: they were *bringing it back* when the memory failed. Level 6.
- **Image:** A cloud-whale the size of a city with a slowly opening eye filling the view, a tiny ship with a child holding a crayon drawing up to the window
- **Music:** whale-song and the melody together

### Scene 4: The deep (action, flight)
> The Whale turns, slowly, and opens a path into the deeper cloud with its body, and the path is
> dark, and the small voice is down there. "Storm pockets," says the ship. "Lightning that
> sings. Hold on to something. Hold on to each other."
- **Setup:** Flight sequence through singing lightning (DEX saves Tricky; the ship takes 1d6 per fail, HP 30 for the ship; the kids' jobs reduce risk). Cloud-fish scatter. At the bottom: a small glowing shape the size of a house, dim, singing a thin tired song. The child. Too weak to follow the Whale home without its light.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha holds the ship's "hands" (a lever) steady. Arya calls out the lightning (Perception Easy gives advantage on the pilot's save).
- **Choices:**
  - ⚡ Fly straight through
  - 🐟 Follow a cloud-fish's path
  - 🐋 Ride in the Whale's wake (safest, slowest)
- **Outcomes:** The child is found, dim and tired. It cannot be moved. Its light is on the station. The party must go back, get the jar, and bring it here, with the whole Council trying to stop them. Cliffhanger.
- **Image:** A small round ship diving through violet storm-canyons lit by singing lightning, a tiny glowing shape far below, children bracing at the controls
- **Music:** storm, then the thin tired song

## Complications (roll d6)
1. A cloud-fish gets inside the ship. It likes the tea.
2. The Lantern Jack's cook was mid-soup when the song took them. The soup is still good.
3. The Whale sneezes. The *Pakora* tumbles; everyone floats; nobody is hurt; the ship is indignant.
4. Tomas proposes to the *Pakora*. She declines kindly.
5. A kid's drawing, held to the window, appears in the cloud a mile wide.
6. The lightning sings a kid's name. They sing it back; the lightning is pleased and goes quiet.

## Rewards
- 300 XP. Level 6. Six rescued crew and the *Lantern Jack* (Dock Guild +3). The Whale's trust. The child's location.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The jar in Airlock 7 is a child's light. The Council has sealed the dock again, this time with
Vexley's own ship across the doors. Chapter 4 is a heist on your own station.
