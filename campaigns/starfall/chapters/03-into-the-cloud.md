# Chapter 3: Into the Cloud

## Premise
Inside the nebula the instruments spin and the only way to navigate is by the song. The
*Lantern Jack* is drifting with six crew holding hands in the galley and the air nearly out.
Past it, the thing that turns: the mother, who sings the low half. Below her, the high half:
her child, dim, who cannot follow her home.

## Goal the party will understand
"Fly in, find the lost ship, and find out what is singing."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Eyes and ears (flight)
> The stars go out and the cloud comes in, violet, lit from somewhere inside like a lamp behind
> a curtain. The instruments spin and give up. "Right," says the ship. "Eyes and ears. The
> song's louder to the left. Who's got a good ear?"
- **Setup:** Three legs. Each hero takes a job: ears (Perception), hands (DEX), eyes (WIS), heart (CHA, talking to the ship). One roll per leg, Easy; a child humming counts as a success without a roll.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha hums and points where it is louder. Arya watches for shapes.
- **Choices:**
  - 👂 Follow the song
  - 👀 Follow the lights
  - 🐟 Follow the cloud-fish
- **Outcomes:** Cloud-fish, glinting and harmless, swim alongside. On the third leg, a hull with no lights.
- **Image:** A small round ship inside violet cloud with glinting cloud-fish alongside, a child in the cockpit pointing, the instruments spinning
- **Music:** a drone, the tune under it

### Scene 2: Holding hands (rescue)
> The lost ship is dark and cold and full of singing. In the galley six people float in a
> circle holding hands with their eyes shut, humming in parts, smiling. Soup has gone cold on
> the stove. The air is thin. The captain opens his eyes when Keisha hums. "You hear it," he
> whispers. "It's a child. It's a child singing. And its mother singing back."
- **Setup:** Zero-g throughout; the *Lantern Jack*'s gravity is dead. Six crew; each hero guides one or two (Athletics Easy). A kid humming wakes each one as they are reached. The trick of the campaign: answer the song and the singer wakes.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha hums to wake each crew member. Arya guides Captain Tomas by the ship's lamp.
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Hum them awake
  - 🪢 Line them up and tow
  - 🔦 Light the corridors with cloud-fish (they follow if asked)
  - 🤖 Send a robot to patch the air
- **Outcomes:** All six out. Tomas: two voices, a small one lost, a vast one answering. The ship, quietly: "The big one's turning toward us. I think it's curious."
- **Image:** A dark galley lit by a child's lamp, six crew floating in a circle holding hands with their eyes closed, a small girl humming to them
- **Music:** harmony, then one voice

### Scene 3: Hello (first contact)
> It fills the window. It fills every window. A whale, a hill, a cloud that has decided to be
> an animal, lit from inside in slow pulses, and an eye the size of a dock door opens and looks
> at the ship, and the low half of the song is so close the tea trembles in its cup. The ship
> says, very small: "Hello."
- **Setup:** No fight. The Whale talks in song and light; the heroes talk back with anything: a hummed phrase, the hull lights in rhythm, a drawing held to the window, a question. She answers in pictures in the cloud: a small shape, lost; a station taking its light in a jar.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha holds her drawing to the window. Arya flashes the lights in the tune's rhythm.
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Sing to her
  - 💡 Flash the lights
  - 🖼️ Show a drawing
  - ❓ Ask what she wants
- **Outcomes:** She wants her child found and its light returned. The jar in Airlock 7 is the child's light; the crew were bringing it back when the station forgot. Level 6.
- **Image:** A cloud-whale with a slowly opening eye filling the view, a tiny ship with a child holding a crayon drawing up to the window
- **Music:** whale song and the tune together

### Scene 4: Lightning that sings (flight)
> The Whale turns and opens a path into the deep cloud with her body. It is dark down there,
> and the small voice is in it. "Storm pockets," says the ship. "Lightning that sings. Hold on
> to something. Hold on to each other."
- **Setup:** DEX saves Tricky per leg; the ship takes 1d6 per fail (HP 30). Cloud-fish scatter. At the bottom, the child: as long as the dock, dim, singing thinly. It cannot be moved. Its light is on the station.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha holds the lever steady. Arya calls the lightning (Perception Easy gives the pilot advantage).
- **Choices:**
  - ⚡ Straight through
  - 🐟 A cloud-fish's path
  - 🐋 In the Whale's wake (slow, safe)
- **Outcomes:** The child found, too weak to follow. The party must go back for the jar, with a council that has sealed the dock again. Cliffhanger.
- **Image:** A small round ship diving through violet storm canyons lit by lightning, a tiny glowing shape far below, children bracing at the controls
- **Music:** storm, then a thin high note

## Complications (roll d6)
1. A cloud-fish gets inside the ship. It likes tea.
2. The *Lantern Jack*'s soup is still good.
3. The Whale sneezes. Everyone floats. The ship is indignant.
4. Tomas proposes to the ship. She declines kindly.
5. A child's drawing held to the window appears in the cloud a mile wide.
6. The lightning sings a child's name. Sing it back and it goes quiet.

## Rewards
- 300 XP. Level 6. Six crew and the *Lantern Jack* (Docks +3). The Whale's trust. The child's position.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The jar is a child's light. The council has sealed the dock again, and this time Vexley has
parked his own ship across the doors.
