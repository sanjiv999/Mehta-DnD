# Chapter 6: The Lantern of Many Roads

## Premise
Played only when all four worlds are complete. The Lantern is assembled, and when it is lit
every Door opens at once onto one place. Every friend from every world arrives. The family
decides what the Lantern lights: a fifth world they write themselves, a way for friends to visit
between worlds, or a very good party.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Four pieces (ceremony)
> Four things on a table. A brass frame, still warm. A paper shade painted with a fox and a
> mountain. A wick that is one peacock feather. A jar with a light in it, humming. Nobody has
> ever put these together. It is probably fine.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Each fits one piece. They decide the order and are right.
- **Choices:**
  - 🔧 Frame first
  - 🏮 Shade first
  - 🪶 Wick first
  - 💡 Light last, always
- **Outcomes:** The Lantern lights. Four Doors open in the walls of wherever you are.
- **Image:** Four children's hands fitting a brass frame, a painted paper shade, a feather wick and a jar of light together on a table

### Scene 2: Everyone (reunion)
> Through one door, a white elephant with marigolds on his forehead, a girl with ink to the
> knuckle, a parrot. Through the next, a four-tailed fox and an oni bowing as he comes. Through
> the third, a seven-year-old on an eleven-foot bear, and a dragon's head, politely, just the
> head. Through the last, an octopus with a banner, a ship's voice on a speaker, and a tall man
> with a clipboard who has been crying a little.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Introduce the friends to each other. Dhoomketu and Ponkichi will get on alarmingly well.
- **Choices:**
  - 🐘 Badal meets the dragon
  - 🦊 Kikyo meets Wren
  - 🐙 Oola meets Rani Padma
  - 🎉 Start the feast
- **Outcomes:** A feast. Every world's reputation becomes "family".
- **Image:** A reunion in a lantern-lit room: an elephant, a fox, a dragon's head through a door, an octopus, robots, goblins, naga, tanuki, all around four children

### Scene 3: What it lights (choice)
> The Lantern can light one more road. Nobody knows where. Zeb says a library. Kikyo says a
> mountain. Wren says a tower, obviously. The ship says somewhere with a good repair bay.
> Everyone looks at you.
- **Setup:** The family decides the fifth world. `python tools/campaign.py new <id> "<Title>"`. The kids name it. The Lantern is its Door.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha chooses the first thing the new road should have. Arya names the world.
- **Choices:**
  - 🌍 A new world
  - 🚪 Keep the Doors open for visiting
  - 🎉 Just the party, for now
- **Outcomes:** 1,000 XP. "Keeper of the Lantern" for everyone, forever.
- **Image:** A lantern held up by four children lighting a road that leads somewhere nobody has drawn yet

## Complications (roll d6)
1. Badal does not fit through his Door. Sunder, politely, widens it.
2. Dhoomketu and Ponkichi organise a parade in four minutes.
3. The ship has parked herself in the Crossroads and will not move. It is Tuesday somewhere.
4. Kikyo and Mirza compare riddles. Nobody else can follow.
5. The Lantern flickers when two children argue about the fifth world. Decide together and it steadies.
6. Oola's banner is forty metres long. KNIT-9 is delighted.

## Rewards
- 1,000 XP. The Lantern. A fifth world, if chosen. Every friend, always.
- "Keeper of the Lantern" for everyone, forever.

## How it ends and what it sets up
Whatever the family decides. The next campaign folder is where the Lantern goes.
