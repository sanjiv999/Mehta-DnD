# Chapter 6: The Lantern of Many Roads

## Premise
This chapter is played only when all four campaigns are complete. The Lantern is assembled
(frame, shade, wick, light) in whichever world the party is in, and when it is lit, every Door
opens at once into one shared place: the Crossroads. Every friend from every world arrives. The
party decides what the Lantern lights: a new road (a fifth campaign, written by the family), a
way for friends to visit between worlds, or simply a very good party.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Assembly (puzzle, ceremony)
> Four pieces on a table. The brass frame, warm. The paper shade, painted with a fox and a
> mountain. The wick, a single peacock feather. And the light, in a jar, humming. Nobody has ever
> put these together. It is probably fine.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Each kid fits a piece. The order matters: the kids decide and are right.
- **Choices:**
  - 🔧 Frame first
  - 🏮 Shade first
  - 🪶 Wick first
  - 💡 Light last (always)
- **Outcomes:** The Lantern lights. Four Doors open in the walls of wherever you are.
- **Image:** Four children's hands fitting a brass frame, a painted paper shade, a feather wick and a jar of light together on a table

### Scene 2: The Crossroads (celebration, reunion)
> Through one door, a white elephant, and a girl with ink on her fingers, and a parrot. Through
> another, a four-tailed fox and a very polite oni. Through the third, a seven-year-old wizard on
> an eleven-foot bear, and a dragon's head, politely, just the head. Through the last, an octopus
> with a banner, a ship's voice on a speaker, and a tall man with a clipboard who is crying a little.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Introduce the friends to each other. (Dhoomketu and Ponkichi are going to get on *extremely* well.)
- **Choices:**
  - 🐘 Introduce Badal to the dragon
  - 🦊 Introduce Kikyo to Wren
  - 🐙 Introduce Oola to Rani Padma
  - 🎉 Start the feast
- **Outcomes:** A feast. Every world's reputation becomes "family".
- **Image:** A great reunion in a lantern-lit crossroads: an elephant, a fox, a dragon's head through a door, an octopus, robots, goblins, naga, tanuki, all around four children

### Scene 3: What the Lantern lights (choice)
> The Lantern can light one more road. Nobody knows where. Zeb says it should be a library.
> Kikyo says a mountain. Wren says a tower, obviously. The *Pakora* says "somewhere with a good
> repair bay". Everyone looks at you.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha picks the first thing the new road should have. Arya names the world.
- **Setup:** The family decides the fifth world. `python tools/campaign.py new <id> "<Title>"`. The kids name it. The Lantern is its Door.
- **Choices:**
  - 🌍 A new world (name it)
  - 🚪 Keep the Doors open for visits
  - 🎉 Just the party, for now
- **Outcomes:** 1,000 XP. "Keeper of the Lantern" achievement for everyone, forever.
- **Image:** A lantern held up by four children lighting a road that leads somewhere nobody has drawn yet

## Rewards
- 1,000 XP. The Lantern. A fifth world, if chosen. Every friend, always.

## Complications (roll d6)
1. Badal does not fit through his Door. Sunder, politely, widens it.
2. Dhoomketu and Ponkichi discover they both love a parade and organise one in four minutes.
3. The *Pakora* has parked herself in the Crossroads and refuses to move. It is Tuesday somewhere.
4. Kiko and Mirza compare riddles. Nobody else can follow.
5. The Lantern flickers whenever two kids argue about the fifth world. Decide together and it steadies.
6. Oola's banner is forty metres long. KNIT-9 is delighted.

- 1,000 XP. The Lantern. A fifth world, if chosen. Every friend, always.
- "Keeper of the Lantern" achievement for everyone, forever.

## How it ends and what it sets up
Whatever the family decides. The Lantern lights the next road, and the next campaign folder is where it goes.
