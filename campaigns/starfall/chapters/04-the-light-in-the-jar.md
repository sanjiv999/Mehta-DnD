# Chapter 4: The Light in the Jar

## Premise
Back on the station, Vexley has parked his own ship across the dock doors and the Council has
voted to seal the nebula lane permanently. ORBIT is remembering more every hour and is not sure
whose side she is on, because she is the station and the station is scared. The heroes must
get the jar from Airlock 7, get the *Pakora* out, and bring Vexley around, or go through him.

## Goal the party will understand
"Get the jar, get the ship out, and don't let anyone get hurt, including Vexley."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The vote (social)
> The Council votes seven to one. Oola is the one. Vexley is pale. "I have the *Lantern Jack*'s
> crew back because of you," he says to the heroes, quietly, after. "I know. I know. And I am
> still going to seal it, because next time it might be your ship that doesn't come back, and I
> would have to tell ORBIT." ORBIT, from the ceiling, says nothing, which she has never done.
- **Setup:** Vexley is the "antagonist" and he is right to be scared. The heroes can plan a heist, or plan to convince him, or both. The planning scene is the kids' job: a map on the table, robots to assign, a gravity failure in 48 minutes.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha assigns each robot a job in the plan. Arya picks the route on the scarf-map.
- **Choices:**
  - 🗺️ Plan the heist
  - 🗣️ Try Vexley one more time (Persuasion Hard; he almost)
  - 🐙 Ask Oola to stall the Council
  - 🤖 Ask ORBIT, directly, what she wants (she says "I want my crew to come home. I don't know how to say that to a Council.")
- **Outcomes:** Plan made. ORBIT will not stop them. She will not help, yet.
- **Image:** Children leaning over a station map on a table with four robots, an octopus and a worried AI's glowing panel, drawing a route with a crayon

### Scene 2: The heist (action, stealth, zero-g)
> Forty-eight minutes later, gravity fails on schedule. TUBA plays, badly, loudly, in the Council
> chamber. SONNET reads the dock guards a poem so long they sit down. KNIT-9 has knitted the
> security cameras tiny hats. And you float, in a line, holding hands, down the maintenance
> shaft to a wall between hatches 6 and 8.
- **Setup:** Stealth montage with jobs: get to Airlock 7 (Sneaking Easy with the distractions), retrieve the jar (it hums louder; it knows where it is going), get back to the dock (Athletics Easy in zero-g), where Vexley's ship, the *Prudence*, is across the doors with Vexley on its bridge.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha carries the jar (cannot drop it; the jar is on her side). Arya leads the hand-chain through the shaft.
- **Choices:**
  - 🤫 Sneak with the robots' cover
  - 💨 Rush during the gravity failure
  - 🎵 Hum: the jar lights the way
  - 🧶 Take the scarf route (longer, safer)
- **Rolls:** Sneaking Easy; a fail means a guard sees you and SONNET reads him a *second* poem.
- **Outcomes:** The dock. The *Pakora* is ready. The *Prudence* is in the way. Vexley comes out onto its ramp, alone.
- **Image:** Children floating in a hand-chain through a maintenance shaft during zero gravity, the lead child holding a glowing jar, security cameras wearing tiny knitted hats

### Scene 3: Vexley (social, climax)
> He does not have a weapon. He has a clipboard. "I can't move my ship," he says. "I promised the
> Council. If you want to go, you'll have to go around me, and there isn't an around." The jar in
> Keisha's hands pulses, and from the big window the nebula pulses back, and ORBIT says, for the
> first time: "Chair Vexley. The crew would like to speak."
- **Setup:** The resolution is persuasion and honesty, not force. Three things move Vexley: the jar (show him it is a child's light), the rescued crew (Tomas speaks for them), and ORBIT (she tells the Council who the heroes are and what they were doing when she forgot them). Each is a scene beat; the kids deliver the first.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha shows Vexley the jar and tells him it is a baby's light. Arya tells him the Whale is a mother.
- **Choices:**
  - 🫙 Show him the jar
  - 🗣️ Let Tomas speak
  - 🤖 Let ORBIT speak
  - 🚀 Go around (the *Pakora* says she can, "just barely, if everybody breathes in")
- **Rolls:** Persuasion Tricky per beat; sincere kid statements are automatic.
- **Outcomes:** Vexley moves his ship. He asks to come. ("If it goes wrong, someone should be there to tell them I was wrong.") The dock opens. ORBIT, over every speaker: "Crew departing. Come home."
- **Image:** A tall man with a clipboard on a ship's ramp looking down at a small girl holding up a glowing jar, an AI panel lit warm behind them
- **Music:** ticking stops, a hopeful swell

### Scene 4: Departure (celebration, flight)
> The *Pakora* lifts with the jar on the dashboard, Vexley strapped in looking terrified, Oola
> waving with all eight arms, the Hobby Club lined up along the dock, and TUBA, for once, playing
> in tune. The cloud-fish are waiting at the edge. The Whale is a shape in the mist, turning
> toward you.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha names the song (it needs a title; canon). Arya plots the course by the scarf.
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Name the song
  - 🧶 Plot the course
  - 👋 Wave to the Hobby Club
- **Outcomes:** 250 XP. Council +2 (Vexley's change of heart), ORBIT remembers everything. Chapter 5.
- **Image:** A small ship lifting from a dock lined with waving robots and an octopus, a glowing jar on the dashboard, a nebula ahead

## Complications (roll d6)
1. TUBA's distraction is so good the Council applauds and asks for an encore.
2. The jar wants to float. Gravity fails and it drifts toward the window. Catch it.
3. ORBIT remembers something embarrassing about a hero from before. She shares it.
4. A guard is won over by SONNET's poem and joins the Hobby Club mid-heist.
5. Vexley's clipboard contains, on the last page, a drawing of the nebula by his own daughter.
6. The *Pakora* gets stage fright at the open doors. A kid talks her through it.

## Rewards
- 250 XP. The jar. Vexley as a passenger and ally. ORBIT whole. Robots +3, Council +2.

## How it ends and what it sets up
Into the cloud, with the light.
