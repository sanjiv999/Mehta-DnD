# Chapter 4: The Light in the Jar

## Premise
Back on the station, the council has voted seven to one to seal the lane. Vexley has parked
his own ship across the dock doors. ORBIT remembers more by the hour and cannot bring herself
to overrule a council. The heroes must get the jar from Airlock 7, get the ship out, and bring
Vexley round or go through him. Nobody gets hurt.

## Goal the party will understand
"Get the jar, get the ship out, and don't let anyone get hurt, including Vexley."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Seven to one (social)
> Seven hands up. One octopus's eight arms down. Vexley is pale. Afterwards, in the corridor,
> he says to the heroes, quietly: "I have the Lantern Jack's crew back because of you. I know.
> And I'm still going to seal it, because next time it might be your ship, and I'd have to tell
> ORBIT." From the ceiling, ORBIT says nothing, which she has never done.
- **Setup:** Plan a heist, or plan to convince him, or both. The planning scene is the kids' job: a map on a table, robots to assign, forty-eight minutes to the outage.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu assigns each robot a job. Aarya picks the route on the scarf.
- **Choices:**
  - 🗺️ Plan the heist
  - 🗣️ Try Vexley once more (Persuasion Hard; he nearly)
  - 🐙 Ask Oola to stall the council
  - 🤖 Ask ORBIT what she wants ("I want my crew to come home. I don't know how to say that to a council.")
- **Outcomes:** A plan. ORBIT will not stop them. She will not help, yet.
- **Image:** Children over a station map with four robots, an octopus and a glowing wall panel, drawing a route in crayon
- **Music:** a clock

### Scene 2: Forty-eight minutes (stealth)
> On the hour the floor lets go. In the council chamber TUBA starts to play, loudly, with
> feeling. At the dock SONNET is reading the guards a poem so long they have sat down. KNIT-9
> has knitted every security camera a small hat. And you float in a line down the maintenance
> shaft, holding hands, to a wall between hatch 6 and hatch 8.
- **Setup:** Sneaking Easy with the distractions; a fail means a guard sees you and SONNET starts a second poem. The jar hums louder as it is carried; it knows where it is going. Back at the dock: Vexley's ship the *Prudence* across the doors, Vexley on its ramp.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu carries the jar (cannot drop it). Aarya leads the chain.
- **Choices:**
  - 🤫 Sneak under cover
  - 💨 Rush the outage
  - 🎵 Hum; the jar lights the way
  - 🧶 The scarf route (longer, safer)
- **Outcomes:** The dock. The ship ready. One man on a ramp.
- **Image:** Children floating in a hand-chain through a maintenance shaft, the lead child holding a glowing jar, cameras wearing tiny knitted hats
- **Music:** a tuba through three decks

### Scene 3: A clipboard (climax)
> He has no weapon. He has a clipboard. "I can't move my ship," he says. "I told the council.
> If you want to go, you go around me, and there isn't an around." The jar in Keshu's arms
> pulses, and out through the big window the cloud pulses back, and ORBIT says, for the first
> time: "Chair Vexley. The crew would like to speak."
- **Setup:** Honesty, not force. Three things move him: the jar (a child's light), the rescued crew (Tomas speaks), and ORBIT (she tells the council who the heroes are and what they were doing when she forgot). The kids deliver the first.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu shows him the jar and says it is a baby's light. Aarya says the Whale is its mother.
- **Choices:**
  - 🫙 Show him the jar
  - 🗣️ Let Tomas speak
  - 🤖 Let ORBIT speak
  - 🚀 Go around (the ship says she can, "just, if everyone breathes in")
- **Rolls:** Persuasion Tricky per beat; sincere kid statements are automatic.
- **Outcomes:** He moves his ship. He asks to come. "If it goes wrong, someone should be there to tell them I was wrong." Over every speaker: "Crew departing. Come home."
- **Image:** A tall man with a clipboard on a ship's ramp looking down at a small girl holding up a glowing jar, a wall panel lit warm behind them
- **Image (after):** A ship moving aside from a dock's open doors, a tall man with a clipboard walking up the ramp of the heroes' small round ship to join them, robots and an octopus waving, the nebula glowing ahead
- **Music:** the clock stops

### Scene 4: Come home (departure)
> The ship lifts with the jar on the dashboard and Vexley strapped in with his eyes shut.
> Oola waves with all eight arms. The Hobby Club is lined up along the dock, and TUBA, for
> once, is playing in tune. At the edge of the cloud the cloud-fish are waiting. Beyond them,
> something turns to look.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu names the song (canon). Aarya plots the course from the scarf.
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Name the song
  - 🧶 Plot the course
  - 👋 Wave to the Hobby Club
- **Outcomes:** 250 XP. Council +2. ORBIT whole. Chapter 5.
- **Image:** A small ship lifting from a dock lined with waving robots and an octopus, a glowing jar on the dashboard, nebula ahead
- **Music:** a tuba, in tune, once

## Complications (roll d6)
1. TUBA's distraction is so good the council applauds and asks for more.
2. The jar wants to float. The outage comes and it drifts toward a window. Catch it.
3. ORBIT remembers something embarrassing about a hero and shares it with the dock.
4. A guard is won over by SONNET and joins the Club mid-heist.
5. The last page of Vexley's clipboard is a child's drawing of the nebula. He does not know you saw.
6. The ship has stage fright at the open doors. A child talks her through it.

## Rewards
- 250 XP. The jar. Vexley aboard. ORBIT whole. Robots +3, Council +2.

## How it ends and what it sets up
Into the cloud, with the light.
