# Chapter 5: First Contact

## Premise
The storms are at their worst because the mother is frightened. The light must be released by
hand, outside, during the only calm there is: ten seconds in which the Whale holds still and
the cloud with her. Then the song gets its answer, and every humming crew on the station stops
and laughs at once.

## Goal the party will understand
"Bring the light back to the baby whale and answer the song."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Her worry (flight)
> The cloud is wild. Lightning everywhere, singing. The cloud-fish hide in the ship's shadow.
> The Whale is above, circling, her pulses too fast. The ship grits whatever she has
> instead of teeth. "Going down. Vexley, stop screaming, it's bad for the hull."
- **Setup:** Four legs, Tricky; a fail costs the ship 1d6 (HP 30). Vexley turns out to be a good navigator and calls the lightning. The jar bends the lightning away from the ship on one leg; the cloud-fish shield the hull once.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu holds the jar to the window (one leg succeeds without a roll). Aarya calls the course from the scarf (advantage on one leg).
- **Choices:**
  - 🫙 Light the way with the jar
  - 🐟 Ask the fish to shield
  - 🐋 Sing up to the Whale to calm her (Performance Tricky; she slows)
  - ⚡ Straight down
- **Outcomes:** The deep. The child, dimmer than last week.
- **Image:** A small ship diving through violet storm canyons, a glowing jar held to the window bending the lightning away
- **Music:** storm

### Scene 2: Ten seconds (the walk)
> The child is right there, as long as the dock, barely lit. The jar has to be opened beside it,
> outside, by hand. Above, the Whale understands, and holds still, and when she holds still the
> cloud does, and the lightning stops, and there is quiet. The airlock opens. Somebody has to go.
- **Setup:** The kids decide who goes out on the line (the jar prefers Keshu; a grown-up can go too). The DM counts down from ten aloud. At three the jar opens. It cannot really fail; a wobble is a tug on the line and a grown-up pulling.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu goes out and opens the jar. Aarya counts, and holds the line.
- **Choices:**
  - 🧑‍🚀 Go out with the jar
  - 🪢 Hold the line
  - 🎵 Sing from inside so the baby hears you coming
- **Outcomes:** The child brightens all through, like a lamp turned up, and sings, and the Whale sings back, and for the first time the song has its answer, and every humming crew on the station and on the *Lantern Jack* and in the *Pakora* stops humming and laughs.
- **Image:** A small child in a striped spacesuit floating on a tether beside a house-sized glowing creature, opening a jar, light pouring out
- **Music:** the tune, answered

### Scene 3: Who are you (first contact)
> The Whale wraps round her child. The cloud lights from inside like a lantern the size of a
> sky. Then the song changes. It turns toward the ship, toward you, and it is a question, and
> it is simple: who are you?
> ORBIT, over the speaker, from very far away: "They're my crew."
- **Setup:** Each hero says their name and one true thing. The Whale answers each with a picture in the cloud a mile wide. Vexley says his name last, and the Whale shows him his daughter's drawing. Nobody knows how.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Say your name to a whale the size of a city.
- **Choices:**
  - 🗣️ Say your name
  - 🖼️ Show a drawing
  - 🎵 Sing your own song (canon; the Whale will hum it in future sessions)
- **Outcomes:** A gift: a fragment of her own light, freely, in place of the one that was taken. The **light of the Lantern of Many Roads**, given. Level 7.
- **Image:** A city-sized cloud-whale curled around a small bright creature, the whole nebula lit, four tiny figures in a ship window, a mile-wide crayon drawing in the cloud
- **Music:** everyone singing

### Scene 4: Fix my left thruster (epilogue)
> The station is lit every colour. Oola has made a banner with all eight arms. The Hobby Club
> plays the song mostly in tune. Vexley stands up in council and says he was wrong and proposes
> a school. The ship parks in Bay 7 and says, "Good. Now somebody fix my left thruster, I've
> been asking for a month."
- **Setup:** The Choir goes home. ORBIT keeps a crew log now, with the drawings in it. Airlock 7 is crew quarters, on the plans. Its far door opens onto a stepwell, a torii in the sea, a hollow oak.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu names the school. Aarya writes the first line of ORBIT's log.
- **Choices:**
  - 🏫 Name the school
  - 📖 Start the log
  - 🚪 Open Airlock 7's far door
- **Outcomes:** `python tools/campaign.py complete starfall`.
- **Image:** A station dock hung with banners, robots playing instruments, an octopus holding a banner with eight arms, a dented ship parked proudly
- **Music:** the station band

## Complications (roll d6)
1. Vexley is a natural pilot and will not give up the co-pilot seat.
2. The baby wants to keep the jar. It can.
3. A cloud-fish swallows the tether. The child is fine. The fish is embarrassed.
4. The Whale hums TUBA's practice piece back, correctly. TUBA is overcome.
5. ORBIT plays the wrong recording at the party: the party's first, terrible hum. Everyone cheers.
6. The ship asks to be painted. The kids design it. Canon, forever.

## Rewards
- 500 XP. Level 7. The Lantern's light. "First Contact" for everyone. The ship repainted. ORBIT's crew log.
