# Chapter 5: First Contact

## Premise
The finale is a flight down to the child, the return of the light, and the answering of the
song, with every friend the party has made singing a part. The cloud's storms are at their
worst because the Whale is anxious. The child's light must be released by hand, outside the
ship, in a suit, during the only calm there is: the ten seconds of a gravity failure the Whale
makes by holding still.

## Goal the party will understand
"Bring the light back to the baby cloud-whale and answer the song."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The storm (action, flight)
> The cloud is wild. Singing lightning everywhere, cloud-fish hiding in the ship's shadow, and
> the Whale above, turning and turning, anxious, its pulses too fast. The *Pakora* grits what
> she has instead of teeth. "I'm going down. Vexley, stop screaming, it's bad for the hull."
- **Setup:** The hardest flight: four legs. Each hero has a job; each leg a roll (Tricky); fails cost the ship 1d6 (HP 30). Allies help: Vexley calls out lightning (he turns out to be an excellent navigator), the jar lights the path, the cloud-fish shield the hull once.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha holds the jar up to the window; the lightning bends away from it (one leg automatically succeeds). Arya calls the course from the scarf-map (advantage on one leg).
- **Choices:**
  - 🫙 Light the way with the jar
  - 🐟 Ask the cloud-fish to shield
  - 🐋 Sing up to the Whale to calm it (Performance Tricky; it slows)
  - ⚡ Fly straight down
- **Outcomes:** The deep. The child, dimmer than before, singing in a whisper.
- **Image:** A small round ship diving through violent singing lightning in violet cloud, a glowing jar held to the window bending the bolts away

### Scene 2: Outside (action, the walk)
> The child is right there, the size of a house, barely glowing. The jar has to be opened beside
> it, outside, by hand. The Whale, above, understands, and goes still, and when it goes still the
> cloud goes still, and the gravity goes, and there are ten seconds of perfect quiet.
> The airlock opens. Somebody has to go.
- **Setup:** The climactic kid moment. One hero (the kids decide who; the jar prefers Keisha) goes out on a line with the jar during the ten seconds. It cannot really fail: the DM counts down out loud; the jar opens at "three"; the light pours into the child. If the kid wants a grown-up with them, two go. Everyone else holds the line.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha goes out and opens the jar. Arya counts the ten seconds aloud and holds the line.
- **Choices:**
  - 🧑‍🚀 Go out with the jar
  - 🪢 Hold the line
  - 🎵 Sing from inside so the baby hears you coming
- **Rolls:** None required. A DEX save Easy if the DM wants one wobble; a fail means the line tugs and a grown-up pulls.
- **Outcomes:** The child brightens, all at once, like a sunrise in a bottle, and sings, and the Whale sings back, and for the first time the song has an *answer*, and every Choir member on the station, in the *Lantern Jack*, in the *Pakora*, stops humming and laughs.
- **Image:** A small child in a striped spacesuit floating on a tether beside a house-sized glowing creature, opening a jar, light pouring out like a sunrise
- **Music:** the melody, finally answered, two voices

### Scene 3: The song, answered (celebration, wonder)
> The Whale wraps around its child. The cloud lights from the inside like a lantern the size of
> a sky. And then the song changes: it turns toward the ship, toward you, and it is a question,
> and it is simple: *who are you?*
> ORBIT, over the ship's speaker, from very far away: "They're my crew."
- **Setup:** First contact proper: the kids answer the Whale. Each hero says their name and one true thing. The Whale answers each with an image in the cloud (the DM narrates: Keisha's answer becomes a picture a mile wide). Vexley says his name last and apologises; the Whale shows him his daughter's drawing. Nobody knows how.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Say your name to a whale the size of a city.
- **Choices:**
  - 🗣️ Say your name
  - 🖼️ Show a drawing
  - 🎵 Sing your own song (canon; the Whale will hum it in future sessions)
- **Outcomes:** The Whale gives a gift: a fragment of its own light, freely, to replace the one the station took. The **light of the Lantern of Many Roads**, given, not taken. Level 7.
- **Image:** A city-sized cloud-whale curled around a small bright creature, the whole nebula lit like a lantern, four tiny figures in a ship window, a mile-wide crayon drawing in the cloud
- **Music:** everyone singing

### Scene 4: Home (epilogue)
> The station is lit every colour. Oola has made a banner with all eight arms. The Hobby Club
> plays the song, in tune, mostly. Vexley stands up in Council and says, "I was wrong, and I
> would like to propose the nebula lane be opened, with a school." The *Pakora* parks herself in
> Repair Bay 7 and says, "Good. Now somebody fix my left thruster, I've been asking for a month."
- **Setup:** Epilogue. The Choir goes home. ORBIT remembers everything and keeps a crew log now, with the kids' drawings in it. Airlock 7 becomes the crew's quarters, officially. The Door: Airlock 7's inner corridor opens, at the far end, onto a stepwell, a torii in the sea, a hollow oak.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha names the new school. Arya decides what goes in ORBIT's log first.
- **Choices:**
  - 🏫 Name the school
  - 📖 Start ORBIT's log
  - 🚪 Open Airlock 7's far door
- **Outcomes:** The campaign is complete. `python tools/campaign.py complete starfall`. All four Lantern pieces, if this is the last world.
- **Image:** A space station dock hung with banners, robots playing instruments, an octopus holding a banner with eight arms, a dented ship parked proudly, nebula glowing through the window
- **Music:** the station's whole band

## Complications (roll d6)
1. Vexley is a natural pilot and nobody can get him out of the co-pilot seat.
2. The baby whale wants to keep the jar. It can.
3. A cloud-fish swallows the tether; the kid is fine; the fish is embarrassed.
4. The Whale hums TUBA's practice piece back, correctly. TUBA is overcome.
5. ORBIT plays the wrong recording at the celebration: the party's first, terrible, hummed attempt. Everybody cheers.
6. The *Pakora* asks to be painted. The kids design it. Canon, forever.

## Rewards
- 500 XP. Level 7. The Lantern's light. "First Contact" achievement. The *Pakora* repainted. ORBIT's crew log.

The Lantern of Many Roads, if all four worlds are done: frame, shade, wick and light. See `rules/crossings.md`
