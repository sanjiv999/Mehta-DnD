# Chapter 1: Gravity Day

## Premise
The heroes wake in Repair Bay 7. Gravity is about to fail. A freighter is docking with its
cargo loose and its crew humming. Dockmaster Oola needs four people who can float. By the end
of the session the heroes have a ship, a mystery, and a friend who is an octopus.

## Goal the party will understand
"Catch the cargo, meet the humming crew, and find out whose ship this is."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Waking up (exploration)
> You wake up on a repair bay floor that is warm and humming. Lights come on one by one. Outside
> a huge window, a cloud of violet and teal fills the sky, and it is singing: a long, low, lovely
> note you can feel in your teeth. On the console a note: "Airlock 7. Don't let ORBIT forget."
> A cheerful voice from the ceiling: "Good morning! Welcome to Starfall Station! I'm ORBIT. You
> are... hm. I had this a second ago. You are definitely someone!"
- **Setup:** ORBIT is kind and forgetful. The bay holds the *Pakora* (a dented ship) with a sign on it that says "DO NOT FLY ON TUESDAYS". Each hero's keepsake glows faintly near the engine hatch. One minute to gravity failure.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha reads the note aloud. Arya finds the suit lockers; the suits have their names on them, in the same handwriting.
- **Choices:**
  - 📝 Ask ORBIT about the note
  - 🚀 Look at the ship
  - 🧥 Put on the suits
  - 🚪 Find Airlock 7 (it is not on the map; ORBIT is sure it does not exist)
- **Outcomes:** Suits on. Keepsakes hum near the ship. Then: "Gravity failure in ten, nine..."
- **Image:** Four children waking on the floor of a warm repair bay, a dented ship with a hand-painted name, a huge window full of violet nebula

### Scene 2: Catch the cargo (action, zero-g)
> Gravity goes. Your feet leave the floor. Every loose thing in the bay rises gently: tools,
> crates, a mug, you. Through the big doors comes a freighter, docking badly, and its cargo bay is
> open, and forty crates are drifting out into the station like slow bubbles. A voice from the
> dock, several voices, all from the same creature: "YOU. The floaty ones. CATCH THOSE."
- **Setup:** Zero-g minigame. Ten seconds of no gravity per hour, but in the docking bay it can be extended by Oola. Each hero catches crates: Acrobatics or Athletics Easy per crate; kids catch one automatically each by pushing off something. Drifting crates that are missed bump softly. One crate hums.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha pushes off a wall and catches the humming crate (cannot fail). Arya counts the crates caught.
- **Choices:**
  - 📦 Catch crates
  - 🪢 Throw a line and reel them in
  - 🤖 Ask the robots to help (the welding-bot knits a net; very fast)
  - 🎵 Follow the humming crate
- **Rolls:** Acrobatics Easy per crate; each catch is a point; ten points saves the day.
- **Outcomes:** Gravity returns gently (Oola times it). Dockmaster **Oola**, an octopus in a bubble-suit with eight tool belts, is impressed. The humming crate contains a recording device, still recording the nebula's song.
- **Image:** Crates drifting in a zero-gravity dock like slow bubbles, children pushing off walls to catch them, an octopus in a bubble-suit directing with eight arms
- **Music:** floaty synth, a comic timpani when gravity returns

### Scene 3: The singing captain (social)
> The freighter's captain comes down the ramp humming. Her crew behind her, humming the same tune
> in harmony. Captain Ilse Varga has kind eyes and does not seem to know she is doing it. "Fine
> trip," she says, between notes. "Fine trip. Beautiful out there. We should go back." Her crew
> nods, in rhythm. Oola's eight arms all fold at once. "That's the fourth ship this week."
- **Setup:** The Choir. Not hypnotised exactly: they heard something that wants an answer, and they are trying to answer it and cannot stop. They are happy. They cannot fly their ship (they forget the controls mid-note). Oola is grounding every ship from the cloud. The Traders' Council wants it sealed.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha hums the tune back to the captain (she stops, briefly, startled, and says "oh, you heard it too"). Arya writes the tune down as best she can (a drawing of the melody; canon).
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Hum it back
  - ❓ Ask what they saw
  - 🩺 Check them with a med-kit (they are healthy; just singing)
  - 🐙 Ask Oola what she wants
- **Outcomes:** The tune, hummed back, makes the captain pause: the song *wants an answer*, and nobody has ever answered. Oola, quietly: "Your ship could go. It's not on the Council's list because it's not on anybody's list."
- **Image:** A freighter captain in a worn flight jacket humming with her eyes half-closed, her crew behind her in harmony, a small child humming back at her

### Scene 4: The Pakora flies (exploration, wonder)
> The *Pakora*'s hatch opens by itself. Inside is small and round and warm and smells like
> someone's kitchen. The console lights up. A voice, a little gruff, a little fond: "Finally.
> Took you long enough to wake up. I'm the ship. Don't touch the red one. Sit down. Oola's opening
> the big doors and I would like, just once, to see the cloud up close."
- **Setup:** First flight. A short hop to the nebula's edge and back: a wonder scene. The *Pakora* talks. The keepsakes slot into the engine, one by one (the kids fit theirs), and the ship purrs. At the cloud's edge the song is loud and lovely, and in it, far off, something enormous and slow turns toward the ship.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keisha fits her keepsake into the engine. Arya takes the co-pilot seat and pulls the lever the ship tells her to.
- **Choices:**
  - 🚀 Fly to the cloud's edge
  - 👂 Just listen
  - 🔴 Touch the red one (the ship sighs; a cup of tea appears; that is all it does)
- **Outcomes:** The song, up close, has words almost. The *Pakora* records it. Something turned. Oola calls them back; the Council is meeting. Level 4.
- **Image:** A small round ship's warm cockpit, two children at the controls, the view ahead filled with a glowing violet cloud with something vast and slow turning within it
- **Music:** the hummed melody, full and strange

## Complications (roll d6)
1. The security-bot reads the party a poem about the gravity failure. It is terrible. It is sincere.
2. A crate contains a crate containing a crate containing one very small robot who wanted to see the station.
3. ORBIT forgets which bay the party is in and sends lunch to Bay 9.
4. The captain's crew starts harmonising with the station's air vents.
5. The *Pakora* refuses the first time. It is Tuesday. Wait ten minutes; the station clock is wrong.
6. Oola loses a tool belt in the zero-g; it drifts past the window for the rest of the session.

## Rewards
- 150 XP. Level 4. The *Pakora*. The recording. "Floaty One" achievement. Dock Guild +2.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The Council wants the nebula sealed. The dockmaster wants her crews back. The *Pakora* wants to
go. And Airlock 7 is not on any map, and ORBIT is sure it does not exist, which is exactly what
the note said would happen.
