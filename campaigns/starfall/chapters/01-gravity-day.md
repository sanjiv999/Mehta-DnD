# Chapter 1: Gravity Day

## Premise
A humming freighter docks during the hourly gravity outage. Its cargo is loose, its crew is
singing, and the dockmaster needs four heroes who can float.

## Scenes

### Scene 2: The singing captain (social)
### Scene 3: The *Pakora* flies (exploration): first short hop into the edge of the nebula.
