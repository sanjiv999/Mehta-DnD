# Chapter 1: Gravity Day

## Premise
The heroes wake in Repair Bay 7 with a note, a ship, and a cheerful station that cannot say who
they are. In a minute the gravity goes. A freighter is docking with its cargo loose and its crew
humming. The dockmaster needs people who can float. By the end, the heroes have a ship with
opinions, a recording of a song, and a friend who is an octopus.

## Goal the party will understand
"Catch the cargo, meet the humming crew, and find out whose ship this is."

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Definitely someone (exploration)
> The floor is warm and it hums. Lights come on one by one. Through a window the size of a wall
> a violet cloud goes slowly past and comes back round again, and it is singing, a long low
> note you feel in your teeth. There is a small round ship on a cradle with a name painted on
> her nose by hand. On the console, a note: "Airlock 7. Don't let ORBIT forget." A voice from
> the ceiling: "Good morning! I'm ORBIT. You are... hm. I had this a second ago. You are
> definitely someone!"
- **Setup:** The suit lockers have the heroes' names on them in the same hand as the note. Each keepsake goes warm near the ship's engine hatch. A sign on the ship: NOT ON TUESDAYS. A chime: one minute to the hour.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu reads the note aloud. Aarya finds the suits with their names on.
- **Choices:**
  - 📝 Ask ORBIT about the note
  - 🚀 Look at the ship
  - 🧥 Suits on
  - 🚪 Ask where Airlock 7 is (ORBIT is sure there isn't one)
- **Outcomes:** Suits on. Keepsakes humming. "Gravity outage in ten, nine..."
- **Image:** Four children waking on the floor of a warm repair bay, a dented round ship with a hand-painted name, a window full of violet cloud
- **Music:** fans, a chime

### Scene 2: Catch those (action, zero-g)
> The floor lets go of your feet. Everything that is not stuck down lifts: a mug, a wrench, you.
> Through the bay doors a freighter is docking badly with its cargo door open, and forty crates
> are drifting out into the station like slow bubbles. A voice from the dock, several voices,
> all the same person: "YOU. The floaty ones. CATCH THOSE."
- **Setup:** Zero-g minigame. The dockmaster can hold the outage longer in the dock. Each hero catches crates: Acrobatics or Athletics Easy per crate; a kid who pushes off a wall catches one without a roll. Missed crates bump softly. One crate hums.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu pushes off and catches the humming crate. Aarya counts the catches.
- **Choices:**
  - 📦 Catch crates
  - 🪢 Throw a line and reel
  - 🤖 Ask a robot (the welding bot knits a net in seconds)
  - 🎵 Go for the humming one
- **Rolls:** Easy per crate; ten catches saves the cargo.
- **Outcomes:** Gravity comes back gently. Dockmaster **Oola**, an octopus in a bubble suit with eight tool belts, looks at the heroes for a long moment and then says "Well. Good." The humming crate holds a recorder, still recording the cloud.
- **Image:** Crates drifting through a docking bay like slow bubbles, children pushing off walls to catch them, an octopus in a bubble suit on the ceiling pointing with four arms
- **Image (after):** Gravity coming back gently in a docking bay, the heroes landing on their feet holding stacked crates, an orange octopus in a bubble suit nodding approval with four arms folded, one humming crate glowing
- **Music:** fans, then a soft thump as gravity returns

### Scene 3: We should go back (social)
> The captain comes down the ramp humming. Her crew behind her are humming the same tune in
> parts. She has kind eyes and does not seem to know she is doing it. "Fine trip," she says,
> between notes. "Beautiful out there. We should go back." Her crew nod on the beat. Oola's
> arms all fold at once. "That's the fourth ship this week."
- **Setup:** The Choir. Not hypnotised: they heard a song that wants an answer and cannot stop trying. They are happy. They forget the controls mid-note. The council is grounding every ship from the cloud.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu hums the tune back at the captain. She stops, and her eyes fill, and she says "oh. You heard it too." Aarya draws the tune (canon).
- **Choices:**
  - 🎵 Hum it back
  - ❓ Ask what they saw
  - 🩺 Med-kit them (healthy; singing)
  - 🐙 Ask Oola what she wants
- **Outcomes:** The song wants an answer, and nobody has ever given one. Oola, quietly: "Your ship could go. She's not on the council's list. She's not on anyone's list."
- **Image:** A freighter captain in a worn jacket on a ramp with her eyes half closed, humming, her crew behind her, a small child humming back up at her
- **Music:** the tune, in parts

### Scene 4: Don't touch the red one (flight)
> The ship's hatch opens on its own. Inside is small and round and warm and smells like
> somebody's kitchen. The console lights up. A voice, gruff but pleased: "Finally. Took you
> long enough to wake up. I'm the ship. Don't touch the red one. Sit down. Oola's opening the
> big doors and I would like, once, to see the cloud up close."
- **Setup:** First flight. The keepsakes slot into the engine one by one (the kids fit theirs) and the ship purrs. At the cloud's edge the song is loud and something very large and slow turns toward the ship.
- **Jobs for the kids:** Keshu fits her keepsake. Aarya takes the co-pilot seat and pulls the lever the ship tells her to.
- **Choices:**
  - 🚀 Fly to the edge
  - 👂 Just listen
  - 🔴 Touch the red one (a cup of tea appears; that is all it does)
- **Outcomes:** The song has words, nearly. The ship records it. Something turned. Oola calls them back: the council is meeting. Level 4.
- **Image:** A small round ship's warm cockpit, two children at the controls, the view ahead filled with violet cloud and a vast slow shape inside it
- **Music:** the tune, whole

## Complications (roll d6)
1. SONNET reads the party a poem about the gravity outage. It is bad. It is sincere.
2. A crate holds a crate holding a crate holding one very small robot who wanted to see the station.
3. ORBIT sends lunch to Bay 9. There is nobody in Bay 9. There used to be.
4. The captain's crew harmonise with the air vents.
5. The ship refuses to start. It is Tuesday. The station clock is wrong; wait ten minutes.
6. Oola loses a tool belt to the outage. It goes past the window for the rest of the session.

## Rewards
- 150 XP. Level 4. The *Pakora*. The recording. "Floaty One" for everyone. Docks +2.

## How it ends and what it sets up
The council wants the cloud sealed. The dockmaster wants her crews back. The ship wants to go.
And Airlock 7 is on no map, and ORBIT is certain it does not exist, which is what the note said would happen.
