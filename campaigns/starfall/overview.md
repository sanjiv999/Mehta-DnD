# Starfall Station

## The pitch (read this to the players)
You wake up in Repair Bay 7 of Starfall Station with no memory of how you got there, a ship of
your own (small, dented, and named the *Pakora*), and a note on the console in handwriting you
half-recognise: "Airlock 7. Don't let ORBIT forget." Outside the window, a nebula glows violet
and teal and fills half the sky. And it is singing. You can hear it through the hull.

Ships are coming back from the nebula with their crews humming the same tune. The traders want
the cloud sealed off. The dockmaster wants her ships back. And ORBIT, the station's cheerful AI,
keeps forgetting what she was about to say. Somebody has to go into the cloud and find out
what is singing. Somebody with a ship.

## The world in five facts
1. **Magic is technology here.** Spells are gadgets. Spark is a blaster. Healing Touch is a med-kit. Everything your hero could do, they can still do; it just has a battery.
2. **Every robot on the station has a hobby.** The welding-bot knits. The security-bot writes poetry. The hobby is the way to its heart.
3. **Gravity fails for ten seconds every hour.** Everyone has learned to float politely. Loose things drift. Kids love it.
4. **The nebula is alive and lonely.** It sings to be answered.
5. **The *Pakora* has a personality.** She refuses to fly on Tuesdays, loves the kids, and is braver than she looks.

## What heroes do here
Repair, explore, rescue, trade, ride out gravity failures, befriend robots, fly their own ship
into the cloud, and make first contact with something that has never been answered.

## Factions
| Faction | What they want | Face |
|---|---|---|
| ORBIT (the station) | To remember | ORBIT |
| The Dock Guild | Their ships and crews back | Dockmaster Oola (an octopus) |
| The Traders' Council | The nebula sealed off | Chair Vexley |
| The Choir | To finish the song | Captain Ilse Varga (humming) |
| The Robots' Hobby Club | A new member | Welding-bot KNIT-9 |

## Hooks for each hero
- A **kid hero**'s keepsake is a piece of the *Pakora*'s missing engine. The ship knows it.
- A **warrior or protector** wakes up with a stripe on their suit that marks them as "crew". Of what, ORBIT cannot remember.
- A **clever hero** can read the handwriting on the note. It is their own.
- A **charming hero** is already owed a favour by the dockmaster, who recognises them and cannot say from where.
