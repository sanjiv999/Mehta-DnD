# Starfall Station

## The pitch
The heroes wake up in a repair bay on Starfall Station with a ship of their own (small, dented,
lovable) and a job: find out why ships are coming back from the nebula with their crews
humming the same tune. The station is run by a cheerful AI named ORBIT who keeps forgetting
things, a dockmaster octopus, and a council of traders who want the nebula left alone.

## The world in five facts
1. Magic is technology here. Spells are gadgets, Spark is a blaster, Healing Touch is a med-kit.
2. Every robot on the station has a hobby.
3. Gravity on the station fails for ten seconds every hour. Everyone has learned to float politely.
4. The nebula is alive and lonely.
5. The heroes' ship, the *Pakora*, has a personality and refuses to fly on Tuesdays.

## What heroes do here
Repair, explore, rescue, trade, and eventually make first contact.

## Factions
ORBIT · The Dock Guild · The Traders' Council · The Choir (humming crews)

## Hooks for each hero
- Each hero's keepsake turns out to be a piece of the ship's missing engine.
