# Starfall Station

## The pitch (read this to the players)
You wake up on the floor of a repair bay. The floor is warm. The whole room hums like the
inside of a vacuum cleaner, which is what a space station sounds like, and through the big
window a cloud fills half the sky, violet going to teal, lit from inside. It is singing. You can
feel the note in your teeth.

There is a small round ship on a cradle with a name hand-painted on her nose, and a note on
the console in handwriting you half recognise: "Airlock 7. Don't let ORBIT forget." A cheerful
voice comes from the ceiling. "Good morning! Welcome to Starfall Station! I'm ORBIT. You are...
hm. I had this a second ago. You are definitely someone!"

In about a minute, the gravity is going to switch off for ten seconds. Everyone here has learned
to float politely. You will learn fast.

## The world in five facts
1. The fans never stop. Everyone sleeps in earplugs, in bags hooked to a wall near a fan.
2. Once an hour, on the hour, the ring stops spinning for ten seconds and everything floats. Velcro on every surface. Tortillas, not bread.
3. Magic is equipment here. Spark is a hand-blaster, Healing Touch is a med-kit, a spell is a thing with a battery.
4. Every robot has a hobby, because they were built to copy the people, and the people are bored.
5. Ships are coming back from the cloud with their crews humming the same tune, happy, unable to stop.

## What heroes do here
Catch floating cargo. Win over robots. Find a corridor that is not on the plans. Fly a ship with
opinions into a singing cloud. Rescue a crew. Rob your own station. Give something back.

## Hooks for each hero
- A **kid hero**'s keepsake slots into the ship's engine as if it were made for it. It was.
- A **protector**'s suit has a stripe on the sleeve that means "crew". ORBIT cannot say of what.
- A **clever hero** can read the note on the console. It is her handwriting.
- A **charming hero** is recognised by the octopus, who cannot say from where and is annoyed about it.
