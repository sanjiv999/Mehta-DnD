# Dockmaster Oola

**Looks:** Orange, eight tool belts, a bubble-suit, turns red when furious.
**Sounds like:** Several voices at once, all hers. "YOU. The floaty ones."
**Wants:** Her ships and crews back, and the Council to stop being cowards.
**Offers:** The dock doors, a banner, a vote.

## Secret
She met the heroes a hundred days ago and liked them. She cannot remember why she likes them and it bothers her.
