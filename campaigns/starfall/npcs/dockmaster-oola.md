# Dockmaster Oola

**Looks:** Orange, eight tool belts, a bubble suit with a small leak she keeps meaning to fix. Turns red when angry and does not mind who sees.
**Sounds like:** Several voices at once, all hers. "YOU. The floaty ones. CATCH THOSE."
**Wants:** Her ships and her crews back, and for the council to stop voting on things they have not seen.
**Afraid of:** Nothing in the cloud. The council frightens her.
**Offers:** The dock doors, a berth for the *Pakora* nobody has told the council about, a vote, and a banner made with all eight arms.

## Secret
She met the heroes a hundred days ago and liked them. She cannot remember why, and it has been bothering her, and she has been rude to them about it.
