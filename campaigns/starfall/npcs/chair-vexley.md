# Chair Vexley

**Looks:** Tall, a grey coat, a clipboard held flat to his chest, posture like a mast. Straightens the clipboard when he is about to say no.
**Sounds like:** "I'm sure you understand." He means it. That is the problem.
**Wants:** Nobody else lost to the cloud. He signed the letters last time.
**Afraid of:** Telling ORBIT.
**Offers:** No. Then his own ship across the doors. Then, when he is shown the jar, himself as a passenger, and a navigator's eye nobody knew he had.
**Thinks:** The dockmaster is reckless and the only honest person on the council.

## Secret
His daughter drew the nebula from the school window on the last page of his clipboard. The Whale shows it to him in the cloud. That is the moment he changes, not any argument.
