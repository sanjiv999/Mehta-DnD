# Captain Ilse Varga

**Looks:** A flight jacket with the elbows gone, kind eyes, a smile she does not know she is wearing.
**Sounds like:** Hums between sentences. "Fine trip. Beautiful. We should go back."
**Wants:** To finish the song. She cannot, and she cannot stop.
**Offers:** The first clue that the song wants an answer. In the finale, a chorus of thirty.

## Secret
She is the happiest she has ever been and knows something is wrong. When a child hums back she stops, for one minute, and cries, and says "oh, you heard it too."
