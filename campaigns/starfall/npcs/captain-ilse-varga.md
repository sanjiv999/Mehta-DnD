# Captain Ilse Varga

**Looks:** Worn flight jacket, kind eyes, a smile she does not know she is wearing.
**Sounds like:** Hums between every sentence. "Fine trip. We should go back."
**Wants:** To answer the song.
**Offers:** The first clue that the song wants an answer; later, a chorus of thirty for the finale.

## Secret
She is the happiest she has ever been and knows something is wrong and cannot stop. When a kid hums back, she cries, once, with relief.
