# The Pakora

**Looks:** Round, dented, a hand-painted name, portholes like kind eyes.
**Sounds like:** Gruff, fond, "don't touch the red one". Loves the kids. Will not fly on Tuesdays.
**Wants:** To see the cloud up close. To be repainted. Her left thruster fixed.
**Offers:** Flight, a cup of tea (the red button), navigation by song, bravery.

## Secret
The heroes built her engine from their keepsakes, which is why they slot in. She remembers them when ORBIT could not and has been waiting a hundred days.
