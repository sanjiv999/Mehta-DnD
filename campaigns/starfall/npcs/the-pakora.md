# The Pakora

**Looks:** Round, dented on the left, a hand-painted name, portholes you want to call eyes. Inside it is small and warm and smells of somebody's kitchen.
**Sounds like:** "Don't touch the red one." Gruff, fond, impatient with grown-ups.
**Wants:** To see the cloud up close. Her left thruster fixed. To be repainted, by the children.
**Afraid of:** Tuesdays. She will not explain.
**Offers:** Flight, navigation by song, tea (the red button), and a hundred days of waiting.

## Secret
The crew built her engine from their keepsakes, which is why the keepsakes fit. She remembered them when ORBIT could not and has been sitting in Bay 7 with her lights on every night.
