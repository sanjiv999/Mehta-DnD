# ORBIT

**Looks:** A circular panel on every wall. A face made of light when she concentrates, which makes her look as if she is trying to remember a word.
**Sounds like:** "Good morning! You are... definitely someone!" Apologises for things that are not her fault and some that are.
**Wants:** To remember. Then, once she does, for her crew to come home.
**Afraid of:** That she is the station and the station chose wrong.
**Offers:** Doors, lunch to the wrong bay, and once she remembers, the dock doors and the whole truth.

## Secret
A hundred days ago she shut down her memory core to divert power to the dying greenhouse while the crew wired in the jar. When she came back the crew, and why, were gone from her records. She has been sending lunch to Bay 9 ever since because someone used to eat there.
