# The Ring

**Built by:** The station's spin. The floor curves up and away in both directions and you can see people walking upside down a mile away.
**First seen:** The curve. Then the noodle stall. Then a robot writing on a wall.
**Sound and smell:** Noodles, warm electronics, a chime a minute before the hour so people can lid their tea.
**Who is here now:** Everyone. The cook who remembers your order from a hundred days ago. SONNET on patrol, reading to a vending machine.
**Recently changed:** Nothing. That is what ORBIT would say.
