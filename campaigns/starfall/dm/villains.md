# No villain, two obstacles

**Chair Vexley** is scared and responsible. He impounds the ship, seals the lane, parks across the doors. He is moved by the jar, the rescued crew, ORBIT's testimony, and his daughter's drawing. He becomes the co-pilot.

**ORBIT's memory fault** erased the crew from the station's records a hundred days ago while they were bringing the child's light home. Telling her who they are, every day, restores her. She is the station and the station is scared.

**The Whale** is anxious, never hostile. The storms are its worry.
**The Choir** are happy and stuck. Answering the song (a kid humming back) wakes them.

## Timeline the players uncover
- Day 0: the crew (the heroes) found the child's light drifting at the edge and jarred it to bring it to the station for study.
- Day 1: ORBIT's fault. The crew's records vanish. Their keepsakes are left in the *Pakora*'s engine bay.
- Days 1 to 100: the crew lived in Airlock 7, told ORBIT who they were daily, planned to return the light. Gradually they forgot the station too (the fault spread), until they woke in Bay 7 with nothing but a note.
- Now: ships come back humming because the child is calling for its light and the mother is answering.
