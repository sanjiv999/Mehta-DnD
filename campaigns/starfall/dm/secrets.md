# Secrets and answers

- **Why are crews humming?** The child-whale is calling for its light; the mother answers; the song wants an answer and humans try to give one and cannot stop.
- **Who wrote the note?** The heroes, to themselves, a hundred days ago.
- **Where is Airlock 7?** Between hatches 6 and 8; KNIT-9 built it; the scarf is the map; the wall opens to the hummed melody.
- **What is in the jar?** The child's light, which is also the Lantern's light. It is returned in chapter 5, and the Whale gives a fragment of its own light freely in exchange.
- **Why does the Pakora know the kids?** Her engine is their keepsakes.
- **Door:** Airlock 7's inner corridor; its far door opens onto the other worlds' Doors.
