# Bestiary (kid-safe)

## Drifting Crate (chapter 1)
AC 10 · Catch: Acrobatics Easy. Missed crates bump softly. One hums.

## Cloud-Fish
HP 4 · AC 14 · No attack · Trick: glow; shield a hull once if asked nicely; like tea.
**How it ends:** swims off, or into the kitchen.

## Singing Lightning (chapters 3 and 5)
Not a creature. DEX save Tricky per leg; 1d6 to the ship (HP 30) on a fail. Bends away from the jar. Hums names; sing it back and it quiets.

## Gravity Failure (hourly)
Ten seconds. Everything floats. DEX save Easy to grab something; failing means a soft bump and a giggle.

## Dock Guard (chapter 4)
HP 15 · AC 14 · Attack +4 (stun-baton, 1d6, knocks out, never worse) · Trick: sits down for poetry.
**How it ends:** joins the Hobby Club.

## The Cloud-Whale
Not a fight. Communication: hum, lights, drawing, question. Responds in cloud-images.

## The Child
Not a fight. Needs its light. Brightens like a sunrise.
