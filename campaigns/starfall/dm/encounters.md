# Ready encounters

## Station
- **Gravity chime:** once per session, on the hour; everything floats for ten seconds. Kids' favourite.
- **SONNET on patrol:** reads a poem; a DC Easy "keep a straight face".
- **Noodle stall on the Ring:** a bowl heals 1d6; the cook remembers the crew's orders.
- **Council inspector:** asks for papers; KNIT-9 recruits them.

## The Garden Deck
- **Hobby minigames:** knit (DEX Easy), poem (a kid writes two lines), paint (describe), tuba (Performance Easy, being bad together counts).

## The cloud
Roll d6 per leg: 1 singing lightning, 2 cloud-fish escort, 3 lost cargo (loot: a crate of something silly), 4 a Choir ship adrift (rescue, 50 XP), 5 a pocket of perfect calm (long rest), 6 the Whale's eye, briefly.

## Airlock 7
- **The corridor:** four bunks, crayon drawings, a calendar, the jar. No enemies. Ever.
