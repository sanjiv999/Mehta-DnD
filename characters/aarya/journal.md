## Before the story (Session 0)

I am Machli. I am a hammerhead, and my stripes glow every colour.
I come from the bottom of the sea, where it is dark and the whales sing.
I found a map in an old ship. One place on it is blank, and I want to find it.
The map said go up the river, so I did. Now I am in a red city with kites in the sky.
I am a bit scared of high cliffs. When I am scared, I take a bite and jump anyway!
I want to make good memories with my brother Keshu. He is my best friend.
