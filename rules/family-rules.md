# Family Rules

These are the complete rules the table uses. They are a simplified cousin of classic
fifth-edition play, tuned for a seven-year-old and a five-year-old. Grown-ups can layer on
anything from `rules/advanced.md`.

## The one rule

When you try something risky, roll a twenty-sided die (d20), add your bonus, and compare it
to the difficulty. Meet it or beat it and you succeed.

| Difficulty | Number | When |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 10 | Most people could do it |
| Tricky | 13 | Default. A hero probably can |
| Hard | 16 | Needs skill or luck |
| Heroic | 20 | Stories are told about this |

A natural 20 is a **Big Win**: it works and something extra good happens. A natural 1 is a
**Whoops**: it fails and something funny or complicating happens. Nobody gets hurt from a Whoops alone.

## Six things a hero can be good at

| Ability | Short | Kid words |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | STR | Strong |
| Dexterity | DEX | Quick |
| Constitution | CON | Tough |
| Intelligence | INT | Clever |
| Wisdom | WIS | Noticing |
| Charisma | CHA | Charming |

Each is a score from 8 to 18. Your **bonus** is (score minus 10) divided by two, rounded down.
New heroes assign these scores in any order: **15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8**.

## Skills

A hero is **trained** in four skills from this list and adds +2 (their proficiency bonus) to
rolls with them. Proficiency grows: +2 at levels 1 to 4, +3 at 5 to 8, +4 at 9 to 12.

Athletics (STR), Acrobatics (DEX), Sneaking (DEX), Tinkering (DEX), Lore (INT), Investigation (INT),
Nature (WIS), Animals (WIS), Perception (WIS), Medicine (WIS), Persuasion (CHA), Performance (CHA),
Bluffing (CHA), Intimidation (CHA).

## Hit points and getting hurt

Heroes have **hit points (HP)**. When HP reaches 0 the hero is **knocked out**, not dead.
A knocked-out hero wakes up at 1 HP after the fight, or sooner if a friend helps them
(Medicine check, Easy). Heroes in this game do not die unless the player chooses it as part of their story.

Starting HP by class is in `rules/classes.md`. Each level adds the class's hit die average plus the CON bonus.

## Hero Points

Every hero starts each session with **3 Hero Points**. Spend one to:
- Reroll any die you just rolled.
- Add a small true thing to the scene ("There is a rope here").
- Help a friend: give them advantage on their roll.

Hero Points reward brave, kind or clever ideas. The DM can hand out extras at the table.

## Advantage and disadvantage

Roll two d20 and take the better (advantage) or the worse (disadvantage). They cancel each other out.

## Resting

- **Short rest** (an hour): spend hit dice to heal, regain short-rest powers.
- **Long rest** (a night): full HP, all powers, Hero Points back to 3.

## Levels and experience

Heroes gain XP for scenes resolved, not just monsters defeated. The DM awards it in the session log.

| Level | XP | Proficiency |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | +2 |
| 2 | 100 | +2 |
| 3 | 250 | +2 |
| 4 | 500 | +2 |
| 5 | 1,000 | +3 |
| 6 | 2,000 | +3 |
| 7 | 3,500 | +3 |
| 8 | 5,500 | +3 |
| 9 | 8,000 | +4 |
| 10 | 12,000 | +4 |

Milestones can replace XP: finishing a chapter grants a level.

## Content lines

- Villains can be scary for a moment but are always beatable and often fixable.
- No blood descriptions, no torture, no harm to animals the kids love.
- If a player says "pause" the scene pauses, no questions asked.
- Scary or sad moments get a "but then" within a minute.
