# Classes

A class is what your hero does. Pick one. Each gives starting HP, a hit die, a signature
power at level 1, and more powers as you level. All powers are written to be usable at any
table; Kids Mode heroes rename them.

| Class | Hit die | Level 1 HP | Core idea | Level 1 power |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | d10 | 10 + CON | Strong, brave, protects friends | **Shield Friend**: when an ally within reach is hit, take the hit instead (once per short rest) |
| **Ranger** | d10 | 10 + CON | Tracks, shoots, has an animal friend | **Animal Companion**: a loyal creature with 5 HP that can do one simple job per turn |
| **Rogue** | d8 | 8 + CON | Sneaky, quick, finds secrets | **Sneak Attack**: +1d6 damage when attacking with advantage or beside an ally |
| **Mage** | d6 | 6 + CON | Learns magic, clever | **Spark**: ranged magic attack, 1d8, plus one cantrip (Light, Mend, Message) |
| **Priest / Sage** | d8 | 8 + CON | Heals, wise, protects with faith or knowledge | **Healing Touch**: heal 1d8 + WIS to a friend (uses = WIS bonus + 1 per long rest) |
| **Bard** | d8 | 8 + CON | Music, stories, charm | **Inspire**: give a friend a d6 to add to any roll (uses = CHA bonus + 1 per long rest) |
| **Beast-Friend** | d8 | 8 + CON | Talks to animals, shapeshifts a little | **Wild Shape**: become a small animal for a scene (once per short rest) |
| **Inventor** | d8 | 8 + CON | Gadgets, tinkering | **Gadget**: one device per long rest that solves a Tricky problem automatically |

Levels 2 to 5 add one new power each level from the class list in `rules/advanced.md`.

## Species (what your hero is)

Species gives flavor and one small trick. Kids can invent their own; the DM assigns a trick.

| Species | Trick |
|---|---|
| Human | One extra trained skill |
| Elf / Apsara / Tennin | Darkvision; advantage against being charmed |
| Dwarf / Yaksha | Advantage against poison; know stonework |
| Halfling / Vanara | Reroll a natural 1 once per session |
| Naga-born | Can breathe underwater; speak with snakes |
| Tiger-kin / Kitsune | Can jump twice as far; keen smell |
| Clockwork / Android | Does not need to breathe; +1 AC |
| Dragonkin | Breath weapon 2d6 in a line, once per short rest |
| Fairy / Yosei | Can fly briefly (10 feet) once per short rest |
| Your own idea | Write it on the sheet; the DM assigns a trick of the same size |

## Armor Class

AC is how hard you are to hit. Start at 10 + DEX bonus. Light armor +1, medium +3 (no DEX above +2),
heavy +6 (no DEX), shield +2.
