# Combat Quick Reference

## Start

1. Everyone rolls d20 + DEX bonus for **initiative**. Highest goes first. Kids Mode: the kids go first.
2. The DM describes the scene and says what the enemies seem to want.

## On your turn

You can **move** (up to your speed, usually 30 feet or "six squares") and do **one action**:

| Action | Roll |
|---|---|
| Attack | d20 + ability bonus + proficiency vs the target's AC. Hit: roll the weapon's damage die + ability bonus |
| Use a power | As written on your sheet |
| Help | A friend gets advantage on their next roll |
| Hide | Sneaking check vs the enemy's Perception |
| Dash | Move again |
| Protect | Until your next turn, attacks against a friend next to you have disadvantage |
| Talk | Try to end the fight with words: Persuasion or Intimidation, usually Hard |
| Anything else | Say it; the DM picks the difficulty |

## Weapons

| Weapon | Damage | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dagger, sling | 1d4 | Thrown or ranged |
| Short sword, mace, hand axe, talwar | 1d6 | |
| Long sword, katana, spear, bow | 1d8 | Bow is ranged |
| Great weapon (two hands) | 2d6 | |
| Magic Spark | 1d8 | Ranged |

## Monsters

Use the kid-safe stat blocks in each campaign's `dm/bestiary.md`. Every monster has:
HP, AC, attack bonus, damage, one trick, and a **"How it ends"** line (what happens when it is beaten).

## Ending a fight

Fights end when the enemies are beaten, run, surrender, or the heroes find another way.
Award XP for the scene, not per monster. Let the kids describe the final blow.
