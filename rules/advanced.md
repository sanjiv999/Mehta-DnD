# Advanced Options

Optional layers for the adult players. None are required.

## Class powers by level

**Warrior:** 2 Second Wind (heal 1d10+level, short rest) · 3 Fighting Style (+2 to hit or +1 AC) · 4 Extra Attack · 5 Rally (allies within 30 ft gain 5 temp HP).

**Ranger:** 2 Hunter's Mark (+1d6 vs one target) · 3 Companion grows (10 HP, attacks 1d6) · 4 Trackless Step · 5 Volley (attack two targets).

**Rogue:** 2 Cunning Action (hide/dash as a bonus) · 3 Expertise (double proficiency in two skills) · 4 Uncanny Dodge (halve one hit per turn) · 5 Sneak Attack 2d6.

**Mage:** 2 Choose two spells from the list below · 3 Arcane Shield (+4 AC reaction, once per short rest) · 4 Two more spells · 5 Fireburst (3d6 in a 15 ft circle, once per long rest).

**Priest/Sage:** 2 Bless (allies +1d4 to rolls for a scene) · 3 Turn Fear (allies immune to fear) · 4 Healing Touch heals 2d8 · 5 Revive (a knocked-out ally stands at half HP).

**Bard:** 2 Song of Rest (short rest heals extra 1d6) · 3 Cutting Words (enemy gets −1d6) · 4 Inspire d8 · 5 Enthrall (a crowd listens for a scene).

**Beast-Friend:** 2 Speak with Animals at will · 3 Wild Shape medium animal · 4 Thorn Whip (1d8, pull 10 ft) · 5 Call the Pack (summon 1d4 small animals for a scene).

**Inventor:** 2 Two gadgets per long rest · 3 Grappling Hook (climb at full speed) · 4 Smoke Bomb (scene-wide hide) · 5 Flying Contraption (fly 30 ft for a scene).

## Spell list (Mage, Priest)
Light · Mend · Message · Magic Hand · Feather Fall · Sleep (2d8 HP of creatures) · Shield · Charm Person · Detect Magic · Healing Word (1d4+WIS at range) · Hold Person · Misty Step · Fog Cloud · Speak with Dead (Sage only, friendly ghosts) · Water Walk.

## Downtime between sessions
Each hero can do one downtime action: train (gain a skill at level-up), craft, research a thread, or bond (gain a relationship point with an NPC).

## Reputation
Each campaign tracks faction reputation in `state.yaml` under `reputation:`. −3 (hunted) to +3 (celebrated).
