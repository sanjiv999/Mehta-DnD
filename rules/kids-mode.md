# Kids Mode

Switches the DM can flip for the youngest players. Any hero can be in Kids Mode; it is marked
`kids_mode: true` on their sheet.

## Simplifications

- **Three questions instead of a sheet.** "What are you good at?", "What is your special thing?",
  "Who do you love?" The DM maps answers onto the real sheet behind the scenes.
- **Roll one die.** The hero rolls the d20 and the DM adds the bonus and says the result.
- **Describe, don't calculate.** Say "that's a Big Hit" rather than "11 damage".
- **Powers instead of spells.** Every Kids Mode hero has three named powers written in their own words
  (for example "Tiger Roar", "Sparkle Shield", "Super Climb"). Each power maps to a mechanic in `rules/classes.md`.
- **Always a job.** In every scene the DM gives the kid something to do that cannot fail:
  find a thing, name a thing, pick one of three doors, or roll for a friend.
- **Turns are short.** Three choices offered out loud, then the die.

## Pacing with a five-year-old

- Sessions of 30 to 45 minutes; stop while it is still fun.
- One fight, one puzzle, one talking scene per session is plenty.
- Snacks are in-world: the tavern serves what is on the table.
- Let them draw: the drawing goes in `characters/<id>/portraits/` as "player art".

## Pacing with a seven-year-old

- Can hold the full sheet with help. Introduce one new mechanic per session.
- Loves secrets: give them one private clue per session to share or keep.
- Let them narrate their own Big Wins.

## Feelings rules

- A knocked-out hero is "dizzy" and rejoins quickly.
- Monsters that are defeated "run away", "turn back into statues", or "say sorry".
- If a kid is upset by a scene, the scene changes. The story can always bend.
